# Open DEX Application and Device Health

Open DEX Application and Device Health to view your organization's end-user experience and use application and user navigation and trend analysis to identify the root cause of an issue.

## Before you begin

Confirm that the DEX plugin \(sn\_dex\) is installed.

Role required: sn\_dex.user

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **Service Operations Workspace**.
