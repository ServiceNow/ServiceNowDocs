# LEAP AI indicators

AI indicators mark the artifacts that the LEAP AI agent creates. They appear on the home page list and on the automation opportunity details page.

## Where AI indicators appear

The following table describes where the AI indicator appears and how it behaves.

<table><thead><tr><th>

Location

</th><th>

Behavior

</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>

Home page list

</td><td>

An AI icon \[Omitted image "ai-icon.png"\] Alt text: AI icon column identifies the automation opportunities that have artifacts created by the LEAP AI agent. When you hover over the AI icon, a pop up card appears with information related to the agent-generated problem \(PRB\) and knowledge base \(KB\) records:

 -   Select the PRB link to open the record in the Service Operations Workspace.
-   Select kb link to open the article in the Knowledge Center.

</td></tr><tr><td>

Automation opportunity details page

</td><td>

The Action insights section for agent-generated artifact appears in a gradient style.

</td></tr></tbody>
