# Manage the AIOps AI Specialist

Use the AIOps home page to view and manage the alerts assigned to the AIOps AI Specialist. This page is the default home page of the Service Operations Workspace. Which view you see depends on your role.

## Overview of AIOps Supervision

Both admins and operators must be able to view the work done by the AI Specialist. Admins want to view a real-time snapshot of alert activity, workload distribution, and the impact AI is having on the team's performance. Operators, and especially those supervising the AI specialist, want to review operational workload, monitor AIOps AI Specialist activity, and review alerts closed by it.

-   [Monitoring AI operations in ITOM as an admin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-operations-management/ai-specialist-dashboard-metrics.md)

    View performance metrics, including AI efficiency.

-   [Manage as an operator or supervisor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-operations-management/ai-supervisor-in-itom-aiops.md)

    Supervise alerts assigned to your team and monitor and review alerts assigned to the AI specialist.

-   [Reopen alert closed by AI Specialist](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-operations-management/event-management/reopen-ai-closed-alert.md)

    Reopen an alert that the AIOps AI Specialist auto-closed as noise so it bypasses the noise classifier and runs the full investigation pipeline.
