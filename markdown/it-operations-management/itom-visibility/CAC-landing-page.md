# Cryptographic Asset Compliance

ServiceNow Cryptographic Asset Compliance helps you inventory, assess, and manage cryptographic assets across cloud and on-premises environments to support post-quantum cryptography \(PQC\) readiness and help maintain security compliance.

Cryptographic Asset Compliance helps organizations in their goals to future-proof their cryptographic assets like digital certificates and cloud keys. Because quantum computing advances are anticipated to threaten traditional cryptographic algorithms, this application provides visibility and control for PQC migration preparation.

## Key capabilities

|Description|Capability|
|-----------|----------|
|Inventory the cryptographic assets that ServiceNow [Certificate Inventory and Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/discovery/cert-inventory-mgmt.md) and ServiceNow [Discovery](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/discovery/r-discovery.md) find, including certificates \(on-premises and URL-based\) and keys \(from AWS KMS, and Azure Key Vault\).|Multi-source inventory|
|Track cryptographic assets at risk with policy-based risk indicators such as outdated algorithms, quantum vulnerability, and expiring certificates.|Risk indicator monitoring|
|Track PQC compliance and quantum vulnerability through dashboards so you can make data-driven decisions and plan migration to quantum-resistant algorithms.|Post-quantum readiness|
|Leverage artificial intelligence to analyze cryptographic assets and identify necessary actions.|AI-powered risk assessment|
