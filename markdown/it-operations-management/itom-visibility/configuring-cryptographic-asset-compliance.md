# Configuring Cryptographic Asset Compliance

Set up cryptographic asset discovery and configure trusted certificate authorities \(CAs\) to prepare for your organization's cryptographic asset monitoring needs.

## Configuration overview

Configuring Cryptographic Asset Compliance involves the following actions:

-   [Configuring the discovery of cryptographic assets](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/itom-visibility/set-up-cryptographic-asset-discovery.md)- Configuring the discovery of cryptographic assets involves setting up certificate and cloud discovery.
-   [Configure trusted certificate authorities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/itom-visibility/add-trusted-certificate-authorities.md)- Configure certificate authorities \(CAs\) that your organization trusts and activate the certificate authority trust policy so that certificates from other authorities are flagged as untrusted.
