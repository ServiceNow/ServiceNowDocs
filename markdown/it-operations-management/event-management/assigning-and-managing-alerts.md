# Assigning and managing alerts

Learn how to assign and manage alerts in Express List.

-   **[Monitor incoming alerts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-operations-management/event-management/t_EMUseAlertConsole.md)**  
You can monitor incoming alerts in Service Operations Workspace. You can also monitor and manage alerts in the Event Management interface.
-   **[Assign alerts in Express List](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-operations-management/event-management/assign-alerts.md)**  
Assign alerts in Express List to yourself, to someone else, or to an assignment group to balance your organization's work load.
-   **[Find alert records in Express List using text search](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-operations-management/event-management/el-free-text-search.md)**  
Perform a text search for alert records in a filtered list of alerts.
-   **[Highlight alerts by condition in Express List](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-operations-management/event-management/el-highlight-field-values.md)**  
Learn how to apply color-coded highlights to alerts in the Express List based on conditions such as severity, state, and priority, so you can quickly spot critical signals.
-   **[Manage alert states in Express List](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-operations-management/event-management/manage-alert-states-in-express-list.md)**  
Learn how to change alert states.
