# Delete admin role from group

Delete the evt\_mgmt\_admin role from a group when you no longer need them.

## Before you begin

Verify that you have installed the ITOM AIOps and ServiceNow Otto for IT Operations Management \(ITOM\) plugins.

Ensure you're in the Configure IT Operations Management page.

Role required: evt\_mgmt\_admin or evt\_team\_operator

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Configuration Summary** &gt; **Event Management** &gt; **Platform foundations**.

2.  Expand **Platform foundations**.

3.  Select **Assign Event Management admins**.

4.  Select and open the group from which you want to remove the evt\_mgmt\_admin role.

5.  On the Edit group page, select **Remove role from group**.

6.  In the confirmation window, select **Remove role**.

    The evt\_mgmt\_admin role is removed from the specific group.
