# MetricBase templates for Service Observability

Templates used to create MetricBase dashboards in Service Observability. You can edit these templates as needed.

See [Customize Service Observability dashboard templates](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/service-observability/customize-service-observability-dashboard-templates.md) for more information.

-   **[MetricBase Overview tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/service-observability/metricbase-overview-tab.md)**  
Information that is displayed on the MetricBase **Overview** tab of the Service Details page in the SOW.
-   **[MetricBase Observability tab for Service Observability](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-operations-management/service-observability/metricbase-obserability-tab.md)**  
Dashboard and charts on the MetricBase Observability tab of the Service Details page in the SOW.
