# AI in Firewall Audits and Reporting

AI provides an agentic workflow in Firewall Audits and Reporting that helps firewall administrators request new firewall rules using natural language.

Firewall Audits and Reporting includes an AI-powered agentic workflow. This feature requires ServiceNow Otto for IT Operations Management \(ITOM\) to be installed.

**Note:** Depending on your license, you will have access to certain application features, generative AI skills, agentic workflows, and AI agents. For more information, see [ServiceNow product tiers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/ai-native-sku-overview.md).
