# Exploring ITOM/OT SU Licensing

Learn more about ServiceNow® ITOM/OT SU Licensing, the different ITOM subscriptions it includes, and its benefit.

## ITOM/OT SU Licensing overview

The ITOM/OT SU Licensing application calculates and displays how many subscriptions for IT Operations Management \(ITOM\) applications your organization purchases and consumes. Subscriptions for the following ITOM applications are available: ServiceNow® ITOM Visibility, ServiceNow® ITOM Discovery, ServiceNow® ITOM AIOps, ServiceNow® Health Log Analytics, ServiceNow® ITOM Optimization, ServiceNow® ITOM Cloud Accelerate, ServiceNow® Digital End-User Experience \(DEX\), and ServiceNow® Service Observability. You can purchase subscriptions for each ITOM application separately \(a la carte\) or together \(in a bundle\).
