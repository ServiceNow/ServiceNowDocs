# View iteration records in EAP

View all Planning Intervals, Sprints, and other iterations across your teams in a single list, and see the child iterations that belong to a selected iteration in Enterprise Agile Planning.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_apw\_advanced.eap\_user

## About this task

The Backlog shows the iterations of one team at a time. Use the Lists page instead when you want to see iterations across teams, filter or sort them, or open the full record of an iteration.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **Strategic Planning Workspace**.

2.  From the left navigation panel, select **Lists**.

3.  Select **EAP Calendars**, and then select **Enterprise Agile Iterations**.

    The **EAP Calendars** section contains two lists. **Calendars** lists your planning calendars and appears first. **Enterprise Agile Iterations** lists the iterations.

    All iterations are listed, regardless of the team that they belong to. The list includes Planning Intervals, Sprints, and the iterations of any custom planning calendar that you created.

4.  Select the name of an iteration to open its record.

5.  To see the iterations that sit under this one, open the **Child Iterations** related list.

    For a Planning Interval, the **Child Iterations** list shows its child Sprints. A Sprint that has no iterations under it shows an empty list.
