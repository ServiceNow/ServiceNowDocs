# Reject a resource plan from the Resource Plan form

Resource managers can reject plans from the Resource Plan form or from the Resource Workbench.

## Before you begin

Role required: resource\_manager

## About this task

You can also perform this task from the [allocation workbench](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/yokohama/markdown/yokohama/it-business-management/resource-management/manage-resources-allocation-workbench.md).

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Resource** &gt; **Resource Plans** &gt; **Requested**.

2.  Open the plan to reject.

3.  Add a description of the reason for the rejections in the **Notes** field.

4.  Select **Reject**.
