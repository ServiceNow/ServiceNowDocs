# Manage Risk, Issue, Decision, Action, or Request Change \(RIDAC\)

Use RIDAC to identify, track, and manage risks, issues, decisions, actions, and change requests in Project Workspace.

## Overview of RIDAC

RIDAC stands for Risks, Issues, Decisions, Actions, and Change Requests. It is a structured framework that helps you maintain project health and governance by tracking all the factors that can affect your project's success. Each RIDAC element serves a specific purpose:

-   Risks: Potential events or conditions that may impact your project if they occur
-   Issues: Current problems or obstacles that are actively affecting your project
-   Decisions: Key choices made during the project that need to be documented and tracked
-   Actions: Tasks or steps that must be completed to address risks, issues, or decisions
-   Change Requests: Proposed modifications to the project scope, schedule, or resources
