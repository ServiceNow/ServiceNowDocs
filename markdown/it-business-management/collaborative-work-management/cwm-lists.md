# Manage lists in Collaborative Work Management

Access predefined lists and your own custom lists of records from any table, without leaving Collaborative Work Management \(CWM\).

## Lists in CWM

Lists is available under the Quick access section in the CWM left navigation, alongside **My Work**. Open predefined lists or build and save custom lists without navigating to **All** each time.

\[Omitted image "cwm-lists-default-lists.png"\] Alt text: Lists under Quick access in CWM.

## Key benefits

Lists provide the following benefits:

-   Access predefined lists of frequently needed Agile and Connected Work records, organized by category.
-   Build a custom list from any table, such as the Incident table, with the columns and conditions that matter to your team.
-   Save custom lists for quick access instead of rebuilding the same filter each time.

## How it works

**Tip:** Open the Lists panel using the keyboard shortcut **Alt**+**L**.

The Lists panel has two tabs:

-   **Default lists** shows predefined lists organized into categories. It is further divided into the CWM and CWM Agile categories.
    -   CWM includes Boards and Connected work.
    -   CWM Agile includes Epics, My Epics, Stories, My Stories, Active Sprints, and Closed Sprints lists.
-   **My lists** shows the custom lists that you create. This tab is empty until you create your first custom list. For more information, see [Create a custom list in My lists](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/it-business-management/collaborative-work-management/create-a-list-in-cwm.md).

Selecting any list, predefined or custom, opens its records in the main list view. From there, you can filter, sort, and group the records. Selecting the name of a record opens it in a new tab. You can also create records and edit them using the lists. Share the link for a list with users who have the appropriate permissions to view it.
