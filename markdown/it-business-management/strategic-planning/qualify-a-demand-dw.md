# Qualify a demand

Qualify a demand manually in Next Experience for Demand Management, without waiting for an assessment to complete. Qualifying a demand moves it to the Qualified state, where the demand manager can move it forward for approval.

## Before you begin

Demands must be in the Submitted or Screening state.

Role required: it\_demand\_manager

## About this task

Qualifying a demand this way is a manual override — it doesn't require a completed assessment.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **Strategic Planning Workspace**.

2.  Select the Demands icon \[Omitted image "demands-icon.png"\].

3.  Open a demand from the **List** page.

4.  Select **Details** from the navigation menu.

5.  Select **Qualify**.
