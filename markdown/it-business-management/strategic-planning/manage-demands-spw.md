# Managing demands in Next Experience for Demand Management

Next Experience for Demand Management is a workspace for creating, tracking, prioritizing, and approving demands.

From the **List** page, create, edit, or export demands.

\[Omitted image "demands-lists-page.png"\] Alt text: List view of demands with search, filter, sort, and group options

|Action|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Search|Search for demands by name.|
|Filter|Apply filters to the list. Selected filter preferences are retained in the filter criteria.|
|Sort by|Sort the demand list in ascending or descending order based on a selected field.|
|Group by|Group demands by a selected field, such as state or stage.|
|Refresh|Reload the list.|

|Action|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Personalize fields|Customize the columns displayed in the demand list to show only relevant fields.|
|Group by|Group demands by a column using the column header action.|
|Rearrange columns|Drag columns to any position in the grid.|
