# RIDAC

RIDAC \(Risk, Issue, Decision, Action, Change\) in Strategic Planning Workspace gives you complete control over planning uncertainties across your entire organization. Create, track, and manage risks, issues, decisions, actions, and changes—from individual planning items and strategic goals to agile teams and portfolios. Get a unified view across your entire planning hierarchy, stay aligned with organizational strategy, and make informed decisions at every level.
