# RIDAC Action form

Use the RIDAC Action form to create and track action items related to your demand or project planning items. Manage action item ownership, status, and completion.

|Field|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|Number|Unique identifier for the action. This field is auto-generated when the action is created.|
|State|Current status of the action. The available options are **Pending**, **Open**, **Work in Progress**, **Closed Complete**, **Closed Incomplete**, and **Closed Skipped**.|
|Short description|Brief, concise summary of the action item. This field is required and appears as the title of the action record.|
|Description|Detailed information about the action, including what needs to be done, why it is required, and any relevant context or background information.|
|Priority|Priority level of the action. The available options are **1 - Critical**, **2 - High**, **3 - Moderate**, **4 - Low**, and **5 - Planning**. This helps with action prioritization and scheduling.|
|Estimated Cost|Estimated cost in currency \(for example, USD\) to complete the action. Helps with financial tracking and planning.|
|Impact|Potential impact of the action item. The available options are **1 - High**, **2 - Medium**, **3 - Low**. This indicates the severity of the action on planning objectives.|
|Approval|Approval status of the action. Options include **Not Yet Requested**, **Requested**, **Approved**, and **Rejected**. Tracks whether the action item requires or has received approval.|
|Assigned to|User or team responsible for completing the action.|
|Due date|Target date by which the action should be completed.|
|Planning Item|The planning item \(project or demand\) that this action is associated with. This field is auto-populated when an action is created from a planning item context.|
|Parent|Parent action, if this action is dependent on another action or is part of a larger action group. Used for action hierarchy and dependency tracking.|
|Work notes|Internal notes and updates about action progress, blockers, completion status, and any changes. Used to track work history and communication.|
