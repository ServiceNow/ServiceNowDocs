# Submit a time card for a demand task

Record and submit the time spent on a demand task to calculate actual cost and effort. The reported hours are used to track work performed and support demand planning.

## Before you begin

Role required: timecard\_user

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Time Sheets** &gt; **Time Sheet Portal**.

2.  Create a time card.

4.  Provide the hours for each day spent working on the demand task.

5.  Select **Submit**.


## Result

The time card moves to the Submitted state and is sent to an approver for review. For details on the approval process, see [Approve or reject a time card](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-business-management/time-card-management/approve-time-card.md).
