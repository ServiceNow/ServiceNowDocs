# Data Quality tab

The Data Quality tab quantifies the demand, demand tasks, and assessment records that have missing information in key fields.

\[Omitted image "demand-dashboard-data-quality.png"\] Alt text: Data Quality tab in the demand dashboard

The dashboard displays metrics for:

-   Data quality
-   Areas requiring attention or improvement

You can further drill down on each widget to see the list of records associated with them.

|Widget|Description|
|------|-----------|
|No Demand Manager|Count of demands that don't have an assigned demand manager.|
|No Department|Count of demands that don't have an assigned department.|
|No Primary Goal|Count of demands that don't have a set primary goal.|
|No Business Case|Count of demands that don't have a defined business case.|
|No Planned Dates|Count of demands that don't have set planned start and end dates.|
|No Stakeholders|Count of demands that don't have any assigned stakeholders.|
|No Investment Class|Count of demands that don't have a selected investment class.|
|No Investment Type|Count of demands that don't have an assigned investment type.|

|Widget|Description|
|------|-----------|
|No Cost Plans|Count of demands in the Screening or Qualified state that don't have a defined cost plan.|
|No Resource Assignments|Count of demands in the Screening or Qualified state that don't have defined resource assignments.|

|Widget|Description|
|------|-----------|
|No Due Date|Count of demand tasks that don't have a set due date.|
|No Assignee|Count of demand tasks that don't have an assignee.|
|Overdue Demand Tasks|Count of demand tasks whose due date has passed as of the current date.|

|Widget|Description|
|------|-----------|
|No Due Date|Count of demand assessments that don't have a set due date.|
|No Assignee|Count of demand assessments that don't have an assignee.|
|Overdue|Count of demand assessments whose due date has passed as of the current date.|
