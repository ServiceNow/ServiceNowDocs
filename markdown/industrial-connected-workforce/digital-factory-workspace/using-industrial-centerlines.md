# Using Industrial Centerlines

Use Industrial Centerlines to create centerline standards and to confirm equipment settings through centerline tasks.

-   **[Centerline standards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/industrial-connected-workforce/digital-factory-workspace/icw-centerline-standards.md)**

    Define which equipment settings operators confirm, where they apply, and which materials they cover.

-   **[Centerline tasks in the Digital Factory Workspace](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/industrial-connected-workforce/digital-factory-workspace/centerline-tasks-workspace.md)**

    Confirm equipment settings against their setting specifications in centerline tasks.


-   **[Centerline standards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/industrial-connected-workforce/digital-factory-workspace/icw-centerline-standards.md)**  
A centerline standard defines which equipment settings operators confirm, where those settings apply, and which materials they cover. Centerline tasks generated from the standard check each setting against its setting specification.
-   **[Centerline tasks in the Digital Factory Workspace](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/industrial-connected-workforce/digital-factory-workspace/centerline-tasks-workspace.md)**  
Centerline tasks appear in a dedicated list view in the Digital Factory Workspace, where authorized users can view, filter, and manage task records generated from centerline standards.
