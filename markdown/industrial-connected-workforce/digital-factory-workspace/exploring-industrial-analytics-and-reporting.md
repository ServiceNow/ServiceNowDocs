# Exploring Industrial Analytics and Reporting

Learn how Industrial Analytics and Reporting embeds operational analytics in the Digital Factory Workspace so workers can monitor performance without leaving their workflows.

## Industrial Analytics and Reporting overview

Industrial Analytics and Reporting brings operational performance data into the Digital Factory Workspace as embedded dashboards. Workers monitor, analyze, and act on key performance indicators during shift handovers, daily reviews, and cadence meetings without switching applications.

Industrial Analytics and Reporting provides two analytics areas:

-   SQDC Analytics tracks safety, quality, delivery, and cost performance for a functional location through inline Overview and Delivery dashboards.
-   Shopfloor insights provide embedded execution analytics on Industrial Guided Tasks standards to support continuous improvement of manufacturing processes.
