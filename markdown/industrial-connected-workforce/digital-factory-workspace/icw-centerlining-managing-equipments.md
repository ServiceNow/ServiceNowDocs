# Industrial Centerlines standards and equipment settings

Manage Industrial Centerlines standards to define which equipment settings to verify and when. Define setting definitions, setting plans, and setting specifications to establish standard base conditions for operational equipment.

## Managing Industrial Centerlines standards and tasks

After equipment settings and their standard values are defined, you can create Industrial Centerlines standards and manage the tasks generated from them.

You must have the following roles:

-   Create and manage centerline standards: Centerline Standard Author \[sn\_icw\_ctl.centerline\_standard\_author\] role
-   Execute centerline tasks: Centerline User \[sn\_icw\_ctl.user\] role

## Managing equipment settings

Before you execute Industrial Centerlines tasks, you must define the appropriate equipment settings, create a setting plan, and specify their standard values.

You must have the following roles to perform these activities:

-   Industrial Centerlines Config \[sn\_icw\_ctl.config\] role
-   Industrial Centerlines Admin \[sn\_icw\_ctl.admin\] role

Setting definitions are managed from the **Settings** tab on the operational equipment form in the Digital Factory Workspace.
