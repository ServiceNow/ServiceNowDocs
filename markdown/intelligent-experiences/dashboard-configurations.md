# Dashboard configurations

Configure the tracking settings that determine which usage data appears on the dashboards in Control Tower.

Dashboard configurations determine the data that Control Tower collects for its dashboards. It is important to set up usage tracking. This ensures that the dashboards provide adoption data at the level of detail required by your analysts. Analysts need this information to assess how AI systems are utilized across your organization.
