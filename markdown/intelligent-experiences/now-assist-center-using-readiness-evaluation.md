# Using AI readiness assessments in AI Admin Center

Use AI readiness assessments to help you prepare to launch generative AI and agentic AI for your organization.

The AI readiness assessment features in AI Admin Center help you find actionable items in your implementation preparation and provides direct links to improve those areas.

-   **[Run the AI readiness assessment job in AI Admin Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/now-assist-center-run-assessment-job.md)**  
Run the AI readiness assessment to analyze your instance and evaluate its readiness for AI adoption.
-   **[View your AI readiness assessment in AI Admin Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/view-ai-readiness-now-assist-center.md)**  
Review the readiness assessments that the Now Assist Readiness Evaluation has identified for your instance.
