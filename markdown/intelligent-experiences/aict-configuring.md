# Configuring Super Intelligence Control Tower

Set up Control Tower so your organization can begin managing AI assets, enforcing governance policies, and tracking AI value.

Once you activate Control Tower and assign roles, you can begin managing your AI inventory and configuring governance behavior in the **Settings** tab. The core settings cover integrations, automation rules, value and evaluation templates, playbooks, and platform-level AI controls.

You can unlock additional capabilities in Control Tower by activating add-on plugins. Each plugin extends a specific area of the workspace.

-   **[Activating Super Intelligence Control Tower](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-activating.md)**  
Govern, secure, and measure the value of your AI assets across the enterprise by activating Control Tower.
-   **[Guided setup](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-guided-setup.md)**  
Configure Super Intelligence Control Tower in a logical sequence and track your progress from a single summary page, instead of navigating separate Settings pages.
-   **[Configure users and roles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/configure-users-and-roles.md)**  
Add and manage role to a user group to grant its members the corresponding permissions in Control Tower.
-   **[Configuring integrations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-configuring-integrations.md)**  
Connect Super Intelligence Control Tower to external AI platforms so you can discover AI assets, collect trace data for monitoring, and synchronize governance across multiple ServiceNow instances.
-   **[Configuring rules and templates](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-rules-and-templates.md)**  
Use rules and templates in Super Intelligence Control Tower to configure how AI assets are classified, evaluated, valued, and secured across your organization.
-   **[AI Gateway](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/ai-gateway.md)**  
Use the AI Gateway settings page to control global transaction processing for all connected MCP servers and manage individual MCP server connections independently of the global gateway state.
-   **[Control Tower playbooks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-playbooks-reference.md)**  
Drive the lifecycle and approval workflows for AI assets using playbooks in Control Tower.
-   **[Configuring ServiceNow AI settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-configuring-servicenow-ai-settings.md)**  
Use Super Intelligence Control Tower to decide what AI data your instance shares with ServiceNow, which AI assets need AI steward approval before deployment, and which AI model providers your AI systems can use.
-   **[Configuring pages](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-configuring-page-widgets.md)**  
View and govern your AI portfolio in Super Intelligence Control Tower using terms and visualizations tailored to your organization.
-   **[Creating or extending pages with pro-code tools](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/aict-create-extend-pages-pro-code-tools.md)**  
Extend your Super Intelligence Control Tower pages beyond the built-in configuration options with the ServiceNow Lux Lab for VS Code extension.
