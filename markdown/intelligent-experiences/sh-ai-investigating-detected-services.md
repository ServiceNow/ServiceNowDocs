# Investigating a detected AI service in Shadow AI

Understand exactly how an AI service is being used, before deciding whether to clear it or block it.

## Investigating a detected AI service workflow

When the Shadow AI surfaces a detected AI service that needs a closer look, open the service record to see who's using it and how. The service record includes an activity history and separate views of the devices, users, departments, events, and sources involved on the **Details** tab.

View the service details to analyze the service and decide how to proceed. Read the prompts it includes when Agent Client Collector \(ACC\) covers the service, dismiss it if it isn't a concern, or block it outright if it is. See [Detected AI service details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-service-details.md).
