# Exploring Content Understanding

Content Understanding brings generative AI to document and visual processing, so you can extract, analyze, summarize, and answer questions about the content in your files.

Content Understanding is a suite of multimodal AI features designed to analyze unstructured content such as PDFs, forms, and images. It transforms this content into structured, actionable data within the ServiceNow AI Platform. This suite includes a variety of skills, and agents that deliver answers, information, summaries, and more to enhance your workflows. By converting information from files into usable values, Content Understanding minimizes the need for manual data entry in your processes.

## Skills

Content Understanding includes the following skills:

-   **Extract information from documents skill**

    Extracts specific fields, tables, and answers to questions from documents and images. You can configure this skill and its use cases in the AI Admin Hub.

-   **Attachment summarization skill**

    Summarizes the contents of record attachments alongside the record summary in workspaces such as ServiceNow Otto for ITSM and ServiceNow Otto for HRSD.


## AI agents

Content Understanding includes Content insights AI agent. You can add this agent to an agentic workflow to process documents and images. You can configure this agent in AI Agent Studio.
