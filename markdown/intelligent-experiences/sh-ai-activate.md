# Activate Shadow AI

Activate Shadow AI in AI Control Tower.

## Before you begin

Role required: admin

## About this task

Activating Shadow AI enables **Shadow AI** under **Inventory**, and makes the Armis and Agent Client Collector \(ACC\) connector tiles available in **Settings** &gt; **Integrations** &gt; **Connectors**. No detection happens until you also connect at least one of those sources.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **System Definition** &gt; **Plugins**.

2.  Search for **Shadow AI**.

3.  Select the plugin, and then select **Activate/Upgrade**.

4.  Confirm the activation.

    Shadow AI appears under **Inventory**, and the Armis and ACC connector options appear in **Settings** &gt; **Integrations** &gt; **Connectors**.
