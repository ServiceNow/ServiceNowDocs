# PagerDuty Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for PagerDuty Spoke.

-   **[PagerDuty audit management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-audit-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves PagerDuty audit records to support compliance and operational review.
-   **[PagerDuty business service management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-business-service-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty business services. It creates, updates, and deletes business services and looks up business services, their subscribers, and their dependencies.
-   **[PagerDuty escalation policy management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-escalation-policy-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty escalation policies. It creates, updates, and deletes policies and looks up a specific policy or a list of policies.
-   **[PagerDuty extension management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-extension-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty extensions. It creates and updates extensions and looks up a specific extension or a list of extensions.
-   **[PagerDuty incident management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-incident-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty incidents. It creates and updates incidents and looks up a specific incident by ID or a list of incidents.
-   **[PagerDuty notification management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-notification-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves PagerDuty notifications so that users can review the alerts that were sent.
-   **[PagerDuty schedule management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-schedule-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves PagerDuty schedules. It looks up a specific schedule by ID or a list of schedules.
-   **[PagerDuty service management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-service-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty services. It creates and updates services and looks up a specific service by ID or a list of services.
-   **[PagerDuty tag management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-tag-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty tags. It creates, looks up, assigns, and deletes tags used to categorize PagerDuty resources.
-   **[PagerDuty team management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-team-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty teams. It creates, updates, and deletes teams, adds and removes users and escalation policies, and looks up teams, their users, and their subscriptions.
-   **[PagerDuty user management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-pagerduty-pagerduty-user-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages PagerDuty users. It creates, updates, and removes users, lists users, and looks up who is currently on call.
