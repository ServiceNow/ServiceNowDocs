# Coupa Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Coupa Spoke.

-   **[Coupa approval management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-approval-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Coupa approvals. It looks up an approval, a list of approvals, and approval details by approval ID.
-   **[Coupa catalog management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-catalog-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent creates catalog items in Coupa.
-   **[Coupa contract management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-contract-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Coupa contracts. It creates new contracts and looks up existing contracts.
-   **[Coupa inventory management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-inventory-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Coupa receiving and inventory data. It looks up receipts and receipt details, looks up advanced shipment notices by update date or purchase order line, and creates goods receipts.
-   **[Coupa invoice management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-invoice-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Coupa invoices. It creates invoices, looks up invoices, and retrieves invoice payment details.
-   **[Coupa item management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-item-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves items from Coupa. It looks up a specific item or a list of items.
-   **[Coupa primary data management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-primary-data-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Coupa reference data. It looks up conversion rates, cost centers, currencies, and legal entities.
-   **[Coupa purchase order management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-purchase-order-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Coupa purchase orders and fixed assets. It looks up purchase orders and purchase order lines, closes purchase orders, and creates fixed assets.
-   **[Coupa requisition management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-requisition-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Coupa requisitions. It looks up a specific requisition or a list of requisitions.
-   **[Coupa sourcing management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-sourcing-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Coupa sourcing events. It looks up existing sourcing events and creates new ones.
-   **[Coupa supplier management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-supplier-management-ai-agent.md)**  
The agent manages Coupa supplier data. It creates suppliers, looks up a supplier or a list of suppliers, and looks up product catalogs.
-   **[Coupa user management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-coupa-coupa-user-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Coupa users. It looks up a user or a list of users and resets user passwords.
