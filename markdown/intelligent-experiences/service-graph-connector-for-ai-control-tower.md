# Service Graph Connector for AI Control Tower

The Service Graph Connector \(SGC\) for AI Control Tower is a ServiceNow integration framework that connects AI Control Tower to external cloud hyperscalers and SaaS platforms to discover and import AI assets into the AI asset inventory.

## How does SGC work

When you access third-party APIs, they return data. This data must be stored in ServiceNow tables. Before saving it, we should transform the data from its JSON format into a preferred format and then store it in the ServiceNow tables.

## Benefits of SGC

Let’s explore some of the major benefits:

-   Gain trust in your CMDB data.
-   Minimize operational risk.
-   Minimize custom integrations.
-   Accelerate time to value.

To understand the benefits and features of SGC in detail, see [Service Graph Connectors](https://www.servicenow.com/products/service-graph-connectors.html)
