# Workday HR Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Workday HR Spoke.

-   **[Workday HR absence management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-absence-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves an employee's time off balances from Workday HR by employee ID.
-   **[Workday HR approval management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-approval-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Workday HR approvals. It looks up in-progress approval requests and approves or rejects business processes.
-   **[Workday HR feedback management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-feedback-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent looks up the feedback employees have received in Workday HR to support performance reviews and HR decisions.
-   **[Workday HR goals management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-goals-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent looks up employee goals in Workday HR so that users can review individual objectives.
-   **[Workday HR payroll management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-payroll-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent looks up employee payslips in Workday HR.
-   **[Workday HR performance management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-performance-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves performance review data from Workday HR. It looks up an employee's latest performance review and historical review data for tracking performance trends.
-   **[Workday HR resource management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-workdayhr-workday-hr-resource-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves employee resource data from Workday HR. It looks up worker profiles, holiday calendars, inbox items, and, by employee ID, documents, total rewards, and benefit enrollments.
