# Configure AI specialists inthe legacy AI Agent Studio

Configure your AI specialists in your autonomous workforce inthe legacy AI Agent Studio.

\[Omitted image "aiw-aias-create-manage.png"\] Alt text: Create and manage page in the legacy AI Agent Studio with the AI specialists tab selected, listing each AI specialist with its status and published state

The following is the basic workflow for configuring an AI specialist.

1.  [Profile](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/modify-aiw-profile.md)

    Set basic details like the AI specialist's name, icon, role, and assignment groups. Assignment groups are the most important setting because they determine which groups the AI specialist can act on behalf of.

2.  [Tasks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/modify-aiw-tasks.md)

    Define what the AI specialist can do, such as triaging incoming work, researching context, and responding to requesters.

3.  [Preview the AI specialist](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/test-aiw-ais.md)

    Test the AI specialist on an actual record, not a simulation, to see how it reasons and what actions it takes.
