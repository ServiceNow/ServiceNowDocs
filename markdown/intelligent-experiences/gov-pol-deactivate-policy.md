# Deactivate a policy

Stop a policy from running by deactivating it.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_ai\_governance.ai\_steward

## About this task

You can deactivate a policy when you want to stop enforcement. For example, you might deactivate a policy while you investigate whether it's blocking legitimate requests.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **AI Control Tower** &gt; **Home** &gt; **Govern** &gt; **Policies**.

2.  Find the policy you want to deactivate.

3.  In the More actions menu, select **Deactivate**.

4.  In the confirmation dialog box, confirm that you want to deactivate the policy by selecting **Deactivate policy**.


## Result

The policy is deactivated. You can still view policy history in the **Enforcement activity** tab.
