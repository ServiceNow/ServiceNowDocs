# Miro Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Miro Spoke.

-   **[Miro board management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-miro-miro-board-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Miro boards. It creates, updates, looks up, and deletes boards.
-   **[Miro board item management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-miro-miro-board-item-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent adds items to Miro boards. It creates card, shape, sticky note, and text items on a board.
-   **[Miro team management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-miro-miro-team-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Miro teams. It creates, updates, looks up, and deletes teams and adds or removes team members.
-   **[Miro user management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-miro-miro-user-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Miro users. It creates, updates, activates, deactivates, and deletes users and looks up users, including the authenticated user.
