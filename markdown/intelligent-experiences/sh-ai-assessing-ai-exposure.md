# Assessing AI exposure

See how much unsanctioned AI use exists across your organization, and where to focus first.

## Assessing AI exposure workflow

Determine how much unsanctioned AI use exists in your organization in the Shadow AI overview. The overview shows every AI service detected so far, how many are affected by a policy, and which services need attention first. In addition, you can curate the AI services registry to define what counts as an AI service worth tracking in the first place.

When a specific service needs a closer look, open its own record to see exactly who's using it and how. See [Investigating a detected AI service in Shadow AI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-investigating-detected-services.md).

-   **[Shadow AI overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-overview.md)**  
Identify unsanctioned AI use across your organization by reviewing detected traffic, AI-generated recommendations, detection methods, and policy impact.
-   **[Review AI traffic detected across your organization](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-review-detected-traffic.md)**  
Review what Shadow AI has detected across your organization, by service, by user, or by department.
-   **[Manage the AI services registry](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-manage-registry.md)**  
Manage the AI services that your organization want to track by marking an entry as not shadow AI, or by making it active again.
