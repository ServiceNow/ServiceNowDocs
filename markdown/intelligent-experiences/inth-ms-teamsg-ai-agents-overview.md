# Microsoft Teams Graph Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Microsoft Teams Graph Spoke.

-   **[Microsoft Teams application management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-ms-teamsg-microsoft-teams-application-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Microsoft Teams app installations for users. It looks up the apps installed for a user and installs an app for a user with their consent.
-   **[Microsoft Teams calendar management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-ms-teamsg-microsoft-teams-calendar-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Microsoft Teams meetings. It looks up meeting details and the occurrences of a recurring meeting, and deletes meetings.
-   **[Microsoft Teams channel management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-ms-teamsg-microsoft-teams-channel-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Microsoft Teams channels. It creates and deletes channels, adds and removes channel members, and looks up channels by ID or name, lists channels, and lists channel members.
-   **[Microsoft Teams chat management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-ms-teamsg-microsoft-teams-chat-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Microsoft Teams chat information. It looks up a user's chats and the content and details of specific messages.
-   **[Microsoft Teams notification management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-ms-teamsg-microsoft-teams-notification-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent posts ServiceNow updates to Microsoft Teams channels. It shares change request details, posts custom messages, and replies to existing channel messages.
-   **[Microsoft Teams team management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/inth-ms-teamsg-microsoft-teams-team-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Microsoft Teams teams. It creates, updates, archives, unarchives, and deletes teams, adds and removes members, and looks up a team, its members, or the teams a user belongs to.
