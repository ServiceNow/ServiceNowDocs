# Discovering AI assets

Populate your AI asset inventory automatically as Super Intelligence Control Tower discovers AI assets across external platforms, hyperscaler traces, and your ServiceNow instance.

Control Tower can discover AI assets and add them to your inventory automatically. For example, Control Tower discovers assets running on external platforms through connectors or traces, and detects ServiceNow AI assets natively as they're activated on your instance.

When assets aren't discoverable through automated methods, you can add them manually. See [Creating AI assets manually](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/intelligent-experiences/creating-ai-assets-newexperience.md).
