# Access guided setup

Configure AI Control Tower in a logical sequence and track your progress toward a complete setup.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_ai\_governance.ai\_steward

## About this task

The **Get started with AI Control Tower** widget on the **Home** page is visible only to users with the AI steward role. It tracks your overall setup progress and shows the setup area you're currently working on.

## Procedure

1.  Launch the guided setup experience from the **Home** page in AI Control Tower or the **Admin Home** page.
