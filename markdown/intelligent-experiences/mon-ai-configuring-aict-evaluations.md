# Configuring monitoring and evaluations in AI Control Tower

Set up monitoring in AI Control Tower by activating evaluations, adding metrics, and configuring metric templates.

## Configuration overview

Setting up monitoring involves enabling scoring for each type of AI system you want to evaluate, managing which metrics to include, and configuring metric templates that define how quality and safety scores are calculated. After the initial setup, you can adjust which AI systems are included in scoring as your portfolio changes.

1.  [Monitor a ServiceNow AI system](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/mon-ai-monitor-servicenow-ai-system.md)

    Begin monitoring quality for ServiceNow AI systems by activating evaluation scoring.

2.  [Monitor an external AI system](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/mon-ai-monitor-external-ai-system.md)

    Activate evaluation scoring for external AI systems, generate an API key, and configure trace data collection.

3.  [Configure evaluation metric templates](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/mon-ai-configure-metric-templates.md)

    Define how quality and safety scores are calculated by selecting metrics, assigning weights, and creating the scoring formula for each AI system type.


-   **[Activate evaluation scoring for ServiceNow AI systems](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/mon-ai-monitor-servicenow-ai-system.md)**  
Assess the quality of your ServiceNow AI systems by activating evaluation scoring in AI Control Tower.
-   **[Activate evaluation scoring for external AI systems](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/mon-ai-monitor-external-ai-system.md)**  
Assess the quality and safety of your external AI systems by connecting them to AI Control Tower and activating evaluation scoring.
-   **[Configure an evaluation metric template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/mon-ai-configure-metric-templates.md)**  
Adjust the scoring formula for a metric template to change which metrics contribute to quality or safety scores, or to change how much weight each metric carries.
