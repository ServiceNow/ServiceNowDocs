# AI specialists

AI specialists are autonomous AI units within the ServiceNow Autonomous Workforce. Each AI specialist is purpose‑built to perform a specific job function and deliver complete workflow outcomes, working within your organization’s existing governance, identity, and access controls.

## Key characteristics

Unlike task‑based AI agents, AI specialists plan, execute, and complete multi‑step work autonomously.

-   Outcome‑oriented execution: AI specialists own end‑to‑end workflows rather than individual tasks. They can investigate, take action, and resolve work autonomously.
-   Built‑in governance and identity: Each AI specialist operates as a user record and inherits least‑privilege access automatically, helping make actions auditable and policy‑conforming.
-   Role‑aligned design: AI specialists are aligned with familiar job roles \(for example, Service Desk or IT Operations\), so you can assign and manage them alongside human teams.
-   Continuous monitoring and feedback: You can view an AI specialist’s activity, execution history, and performance, and provide direct feedback to improve outcomes over time.

**Note:** Depending on your license, you will have access to certain application features, generative AI skills, agentic workflows, and AI agents. For more information, see [ServiceNow product tiers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/ai-native-sku-overview.md).
