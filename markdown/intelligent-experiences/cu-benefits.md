# Content Understanding benefits

Content Understanding includes AI-powered skills and AI agents that extract, process, and summarize information from documents and images. Each capability delivers a distinct benefit for administrators and end users.

|Capability|Type|Benefit|
|----------|----|-------|
|[Information extraction](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/cu-extract-information-from-document.md)|Skill|Transforms unstructured content in documents into structured fields, enabling teams to capture data without the need for manual entry.|
|[Attachment Summarization](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/attachment-summarization.md)|Skill|Condenses long attachments into concise summaries, so agents grasp the key points quickly.|
|[Content insights AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/cu-content-insights-ai-agent.md)|AI agent|Analyzes documents and images to extract relevant insights, enabling users to act on findings without reviewing source material.|
