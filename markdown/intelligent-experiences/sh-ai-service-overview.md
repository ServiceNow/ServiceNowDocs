# Detected AI service overview

Review the usage, and policies, and activity history of a detected AI service and act on what needs attention.

## Key benefits

-   Focus on what needs attention using AI-generated recommendations scoped to the current AI service.
-   View the current status, detection method, and key usage details for the AI service.
-   Determine whether a service has already been addressed and review prior actions taken on the service by checking the activity history.
-   Compare usage volume against user and department counts to judge potential data exposure.

Use the date range at the top of the record to change the period that the cards and views cover. Some cards on the **Overview** tab show how a value has changed since the previous period, which can help distinguish a growing pattern from one that has leveled off.

## Required roles

The AI steward \[sn\_ai\_governance.ai\_steward\] role is required to view the **Overview** tab on an AI service.

## Accessing the AI service overview

View the AI service overview by navigating to **All** &gt; **AI Control Tower** &gt; **Home** &gt; **Inventory** &gt; **Shadow AI** and selecting an AI service.

## Top action items

Determine whether a detected AI service has already triggered a risk-pattern recommendation by viewing the **Top action items** list. AI Control Tower surfaces AI-generated recommendations for risk patterns that require your attention, such as a single user driving most of a service's use or adoption spreading broadly across departments.

On the **Overview** tab of an AI service, the top action items are scoped to just this service.

**Important:** Top action items are generated by automated analysis of detected traffic patterns. Review the underlying traffic before acting on a recommendation.

## Key details

Review a detected AI service's current status and more in the **Key details** card.

-   Check the service's current review status in the **Status** field. See [Shadow AI status values](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-status-values.md).
-   Whether a block policy applies to the service, and how broadly in the **Policy status** field.
-   View what kind of work the service is built for and what data is likely flowing to it by checking the **AI category** field.
-   Determine which method or methods reported the service by viewing the **Detection methods** field. These determine which signals appear on the record. See [How Shadow AI detects unsanctioned AI use](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-how-detection-works.md).
-   See the date this service was initially discovered in the **First detected** field. Compare this against **Last activity** to tell a long-running pattern apart from something that appeared recently.
-   See the most recent date usage was detected in the **Last activity** field. A recent date on a service you already reviewed means the pattern is continuing.
-   View the total data sent to the service in the selected date range in the **Payload size** field. High volume relative to the event count suggests users are sending substantial content, such as documents or data extracts, rather than short prompts.

See more information on the **Details** tab by selecting the arrow icon. See [Detected AI service details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/sh-ai-service-details.md).

## Activity

View actions taken on a detected AI service by viewing the **Activity** card. The card records each change made to the service, by whom, and when.

-   Check whether a service has already been snoozed, dismissed, or blocked before deciding what to do about it.
-   Review the change history for a service that reappears after being cleared, since the record persists even after per-event detail ages out after 30 days.
-   Confirm who made a specific change and when, for a governance or audit review.

## Unique users

View the count of known users who used this service in the selected date range in the **Unique users** card. Note that unknown users aren't included in the **Unique users** count.

-   Analyze usage according to the unique user count. A single user accounting for most of the traffic is a different pattern than broad use across many people.
-   Check whether the user count is growing or has leveled off since the previous period, to judge whether a pattern is still spreading.
-   Compare the count against the **Departments** card to judge whether use is concentrated in one person or spread across a team.

View a list of users who have accessed the service on the **Details** tab by selecting the arrow icon.

## Departments

View how many known departments used this service in the selected date range in the **Departments** card. Unknown users from unknown departments aren't included in the **Departments** count.

-   Check whether use is confined to one department or spread across several departments. Adoption across many departments suggests the service is filling a real need your governed tools may not cover.
-   Check whether the department count is growing or has leveled off since the previous period, to judge whether a pattern is still spreading.
-   Compare the department count against **Unique users** to see whether usage is concentrated in one team or spread across the organization.

View usage by department on the **Details** tab by selecting the arrow icon.

## Payload size

Determine how much data has moved to and from a service by viewing the **Payload size** card. The card shows total payload size for the selected date range.

-   Compare payload size against the **Unique users** count to judge whether a few users or many users are responsible for the volume.
-   Check for a large payload against a small user count, which can indicate concentrated data exposure worth a closer look.
-   Check whether payload size is growing or has leveled off since the previous period, to judge urgency.

## Policies

Determine whether a policy already restricts a service by viewing the **Policies** card. The card shows the service's current policy status.

**Note:** Policies only apply to AI services detected by ACC.

-   Check for a status of **Partially blocked** to confirm specific users are already blocked, before deciding on a broader block.
-   Check for a status of **Not applied** to confirm no one has acted on this service yet.
-   Treat an applied policy as a starting point to review or extend, rather than a decision to make from scratch.
