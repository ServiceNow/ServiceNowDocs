# GovNotify Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for GovNotify Spoke.

-   **[GovNotify message management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-govnotify-govnotify-message-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent sends and tracks notifications through GOV.UK Notify. It sends emails, text messages, and letters and looks up notifications and their status.
