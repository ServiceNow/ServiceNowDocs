# Configuring Shadow AI in AI Control Tower

Activate Shadow AI and connect a detection source, such as Armis or Agent Client Collector \(ACC\).

## Configuration overview

Setting up Shadow AI involves activating the plugin and configuring Shadow AI connectors. Detection can't start until the plugin is active, and at least one detection source is connected.
