# Use AI specialists in AI Agent Studio

Onboard and view the performance and activity of AI specialists in AI Agent Studio.

-   **[Review performance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/view-aiw-performance-new.md)**

    Track resolution and coverage rates, incident outcomes, reassignment reasons, quality scores, and knowledge base article usage. Filter metrics by date and assignment group to find where the AI specialist needs tuning.

-   **[View activity](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/view-aiw-activity-new.md)**

    See which records the AI specialist has worked on, the state of each execution, and why an execution didn't complete. Rate individual executions as helpful or not helpful.

-   **[Manage access](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/manage-aiw-access-new.md)**

    Set the workspaces where the AI specialist operates, the roles that can use it, whether it's published, and whether users can create copies of it.

-   **[Create a copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/copy-aiw-new.md)**

    Configure a copy of an AI specialist with its own profile details, assignment group, manager, and role access. Copies inherit their tasks from the base AI specialist.

-   **[Deactivate](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/deactivate-aiw-new.md)**

    Stop the AI specialist from acting on records, for example while you edit its profile or tasks.
