# Edit a Threat Response policy

Change an existing Threat Response policy's threat type, scope, sensitivity, or follow-up actions.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_ai\_governance.ai\_steward

## About this task

You can change the threat type, which agents it applies to, and more by editing an existing Threat Response policy. Editing keeps the same policy record, along with its enforcement history. The policy type can't be changed. To switch types, create a new policy instead.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **AI Control Tower** &gt; **Home** &gt; **Govern** &gt; **Policies**.

2.  Find the Threat Response policy you want to edit.

3.  Select **Edit**.

4.  In the On threat section, update the threat category, which agents the policy covers, or the sensitivity.

5.  In the Do this section, add, remove, or update follow-up actions.

6.  Review the policy summary, then select **Save &amp; Publish**.


## Result

The policy takes effect with your changes. Enforcement activity from before the edit stays available in the policy's history.

## What to do next

Confirm the policy is working as expected by reviewing policy enforcement activity. For details, see [Reviewing policy enforcement in AI Control Tower](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/intelligent-experiences/gov-pol-reviewing-enforcement-activity.md).
