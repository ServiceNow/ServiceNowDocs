# AI inquiries

Ask a question or request information related to AI governance, such as a question about a policy, a process, or a specific AI asset.

An AI inquiry is a general request for information or clarification related to AI governance, such as a question about a policy, a process, or an AI asset. Unlike a case or an issue, an inquiry doesn't require an investigation or an action plan, only a documented response.

## Inquiry lifecycle

An AI steward or asset owner submits an inquiry, and it's triaged and moved into progress by an assignment group or assigned user. If the inquiry requires a decision, it routes to an approver, who approves or rejects it. The inquiry is closed once the requester has the information they need, or it can be canceled if it's no longer needed.

## Where AI inquiries appear

AI inquiries appear on the **Inquiries** sub-tab of the **Team**, **Assigned to you**, **Unassigned**, and **Submitted by you** tabs in Activity Center, depending on assignment.
