# Control enforcement points

The systems an AI Control Tower policy can act on.

<table id="table_pol_enforcement_points"><thead><tr><th>

Enforcement point

</th><th>

Description

</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>

**Agent Client Collector \(ACC\)**

</td><td>

A lightweight agent installed on employee devices. Blocks and unblocks AI agents by model or domain, enforced at the device regardless of which application or browser someone uses to reach it.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**AWS Bedrock**

</td><td>

Blocks and unblocks Amazon Bedrock agents.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**AWS Bedrock AgentCore**

</td><td>

Blocks and unblocks Amazon Bedrock AgentCore agents.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Azure AI Foundry**

</td><td>

Blocks and unblocks Azure AI Foundry agents.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform agents**

</td><td>

Blocks and unblocks Gemini Enterprise agents.**Note:** Applies to agents with unique identities only.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**Okta**

</td><td>

Blocks and unblocks a person's access through their Okta-managed identity.

</td></tr><tr><td>

**ServiceNow agents**

</td><td>

Blocks and unblocks AI agents on the ServiceNow AI Platform®.
