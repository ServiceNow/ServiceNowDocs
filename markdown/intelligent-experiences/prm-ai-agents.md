# PRM AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Partner Relationship Management \(PRM\).

-   **[Deal Registration AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/prm-deal-registration-agent-ai-agent.md)**  
Manages the end-to-end deal registration workflow. Creates, updates, and submits deal registrations for channel partners; resolves customer and product information; and handles product line item management including additions and removals.
