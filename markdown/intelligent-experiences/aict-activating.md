# Activating Super Intelligence Control Tower

Govern, secure, and measure the value of your AI assets across the enterprise by activating Control Tower.

For details on upgrading Control Tower, see the [Super Intelligence Control Tower Migration \[KB3144679\]](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB3144679) article in Now Support.

**Note:** Depending on your license, you will have access to certain application features, generative AI skills, agentic workflows, and AI agents.
