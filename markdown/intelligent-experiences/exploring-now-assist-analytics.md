# Exploring AI Analytics

Learn how AI Analytics enables users with the AI Analytics Viewer or AI Analytics Admin role to monitor the usage, value, and performance of generative AI features and capabilities offered under Now Assist.

## AI Analytics overview

The AI Analytics dashboard is built on the Platform Analytics experience. The dashboard contains indicators and breakdowns that provide actionable insights into the usage, value, and performance of your Now Assist implementation, including AI Guardian, Now Assist context menu, and search.

## AI Analytics users

|User|Description|
|----|-----------|
|AI Analytics Admin|AI Analytics admins monitor usage, value, and performance of the Now Assist implementation, including AI Guardian, Now Assist context menu, and search. They also configure skill-dashboard mappings to view individual skill usage and performance indicators.|
|AI Analytics Viewer|View the dashboard pages to monitor usage and performance indicators.|
