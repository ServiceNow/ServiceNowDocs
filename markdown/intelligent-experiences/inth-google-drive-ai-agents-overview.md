# Google Drive Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Google Drive Spoke.

-   **[Google Drive file and folder management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-google-drive-google-drive-file-and-folder-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Google Drive files and folders. It looks up files, folders, and revisions, creates folders, copies and deletes items, updates metadata, and copies files between Google Drive and ServiceNow attachments.
-   **[Google Drive permission management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-google-drive-google-drive-permission-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Google Drive permissions. It looks up, creates, updates, and deletes file and folder permissions and transfers ownership of all of a user's data.
