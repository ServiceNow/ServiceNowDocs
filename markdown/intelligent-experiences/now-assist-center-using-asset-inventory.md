# Managing AI assets in AI Admin Center

The assetlibrary lists the AI assets in your instance.

These assets include AI capabilities such as AI skills, AI agents, and AI agentic workflows. They also include conversational components that you can use to build an LLM conversation such as topics, subflows, actions, intents,and virtual assistants.You can also view your datasets, knowledge graphs, and catalog items.
