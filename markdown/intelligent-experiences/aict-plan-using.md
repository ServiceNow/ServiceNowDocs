# Planning and tracking AI work in AI Control Tower

Use the Strategize, Prioritize, and Execute pages in the Plan menu of AI Control Tower to manage AI work from goal alignment through delivery tracking.

AI Plan organizes portfolio work across three pages that reflect the natural progression of AI delivery. On the Strategize page, define goals and assess alignment. On the Prioritize page, review and route incoming AI demand. On the Execute page, track active projects and epics. Work on any page independently depending on your role and current focus.

|Page|Use when|Learn more|
|----|--------|----------|
|Strategize|You want to assess how AI systems align to organizational goals, review goal and target status, or identify strategic priorities that are off track.|[Strategizing the AI plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/intelligent-experiences/aict-plan-strategize.md)|
|Prioritize|You want to review incoming AI demand, product ideas, or feedback, identify intake with no owner, or create intake records.|[Prioritizing AI plan in AI Control Tower](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/intelligent-experiences/aict-plan-use-the-prioritize-tab.md)|
|Execute|You want to track active AI projects and epics, view delivery status, or create projects and epics.|[Monitoring AI plan execution in AI Control Tower](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/intelligent-experiences/aict-plan-execute.md)|
