# Google Meet Spoke AI agents

The following AI agents are available for Google Meet Spoke.

-   **[Google Meet artifacts management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-google-meet-google-meet-artifacts-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Google Meet artifacts. It looks up meeting recordings, full transcripts, and individual transcript entries.
-   **[Google Meet conference management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-google-meet-google-meet-conference-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Google Meet conference records. It looks up a specific conference or a list of conferences.
-   **[Google Meet participant management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-google-meet-google-meet-participant-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent retrieves Google Meet participant information. It looks up individual participants, lists of participants, and participant sessions.
-   **[Google Meet space management AI agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/inth-google-meet-google-meet-space-management-ai-agent.md)**  
This AI agent manages Google Meet meeting spaces. It creates, updates, and looks up meeting spaces, and ends active conferences.
