# AI Admin Center user experience \(Lux UI\)

Use the AI Admin Center experience to set up, monitor, and manage your AI solutions.

**Important:** Lux is the new user experience for AI Admin Center. For more information on the Lux experience, see [AI Admin Center user experience \(Lux UI\)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/ai-admin-center-lux-user-experience.md).

The Next Experience AI Admin Center workspace is being prepared for deprecation in the November store release and will no longer be supported. For more information on the Next Experience UI, see [AI Admin Center workspace \(Next Experience UI\)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/now-assist-center-workspace.md).

In AI Admin Center version 6.1, the Next Experience and Lux user interfaces are both available.

**Note:** This topic describes the AI Admin Center feature based on the Lux user interface \(UI\). If you're using the Next Experience UI for AI Admin Center, see the Next Experience UI version of this topic.

## Overview of the AI Admin Center experience

The AI Admin Center experience provides features that enable you to set up your AI solutions in a unified experience without switching between separate AI applications.

With the AI Admin Center experience, you can:

-   Access your integrated AI adoption capabilities using the side navigation panel.
-   Use the home page features to assess your instance readiness, set up AI solutions, find and activate automation opportunities, and open related AI applications.
-   Use the conversational interface in the ServiceNow Otto panel as your AI assistant to perform common setup, building, and question answering tasks.

## Access the AI Admin Center experience

To access the AI Admin Center experience, navigate to **All** &gt; **AI Admin Center** &gt; **Home** or **Admin** &gt; **AI Admin Center**.

## Layout of the AI Admin Center experience

The AI Admin Center experience includes:

-   1 - Side navigation panel
-   2 - ServiceNow Otto panel
-   3 - ServiceNow Otto box

The AI Admin Center home page provides features to quickly set up your AI implementation, find automation opportunities, check the AI readiness status of your instance, and access related AI applications.

The **Overview** page is tailored to suit your specific environment, role, and business objectives. The sections on your home page display accordingly. The plugin installations and data associated with your instance may affect your experience on the page.

The following sections may appear on your home page based on your instance.

### ServiceNow Otto search box

The ServiceNow Otto search box gives you a place on the home page to begin a conversation with your AI companion.

Use this conversational interface to begin a chat, where you can perform setup, solution building, and question answering tasks.

\[Omitted image "ai-admin-center-lux-home-otto.png"\] Alt text: ServiceNow Otto chat box on the AI Admin Center home page.

Type a request or select a quick action button to initiate a conversation. The ServiceNow Otto panel opens to continue your conversation.

For more information, see [Using the conversational experience in AI Admin Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/using-now-assist-panel-now-assist-center.md).

### Top actions

The **Top actions** section of the home page displays a series of AI-generated items that represent quick-start use cases. Each use case is tailored to your environment and proposed to help you implement AI adoption, best practices, and administrative setup on the platform.

\[Omitted image "ai-admin-center-lux-home-top-actions.png"\] Alt text: Top actions section of the AI Admin Center home page.

Select an action to begin, and a conversation opens in the ServiceNow Otto panel. In the ServiceNow Otto panel, you can use natural language to have your AI companion implement the use case.

After it is implemented, the use case disappears from the **Top actions** list.

Select **View all** to see your top actions on the AI Solutions page.

For more information, see [Activating actionable use cases from AI Admin Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/now-assist-center-actionable-use-cases.md).

### Readiness

The **Readiness** section shows the readiness levels of the applications and data quality in your instance based on the readiness assessments performed. Readiness assessments help you to determine whether your organization's instance is ready to implement generative AI and agentic AI features.

\[Omitted image "ai-admin-center-lux-home-readiness.png"\] Alt text: Readiness section of the AI Admin Center home page.

The instance health visualization summarizes the outcome of health checks across your AI applications. The checks look for AI applications that are recommended for installation or might otherwise require a version upgrade, dependency installation, or licensing.

The data quality visualization shows the ratings of the data quality per domain based on a series of assessment checks. Each bar shows the percentage of checks that ranked with a passing score.

Select **View all** to see the AI readiness page with lists for instance health and data quality checks.

For more information, see [Increasing AI readiness in AI Admin Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/now-assist-center-using-readiness-evaluation.md).

### Automation opportunities

The **Automation opportunities** section shows the most relevant opportunities to automate actions or workflows in your instance using AI. The opportunities appear as actionable cards, each showing the results of an assessment performed by AI Agent Advisor.

\[Omitted image "ai-admin-center-lux-home-opportunities.png"\] Alt text: Automation opportunities section of the AI Admin Center home page.

AI Agent Advisor analyzes your instance data to identify the leading AI opportunities, estimates the potential time and cost savings for each opportunity, and finds the AI agents needed to support the automation.

Select **View all** to see a list of all the opportunities identified by AI Agent Advisor. For more information, see [Using AI Agent Advisor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/using-ai-agent-advisor.md).

Select **Edit advisor settings** to configure the data source, filters, analysis schedule, and cost profile that AI Agent Advisor uses to identify the automation opportunities. For more information, see [Set up a data source for analysis \(Lux UI\)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/ai-admin-center-lux-set-up-data-source.md).

### AI tools

The **AI tools** section shows other AI applications on the platform outside of AI Admin Center that can help you build, manage, and monitor your solutions.

\[Omitted image "ai-admin-center-lux-home-tools.png"\] Alt text: AI tools section of the home page showing cards for related AI applications.

Each application appears in a separate card. Select the card to open the application in a separate browser tab.

## ServiceNow Otto panel \(Lux UI\)

The ServiceNow Otto panel is the conversational interface where you can interact with the AI companion to perform setup, solution building, and question answering tasks.

The AI companion in the ServiceNow Otto panel uses generative AI to accomplish AI setup and configuration tasks on your instance based on your requests. Describe what you want to accomplish, and the AI companion guides you through the process, executes actions with your confirmation, and answers questions about your instance and the AI products.

Use the ServiceNow Otto panel to perform the following types of actions:

-   Perform common administrator functions
-   Implement the setup and configuration of AI solutions
-   Answer questions and find help documentation
-   Activate and set up base system skills
-   Enable or disable native and machine translation
-   Configure language support for supported language models
-   View privacy policy details and guidance
-   View data sharing details and guidance
-   View data overflow processing details and guidance
-   View policy and model providers with management guidance
-   View active and deprecated AI model versions
-   Discover and assign AI agents and agentic workflows that implement use cases

For more information, see [Using the conversational experience in AI Admin Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/intelligent-experiences/using-now-assist-panel-now-assist-center.md).
