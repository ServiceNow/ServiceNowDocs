</table>## App Engine Management Center

The App Engine Management Center \(AEMC\) is a centralized location for admins and developers to govern and manage the full life cycle of custom applications, from idea submission to deployment and continuous monitoring.

When your applications are ready for release, use ReleaseOps in AEMC to automate deployment across your pipeline. ReleaseOps moves changes between instances, runs scans and tests, and releases changes to production, so you can deploy custom applications with greater predictability and less risk.

For more information, see [App Engine Management Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/application-development/app-engine-management-center/app-engine-management-center.md).

## ReleaseOps

ReleaseOps orchestrates the entire deployment process, including moving changes between instances, running scans and tests, and releasing changes, while giving release managers and teams transparency into each step. ReleaseOps increases the predictability and reliability of deployments and reduces the risk of releasing unwanted changes to production.

For more information, see [ReleaseOps](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/application-development/releaseops/releaseops-landing.md).

## ServiceNow Otto for App Engine

ServiceNow Otto for App Engine brings AI capabilities to your custom applications at runtime. Once an application is deployed, users can access embedded AI agents, skills, and agentic workflows to complete tasks directly within the applications they use every day. Because these AI assets are part of the application, they scale with your users and can help to improve productivity and efficiency across your organization.

For more information, see [ServiceNow Otto for App Engine](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/application-development/now-assist-for-app-engine/add-ai-to-custom-apps-with-now-assist-for-app-engine-enterprise.md).
