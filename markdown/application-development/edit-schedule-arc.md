# Edit a schedule in Arc

Change a schedule's name, instruction, or timing without creating it again from scratch.

## Before you begin

You have at least one schedule. For more information, see [Create and manage scheduled tasks in Arc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/application-development/create-scheduled-tasks.md).

Role required: sn\_app\_arc.user

## About this task

Editing changes the existing schedule. To keep the original and create a variation, duplicate the schedule instead.

## Procedure

1.  In Arc, select **Schedules** in the sidebar.

    Each schedule appears as a card that shows its name, instruction, how often it runs, and its next and last run.

2.  On the schedule's card, select the edit icon.

3.  Change the **Name**, **Message**, or the settings under **What time?**, and turn **Reminder only** on or off as needed.

4.  Select **Save changes**.


## Result

The card shows the updated details, and the schedule runs at its new time.
