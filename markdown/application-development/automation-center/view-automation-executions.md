# Manage and review automation executions

Manage and review your automation executions, including their states, so that you can make sure that they are on track.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_ac.automation\_business\_user, sn\_ac.automation\_technical\_user, or sn\_ac.automation\_admin

## Procedure

1.  2.  3.  On the **Lists** tab, under **Monitor**, select **Automation Executions**.

4.  Open the automation execution that you want to view.

5.  Review the information on the Automation Executions page.
