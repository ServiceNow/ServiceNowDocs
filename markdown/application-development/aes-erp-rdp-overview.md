# App Engine for ERP rapid deployment packs

App Engine for ERP rapid deployment packs are solution templates built for specific business processes in enterprise ERP workflows.

## Introducing App Engine for ERP rapid deployment packs

App Engine for ERP rapid deployment packs provide a prebuilt starting point that you can configure and extend to meet your business requirements without building ERP workflows from scratch.
