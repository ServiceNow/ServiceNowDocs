# Create a recovery team

Create a recovery team from the administration menu in the BCM classic Workspace with an admin role.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_bcm.admin

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Business Continuity** &gt; **Planning** &gt; **Recovery Teams**.

2.  Select **New**.

    The Recovery Teams record is displayed as shown in the following example.

    \[Omitted image "reco-team-record-admin.png"\] Alt text: Recovery Teams record.

3.  On the form, fill in the fields.

    |Field|Description|
    |-----|-----------|
    |Name|Name of the recovery team.|
    |Plan|Plan associated with the recovery team.|
    |Groups|Groups associated with the recovery team.|
    |Users|Users associated with the recovery team.|
    |Description|Description of the impact analysis.|

4.  Select **Submit**.
