# Raise an AI inquiry form

Use the Raise an AI inquiry form to submit your questions about AI systems, models, or datasets and their business applications.

See the following table for a description of the field values.

**Note:** Some fields in the Raise an AI inquiry form are available only when the form is accessed from Workspace. When the form is accessed from the Employee Center, these fields are not visible.
