# Unified content management

The Unified content management application is a centralized library of regulatory frameworks, standards, and citations to help meet compliance requirements.

The Unified content management application provides a workspace-aware interface to browse, preview, and install regulatory frameworks and citations directly from the Compliance Workspace. The application accelerates the adoption of operational compliance frameworks by providing pre-built, validated content aligned to major regulatory standards.

## Benefits of Unified content management

The Unified content management application provides the following benefits:

-   Framework selection: Choose the framework you want to use based on your organizational requirements.
-   Selective installation: Choose the specific citations associated with a particular framework and install them in the active state in your instance.
-   Version management: Integrate framework updates as they become available and are listed under the respective frameworks and regulations.
