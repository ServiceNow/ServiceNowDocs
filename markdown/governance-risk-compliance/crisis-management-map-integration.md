# Crisis Management map

You can integrate the Crisis Management application with the Crisis Management map to receive alert feeds on possible threats to your organization's business operations.

A threat feed is the data intelligence information of a potential or current threat that can pose a danger to an organization. It can stop its business routine or disable its business processes. Threat feeds can include but are not limited to the following examples:

-   Severe weather or tornado
-   Hurricane
-   Earthquake
-   Wildfire or red flag warnings
-   Flood
-   Civil unrest or riots
-   War or military conflict

As a BCM administrator, you can enable or disable threat feed integrations provided by the base system. You can also configure additional threat feeds into the Crisis Management application. The integration and configurations that you set up alert the crisis manager of the threats and aid to take appropriate action on time.
