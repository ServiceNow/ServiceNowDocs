# Update dependencies of BIAs in Self-Service

Update BIA dependency details in the BCM application using the contributor role.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_bcm.contributor or sn\_bcm.viewer

Contributors in the Contributors list and viewers can update BIA dependency details.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Self-Service** &gt; **My Impact Analysis**.

2.  View the list of business impact analysis records that are assigned to you and the BIAs that you can contribute to.

3.  Select the BIA record and view the details.

4.  Update the **State** of the BIA.

5.  To update the record, select **Update**.
