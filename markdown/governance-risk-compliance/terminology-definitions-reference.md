# Terminology definitions fields

Use these fields to review the seed terminology definition records installed with the Digital resilience third-party registers.

|Field|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|Row identifier|Sequence position of the record within its group, for example, 10 or 20. This field is read-only.|
|Column code|Code of the reporting column that the definition applies to. This field is read-only.|
|Group|Group that the terminology definition belongs to. This field is read-only.|
|Name|Name of the term being defined. This field is read-only.|
|Option label|Label of the option value associated with the term. This field is read-only.|
|Description|Internal definition of the term. This is the only editable field on the record.|
