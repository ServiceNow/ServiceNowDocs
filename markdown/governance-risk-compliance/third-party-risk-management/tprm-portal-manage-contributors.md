# Manage questionnaire contributors and ownership

Add or remove contributors for a questionnaire request, or transfer questionnaire ownership to another contact, so the right team members can collaborate on and submit the questionnaire.

## Before you begin

**Important:**

This task applies to Third-Party Risk Management \(TPRM\) with Smart Assessment Engine \(SAE\) usage only. Questionnaire contributor and ownership management may differ in Classic TPRM environments.

Role required: questionnaire owner for the specific questionnaire request.

## About this task

Use the **Manage Contributors** action to give additional team members access to help complete the questionnaire. Use the **Reassign** action to transfer ownership—for example, when the current owner is unavailable or when a different person needs to submit the questionnaire.

Only the current questionnaire owner can use the **Manage Contributors** and **Reassign** actions. Other contacts must request ownership or contributor access from the questionnaire owner.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to the **Assessments** tab.

2.  Open the questionnaire request.

3.  Choose the action based on what you need to do.

## What to do next

Notify new contributors or the new questionnaire owner so they know they have been added to the questionnaire.
