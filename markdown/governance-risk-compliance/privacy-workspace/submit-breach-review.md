# Perform RadarFirst analysis on a privacy breach assessment

Initiate a RadarFirst analysis to evaluate the breach against applicable privacy regulations and receive an overall risk estimate for each impacted jurisdiction.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_privacy\_case.privacy\_case\_analyst

RadarFirst must be configured in your instance.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to the **Privacy Workspace**.

2.  Select the List icon \[Omitted image "ListsIcon.jpg"\] Alt text:.

3.  In the **Lists** tab, select **Cases** &gt; **All breach assessments**.

4.  Open a breach assessment to analyze.

5.  Select **Initiate RadarFirst analysis**.

6.  On the Initiate RadarFirst analysis dialog, select **Initiate**.

    A RadarFirst summary appears in the **Overview** tab of the privacy breach assessment record.\[Omitted image "radarfirst-integration.png"\] Alt text: Breach assessment overview page displaying the impact of a breach jurisdictions and the number of individuals impacted by it.

7.  To view detailed guidance for each jurisdiction, select **View report**.

8.  Log in with your RadarFirst credentials.


## What to do next

Use the regulatory guidance to determine your organization's privacy obligations for each impacted region and data elements.
