# Transfer mechanisms

Transfer mechanisms identify the legal safeguard that permits a specific data transfer between locations.

Privacy regulations often restrict the movement of personal data across jurisdictions unless a recognized legal safeguard applies. A transfer mechanism records that safeguard on a data transfer.

Most transfers rely on reusable safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses \(SCCs\), Binding Corporate Rules \(BCRs\), adequacy decisions, or approved transfer frameworks. Some transfers rely on case-specific exceptions. For example, an individual may explicitly consent to the transfer, or the transfer may be necessary for legal proceedings. These exceptions are marked **Yes** under Special exemption.

|Transfer mechanism|Description|Special exemption|
|------------------|-----------|-----------------|
|Adequacy / Equivalence Decision|Formal determination by a competent authority that another country, region, or sector provides a level of personal data protection substantially equivalent to domestic standards, allowing transfers without further authorization.|No|
|Binding Corporate Rules \(BCRs\)|Internal policies adopted by multinational corporate groups and approved by a data-protection authority, or an equivalent regulator, to permit intra-group personal data transfers under enforceable and consistent privacy protections.|No|
|Code of Conduct or Certification Mechanism|Approved industry codes or certification schemes that demonstrate compliance with data-protection standards. Organizations that adhere to them and offer binding commitments can rely on these as transfer safeguards.|No|
|Contract in Interest of Individual|Permitted exception allowing a transfer when necessary for concluding or performing a contract in the individual's interest between the data controller and another party.|Yes|
|Contract Necessary for Data Subject|Permitted exception allowing a transfer when it is necessary for the performance, or pre-contractual steps, of a contract directly with the individual whose data is transferred.|Yes|
|Explicit Consent|Informed, explicit consent from the individual after being told of potential risks due to the absence of adequate safeguards.|Yes|
|Framework or International Arrangement|Formal agreement or structured framework between jurisdictions, recognizing that participating organizations provide adequate protection for cross-border transfers, such as a bilateral or multilateral transfer framework.|No|
|Legal Claims or Proceedings|Transfer necessary for establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims, including judicial or administrative proceedings.|Yes|
|Public Interest|Transfer required for reasons of significant public interest recognized in law, policy, or regulation.|Yes|
|Public Register|Transfer of information from a register that is legally intended for public access or for persons with legitimate interest, under defined access conditions.|Yes|
|Standard Contractual Clauses \(SCCs\)|Pre-approved or regulator-endorsed contractual templates that data exporters and importers adopt to ensure appropriate safeguards for cross-border personal data transfers.|No|
|Transfers within Same Regulatory Area|Transfers within jurisdictions or regions governed by the same privacy regime, such as an economic area, trade bloc, or federal system, that are not considered cross-border and therefore, need no additional mechanisms.|No|
|Vital Interests of Individuals|Transfer necessary to protect the life, health, or safety of an individual who can't provide consent.|Yes|
