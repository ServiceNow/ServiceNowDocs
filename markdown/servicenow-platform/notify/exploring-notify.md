# Exploring Notify

Use and integrate Notify with the third-party service providers as a communication channel to meet with the stakeholders to resolve the incidents.

-   **[Notify Twilio Direct driver](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/servicenow-platform/notify/Notify-TwilioDirectDriver.md)**  
Use the Notify-Twilio Direct driver to start and manage conferences and to send and receive SMS messages and calls.
-   **[Notify Zoom connector](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/servicenow-platform/notify/notify-zoom-connector.md)**  
Use the Notify Zoom connector to start and manage Zoom meetings from any task record, such as an incident or a change.
-   **[Notify voice and SMS capabilities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/servicenow-platform/notify/notify-voice-SMS-capabilities.md)**  
Notify provides support for SMS and voice channels for communicating internally with team members and externally with customers and contractors.
-   **[Notify Cisco Webex Connector](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/servicenow-platform/notify/cisco-webex-notify.md)**  
Notify Cisco Webex Connector enables you to manage and initiate a Webex meeting directly from an incident.
