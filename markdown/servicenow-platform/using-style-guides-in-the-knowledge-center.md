# Using style guides in the Knowledge Center

Use style guides to keep the tone, voice, and terminology of AI-generated knowledge articles consistent for writers and easy to understand for readers.

In the Knowledge Center, you create style guides to define the tone, voice, and terminology for AI-generated articles. You assign them to knowledge bases, and authors choose one when they generate an article.
