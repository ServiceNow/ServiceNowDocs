# View CI details on the Explore CI view

ServiceNow Otto answers your questions on CI classes and attributes to help you work in CI forms, dashboards, home pages, and other views on the workspace.

## Before you begin

Role required: cmdb\_inst\_admin

## About this task

In addition to using ServiceNow Otto on the Explore CI page, ServiceNow Otto provides detailed information on any CI form while you create, view, or update a CI record, as described in [View CI attribute descriptions on CI forms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/servicenow-platform/now-assist-for-configuration-management-database-cmdb/na-cmdb-skill-ci-form-help.md).

## Procedure

1.  While viewing a CI on the Explore CI view, select **Ask Otto**.

    The ServiceNow Otto panel opens. Enter a question about any field or section on the view. For more information, see [Working in the ServiceNow Otto panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/intelligent-experiences/now-assist-panel-overview.md).
