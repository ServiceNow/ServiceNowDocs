# Healthcare organizations and locations in Healthcare and Life Sciences Service Management Core

Healthcare and Life Sciences Service Management Core uses the same healthcare organization and healthcare location model as Healthcare Operations Core.

Healthcare organizations and healthcare locations aren't specific to Healthcare and Life Sciences Service Management Core. They're shared, foundational tables that Healthcare Operations Core owns and that every Healthcare Operations application, including Healthcare and Life Sciences Service Management Core, builds on.

For information about the healthcare organization and healthcare location data model and how to set them up, see [Organization and location relationships in Healthcare Operations Core](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/healthcare-life-sciences/healthcare-operations-core/hco-organization-location-relationships.md) in Healthcare Operations Core.
