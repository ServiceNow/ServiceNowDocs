# Input Care team case details into the Care team activities playbook

Complete the **Care team case** playbook stage in the Care team activities playbook.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_cto.loc\_support\_agent and sn\_hco\_orc.plan\_author

## Procedure

1.  In the **Care team activities playbook**, navigate to the **Care team case** stage.

2.  Provide a **Short description**.

3.  Set the **Priority**.

4.  Enter a detailed description.

5.  Select **Save &amp; continue.**
