# View Partner Passport Portal purchase logs

Review the partner application purchases made on your instance, including who made each purchase, what it cost, and when.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_wdf\_token.wdf\_admin

## About this task

Purchases appear in the purchase log as soon as they're confirmed, so you can use the log as a record of each purchase.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Workflow Data Fabric Usage** &gt; **Partner Passport**.

2.  Open the **Purchase Logs** tab.


## Result

The purchase log lists each purchase with the following information:

-   **Application**: The plan purchased, such as Boomi Starter.
-   **Bundle &amp; Quantity**: The number of plans purchased. Each purchase is for one plan.
-   **Cost**: The fabric credits deducted.
-   **User**: The user who made the purchase.
-   **Updated**: The date and time of the purchase.
