# Prerequisites for setting up Boomi

Before you set up Boomi and purchase a Boomi plan, verify that your subscription, instance, and role meet the requirements.

-   Your organization has a Workflow Data Fabric Advanced subscription.
-   You're working on a production instance. Purchases are supported only on production instances, at the instance level.
-   You have the **sn\_wdf\_token.wdf\_admin** role to use the Partner Passport Portal and purchase plans.
-   You can accept the Boomi terms and conditions for your organization. You accept them when you install the Boomi application in the ServiceNow store.
