# Explore Partner Passport Portal

With Partner Passport Portal, you can use your committed fabric credits to purchase partner applications from your instance.

Partner Passport Portal is an application in Workflow Data Fabric. You can use it to purchase partner applications using Fabric Credits your organization has already committed, without a separate procurement cycle.

The portal lists the partner applications available to your instance along with the Fabric Credit cost of each. It also shows your available Fabric Credit balance and retains a record of every purchase you make.

Boomi, a low-code integration platform as a service \(iPaaS\), is the partner application available in the Partner Passport Portal.

\[Omitted image "partner-portal-home.png"\] Alt text: Partner Passport Portal page showing fabric credit balance and Boomi partner application listing with pricing and key features.

## Why use Partner Passport Portal

Fabric Credits are committed for Workflow Data Fabric capabilities. Partner Passport Portal extends what those credits cover: you can direct them toward partner applications as well, broadening the capabilities available to you under an existing commitment.

-   Purchase without a procurement cycle. Partner applications are paid for with committed Fabric Credits, so no purchase order, vendor contract, or additional budget approval is needed.
-   Evaluate and purchase in one place. Browse partner applications, check their cost against your balance, and purchase without leaving Workflow Data Fabric.
-   Track spend in one view. Purchases appear in the Credit Usage dashboard alongside your other Workflow Data Fabric consumption.

## Availability

Partner Passport Portal is pre-installed on every instance. No separate plugin or installation is required. Check your entitlements to determine whether you can purchase partner applications. Purchases are supported only on production instances only. Fabric Credits apply at the instance level and cannot be pooled across instances.

## Navigation

Navigate to **All** &gt; **Workflow Data Fabric Usage** &gt; **Partner Passport**. The portal has two tabs:

-   **Partner Applications**: Has two sections:
    -   **Fabric credit balance**: Shows your **Available credits** \(fabric credits remaining to spend, which decrease with each purchase\) and your **Total entitled credits** \(fabric credits included in your Workflow Data Fabric Advanced or higher subscription\). It also shows the date that your fabric credits reset.
    -   **Partner application**: Shows a card for each partner application, with its description, key features, and the **View plans** and **Get started** buttons.
-   **Purchase Logs**: Lists every purchase made on the instance. For more information, see [View Partner Passport Portal purchase logs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/integrate-applications/view-partner-passport-portal-purchase-logs.md). The Partner Passport Portal records purchases but doesn't track usage. You view usage in the partner application.
