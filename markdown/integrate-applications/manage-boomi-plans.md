# Boomi partner application

Boomi is a low-code integration platform as a service \(iPaaS\) that's available as a partner application in Partner Passport Portal. Set up access to Boomi, and then purchase Boomi plans using your fabric credits.

To start using Boomi, set up Boomi access, and then purchase a Boomi plan. The full cost of a plan is deducted from your fabric credit balance when you confirm the purchase, with no recurring charge. Boomi tracks your usage, which you view on the Boomi platform. For details on how purchased capacity is added to your Boomi account and how long it stays valid, see the [Boomi capacity update article \[KB3225968\]](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=kb3225968).

-   **[Prerequisites for setting up Boomi](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/integrate-applications/prerequisites-partner-passport-portal.md)**  
Before you set up Boomi and purchase a Boomi plan, verify that your subscription, instance, and role meet the requirements.
-   **[Set up Boomi access in Partner Passport Portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/integrate-applications/set-up-boomi-access.md)**  
Install the Boomi store application and request a Boomi account before you purchase your first Boomi plan.
-   **[Purchase a Boomi plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/integrate-applications/purchase-boomi-plan.md)**  
Purchase a Boomi plan with fabric credits to add connections and transactions to your Boomi account.
