# Conversations

The conversations view brings ServiceNow Otto chat activity into Usage Insights. It reports engagement metrics such as total chat users and live agent transfers, chat-related events, from starting a conversation to rendering a chat response to selecting a chat action.

You can analyze conversation trends over time and break them down by dimensions such as, country. This lets you answer questions such as how many users are having chat conversations in a particular region.

## Who uses the conversations view

The **Conversations** view is intended for analysts and product managers who:

-   Understand the volume and quality of conversational engagement at a glance.
-   Track live agent transfers to identify where conversational AI hands off to a human.
-   Examine a single conversation to review the exact sequence of events that occurred.

## What the conversations view contains

-   **Conversation metrics**

    Engagement measures such as total chat users and live agent transfers.

-   **Trend analysis**

    Conversation activity over the selected date range, which can be segmented by the global filters.

-   **Conversation list**

    A list of individual conversations. Selecting a conversation ID opens that conversation's activity and all of its chat-related events.
