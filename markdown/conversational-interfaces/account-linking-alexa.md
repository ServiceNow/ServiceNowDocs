# \(Legacy\) Account linking with Alexa

Link your Alexa account with your ServiceNow instance to retrieve information from your custom skill.

## Link your Alexa account with your ServiceNow® instance

1.  Configure your ServiceNow instance to link it with your Alexa account.

    For more information, see [Create an OAuth API endpoint for external clients](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/platform-security/t_CreateEndpointforExternalClients.md).

2.  To configure your Alexa account for account linking, see [\(Legacy\) Configure your Alexa account](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/conversational-interfaces/configure-alexa-console.md).
3.  To link the Alexa events with your ServiceNow instance using your macOS Terminal, see [\(Legacy\) Link Alexa events using the macOS Terminal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/conversational-interfaces/link-alexa-mac-terminal.md).
4.  To link your ServiceNow instance with your Alexa account using the Alexa app, see [\(Legacy\) Link your account using the Alexa app](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/conversational-interfaces/link-using-alexa-app.md).

-   **[\(Legacy\) Configure your Alexa account](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/conversational-interfaces/configure-alexa-console.md)**  
Configure your Alexa account to link it with your ServiceNow instance.
-   **[\(Legacy\) Link Alexa events using the macOS Terminal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/conversational-interfaces/link-alexa-mac-terminal.md)**  
Link Alexa events using your macOS Terminal to complete the Alexa account linking process with your ServiceNow instance.
-   **[\(Legacy\) Link your account using the Alexa app](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/conversational-interfaces/link-using-alexa-app.md)**  
Link your ServiceNow account using your Alexa mobile application.
