# IT Service Management Advanced release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® IT Service Management Advanced application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

-   **Version 3.4.0 - October 2026**

    IT Service Management Advanced extends the Service Desk experience into proactive service operations, adding capabilities such as Major Incident, Change, Problem, On-Call, and Walk-up. Fulfillers work in a streamlined, AI-assisted workspace where Now Assist is embedded directly on the record, supporting faster triage, guided resolution, and end-to-end management of incidents and requests.
