# ERP Rapid Deployment Packs release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® ERP Rapid Deployment Packs application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 33.0.0 - August 2026**
    -   Overcome the Rigid Bottlenecks of Legacy Systems of Record
    -   In the modern enterprise, core transactional landscapes \(such as legacy ERP platforms\) serve as essential systems of record, yet their operational complexity frequently restricts business agility. Standard adjustments, multi-domain governance updates, and routine entity onboarding become bottlenecked within heavy technical queues, adding unneeded administrative friction to everyday business changes.
    -   Welcome to the Agile System of Engagement
    -   The ServiceNow ERP Rapid Deployment Packs \(RDP\) introduce a cloud-native, lightweight orchestration layer natively on your ServiceNow platform. This architecture allows business teams to request, enrich, validate, and authorize critical corporate and ledger data before any data is permanently committed downstream. Built with a robust, clean isolation layer, the platform coordinates seamlessly with your target systems of record to eliminate manual workflows, reduce corporate compliance risks, and ensure absolute operational velocity.
