# Integrated Risk Management Enterprise release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Integrated Risk Management Enterprise application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 23.0.2 - September 2026 \(Brazil\)**

    Fixed: Entitles users to enterprise IRM features and applications such as Policy and Compliance, Risk management, Case management, Profiles and more on multiple family patch upgrades like Australia Patch 5, Zurich Patch 12 and Brazil etc

-   **Version 22.3.0 - June 2026 \(Australia\)**

    Integrated Risk Management \(IRM\) Enterprise enables organizations to proactively manage risk, compliance, and audit programs at scale through a connected platform enhanced with generative AI capabilities.
