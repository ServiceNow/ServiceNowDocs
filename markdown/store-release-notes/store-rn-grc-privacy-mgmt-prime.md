# Privacy Management Prime release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Privacy Management Prime application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 23.0.2 - September 2026 \(Brazil\)**

    Privacy Management Prime lets teams move from AI that summarizes work to AI that does the work. It includes all AI capabilities in Privacy Management Advanced, plus the ability to build custom AI Agents, skills, and workflows on top of it. Privacy and compliance teams can configure their own agents against the same case, risk, and control data already running across the platform.
