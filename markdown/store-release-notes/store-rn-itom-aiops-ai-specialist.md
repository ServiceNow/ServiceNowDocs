# AIOps AI Specialist release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® AIOps AI Specialist application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 3.0.0 - November 2026**
    -   AIOps AI Specialist is an AI Worker that takes over the first line of alert operations in ServiceNow AIOps. It triages incoming alerts, investigates probable cause using your CMDB, topology, and operational context, and closes noise so your operators can focus on the issues that require human attention.
    -   Instead of making operators sift through alert storms, the AIOps AI Specialist works with each alert group the way an experienced operations engineer would. It gathers evidence, decides whether action is needed, and records its reasoning so every decision can be reviewed. Administrators decide how much autonomy it gets, from recommend-only to automatic closure of noise.
    -   Built on the ServiceNow Otto platform, the AIOps AI Specialist helps reduce mean time to resolution \(MTTR\) and alert fatigue without replacing your existing monitoring tools or processes.
