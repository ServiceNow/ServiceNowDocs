# AI Agents for CSM - Major Issue Management release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® AI Agents for CSM - Major Issue Management application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.0 - November 2026**

    Includes AI agents that can automatically detect a major issue and recommend next steps, aid the major case manager with communication around a major case, assistant finding impacted customers and tracking updates.
