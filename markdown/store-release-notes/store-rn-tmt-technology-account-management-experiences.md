# Technology Account Management Experiences release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Technology Account Management Experiences application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.5 - September 2026**
    -   Account 360: A new multi-tab account workspace giving CSMs and account executives a consolidated view of every account without switching apps.
        -   Overview — Account details, team and customer contacts, key milestones, risk signals, escalations, touchpoint timeline, and a customer activity feed. Quick-create actions directly from the record header.
        -   Account Health — Health indicator visualizations with benchmark comparisons and an aggregated product adoption score.
        -   Financials — ACV, ARR, at-risk ARR, renewal details, and an ACV-over-time trend.
        -   Open Work — Escalated items, SLA achievement, SLA success rate \(year-over-year, quarter-over-quarter, month-over-month\), and aged work items.
        -   Product Adoption — Sold products, adoption scores, product adoption roadmap, top products at risk, and adoption forecast.
        -   Implementations tab, predictive health score, renewal confidence score, Create Touchpoint action from the record header.
    -   Executive Portfolio: A new portfolio-level dashboard for executives and senior CSMs to monitor a full book of business.
        -   Portfolio view — Multi-account view with geo and region filters.
        -   Overview — Renewal pipeline, opportunities by stage, and gross renewal rate.
        -   Financials — At-risk ARR and quarterly ARR trends.
        -   Adoption &amp; Health — Undeployed apps, cases opened by month, and portfolio-wide health roll-ups.
-   **Version 1.1.5 - September 2026 \(Australia\)**
    -   Experience for Sales, Service, and Success
        -   A new Experience plugin that unifies the account lifecycle across the ServiceNow platform, bringing Sales, Service, and Success onto a shared workspace so every persona who touches an account operates from the same source of truth.
        -   Account 360 gives CSMs and account executives one place to understand the full state of a relationship, so they can see where an account stands financially, how healthy it is, whether adopted products are delivering value, and what work is open or at risk, without stitching the picture together across systems. By surfacing risk signals, renewal confidence, and adoption trajectory alongside day-to-day open work, it helps account teams act before problems threaten renewal or expansion.
        -   Executive Portfolio lifts that same intelligence to the book-of-business level, giving executives and senior CSMs a clear read on portfolio health, renewal pipeline, and revenue at risk across every account, filterable by geography and region. It turns hundreds of individual account signals into the roll-ups leaders need to spot exposure, prioritize attention, and steer the business. Together they span the full range of account-facing roles:
            -   Account 360 for the individual relationship, Executive Portfolio for the book of business, grounded in the same account data.
