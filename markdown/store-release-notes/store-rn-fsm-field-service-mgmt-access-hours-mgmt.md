# Field Service Management Access Hours Management release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Field Service Management Access Hours Management application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 30.0.6 - September 2026**
    -   Field Service Management Access Hours Management allows you to define and enforce preferred time windows during which field service technicians can arrive and complete work at a customer site. Access hours ensure that work is scheduled only during times when the location, asset, or customer is available to receive service.
    -   You can configure access hour preferences at multiple levels such as Account, Consumer, Location, Asset, or a combination like Account and Location — to match their operational needs. When a new work order task is created, the application automatically determines and applies the appropriate access hours based on the configured matching criteria. If a task is scheduled outside of the defined access hours, dispatchers are notified so they can take corrective action.
