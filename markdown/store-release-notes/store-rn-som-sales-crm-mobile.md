# Sales CRM Mobile release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Sales CRM Mobile application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.0 - September 2026**

    Sales CRM Mobile is a field-ready mobile application designed to keep sales representatives productive and connected wherever they work. It provides real-time access to pipeline and customer data, enabling reps to manage opportunities, leads, meetings, notes, and tasks from a single, unified platform. With a mobile-optimized experience, sales teams can stay informed and respond quickly to customer needs.
