# Service Graph Connector for Nautobot release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Service Graph Connector for Nautobot application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

-   **Version 1.0.1 - October 2026**

    The Service Graph Connector for Nautobot imports IP and network data quickly, seamlessly, and securely. Nautobot is an open, extensible Network Source of Truth and automation platform designed to bring clarity, consistency, and intelligence to modern network operations. Built on Django and backed by PostgreSQL or MySQL, it provides a powerful foundation for modeling your network, enforcing data integrity, and driving automation at scale. ​

    With an accurate and well-maintained CMDB enriched by Nautobot’s authoritative network data, the organization can identify configuration issues, assess dependencies, and support effective network operations, change management, and service management processes across the entire environment.​

    The Service Graph Connector for Nautobot imports data consistently with the other connectors and the rest of the ServiceNow ITOM suite, ensuring your CMDB is clean, consistent, and fully aligned with all the network information across your environment. Its plug-and-play configuration is compatible with SGC Central, making it easy and straightforward to set up.

    The connector supports selective subnetwork filtering using Nautobot namespaces and network type \(IPv4 or IPv6\). It also handles the life cycle of networks and IP addresses, giving you complete discretion on your policies.
