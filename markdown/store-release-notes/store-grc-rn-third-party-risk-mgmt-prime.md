# Third-party Risk Management Prime release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Third-party Risk Management Prime application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 23.1.0 - November 2026 \(Brazil\)**

    Third‑party Risk Management Prime is an AI‑enabled offering that delivers generative AI capabilities for assessment reviews and issue management. It helps third‑party risk managers and assessors review third‑party risk information efficiently by reducing manual analysis and improving review consistency. With Third‑party Risk Management Prime, users can leverage generative AI to summarize third‑party risk issues and identify potential issues during an assessment review. These capabilities help to make informed and consistent decisions related to risk while working with third‑party risk records.
