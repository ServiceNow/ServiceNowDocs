# Field Service - Questionnaire release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Field Service - Questionnaire application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.5 - September 2026**
    -   Questionnaires give field service organizations a way to standardize what technicians capture while they're on the job, instead of relying on freeform notes or paper checklists that vary by person. Questions adapt based on prior answers, so agents only see what's relevant to the task in front of them.
    -   Technicians can attach photos, scan barcodes to identify assets on the spot, and complete assessments that are marked mandatory before a task can close. That gives organizations a consistent record of what was actually done in the field, and a way to enforce compliance steps that can't be skipped.
