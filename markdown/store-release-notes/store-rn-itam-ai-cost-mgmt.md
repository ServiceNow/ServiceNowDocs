# AI Cost Management release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® AI Cost Management application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.2 - November 2026**

    The ServiceNow AI Cost Management application gives you comprehensive visibility and control over your AI spend and usage. The application consolidates cost and usage data from your external AI accounts, helping you track and optimize the total AI spend across your organization.
