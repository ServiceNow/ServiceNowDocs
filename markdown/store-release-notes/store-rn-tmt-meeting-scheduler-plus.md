# Meeting Scheduler Plus release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Meeting Scheduler Plus application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

-   **Version 1.0.4 - October 2026**

    Meeting Scheduler Plus extends the meeting management experience with structured agenda and follow-up tracking. Customer Success Managers can now plan, track, and triage every aspect of a customer meeting — before, during, and after — without losing context between sessions. This release replaces the freeform agenda text field with per-item records, and introduces a dedicated capture layer for unresolved follow-ups so nothing falls through the cracks post-meeting.
