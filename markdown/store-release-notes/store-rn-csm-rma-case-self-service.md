# RMA Case Self-Service release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® RMA Case Self-Service application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.6 - September 2026**

    The RMA Customer Portal enables customers to independently review and manage RMA cases for product returns, replacements, and related workflows. It gives customers direct visibility into RMA cases and their associated case-lines, whether created through the RMA AI agent or other sources. Customers can track case status, review case-line details, and stay informed on progress without needing to contact an agent—reducing dependence on agent intervention, driving higher CSAT through greater transparency, and enhancing the experience with self-service access and timely, clear updates.
