# E-Signature for EmployeeWorks and Employee Slate release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® E-Signature for EmployeeWorks and Employee Slate application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

-   **Version 1.0.0 - October 2026**

    E-Signature for EmployeeWorks and Employee Slate brings the HR E-signature task directly into ServiceNow EmployeeWorks.

    Employees can open the document assigned to them, read it in full, and sign it with a typed or drawn signature, all in the same experience as the rest of EmployeeWorks. Once signed, the document is saved to the employee's record and the HR process moves forward automatically.

    This application delivers the foundational E-signature experience in EmployeeWorks, helping organizations speed up document sign-offs like offer letters, policy acknowledgements and agreements, while keeping the employee experience consistent.
