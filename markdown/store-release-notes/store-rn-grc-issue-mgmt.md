# GRC: Issue Management release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® GRC: Issue Management application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 23.0.10 - September 2026 \(Brazil\)**

    Manage control gaps, policy exceptions, and remediation activities in one place. Route issues through structured review and multi-level approval workflows, maintain a complete audit trail, and track progress from identification to resolution. Standardized templates help teams work consistently, while real-time status visibility makes it easy to communicate updates to stakeholders, leadership, and auditors.
