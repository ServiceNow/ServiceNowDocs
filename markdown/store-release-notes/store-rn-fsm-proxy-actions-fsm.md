# Proxy Actions for Field Service Management release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Proxy Actions for Field Service Management application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.4 - September 2026**

    Field service work shouldn't stop just because a technician is out of signal or without a device. Proxy Actions for Field Service Management lets managers and dispatchers step in and act on a technician's or crew's work order tasks on their behalf — accepting, starting, and closing work and more. Built on ServiceNow's granular service delegation framework with a dedicated Field Service policy view, it gives managers and dispatchers precise control over who can act for whom, with every proxied action logged to the work order's activity stream and attributed to the proxy for a clear audit trail.
