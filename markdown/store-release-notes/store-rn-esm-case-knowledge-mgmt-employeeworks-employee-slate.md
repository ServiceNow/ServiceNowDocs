# Case and Knowledge Management for EmployeeWorks and Employee Slate release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Case and Knowledge Management for EmployeeWorks and Employee Slate application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

-   **Version 1.5.0 - October 2026**

    Case and Knowledge Management for EmployeeWorks and Employee Slate brings HR case tasks, approvals and delegation directly in ServiceNow EmployeeWorks.

    Employees can open and complete the HR tasks assigned to them, like uploading documents, signing, working through a checklist or watching a video directly in EmployeeWorks. AI insights summarize each task in plain language, and ServiceNow Otto can read the task on screen to answer questions and help complete it where the task allows.

    This application delivers the foundational HR task experience in ServiceNow EmployeeWorks, helping organizations improve task completion, keep the employee experience consistent, and prepare HR tasks for AI-assisted work.
