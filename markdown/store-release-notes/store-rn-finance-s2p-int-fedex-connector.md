# S2P Integration FedEx Connector release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® S2P Integration FedEx Connector application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 2.0.2 - September 2026**

    FedEx Dataworks Intelligence for Source-to-Pay Operations brings critical insights on suppliers, including risk and performance data, into your workspace for sourcing, procurement, and supplier operations. Insights from FedEx Dataworks help procurement teams make better decisions and mitigate risks across their networks, leading to more resilient procurement operations.
