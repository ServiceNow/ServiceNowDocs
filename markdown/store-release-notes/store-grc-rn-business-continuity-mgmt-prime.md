# Business Continuity Management Prime release notes

Version history for the ServiceNow® Business Continuity Management Prime application on the ServiceNow Store.

**Important:** For details on system requirements and family compatibility, view the application listing on the [ServiceNow Store](https://store.servicenow.com/sn_appstore_store.do#!/store/home) website.

## Version history

-   **Version 1.0.3 - October 2026 \(Brazil\)**

    Business Continuity Management Prime is the licensing tier that provides access to the complete BCM feature set. It also provides out-of-the-box access to advanced AI features as well as the ability to build custom skills and agents.
