# View and track standalone contract requests

View the details of a contract request after it has been submitted and track the activities in the request.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_cm\_core.contract\_user

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Employee Center**.

2.  Select **My Requests** option on the header menu.

3.  Open your submitted contract request.

4.  View the details of the contract request.
