# View renewal details

View the renewal details in the contract repository record.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_cm\_core.contract\_fulfiller

## About this task

For a renewal request, once the contract document is finalized and all signatures are complete, a new Contract Repository record is created with the changes from the renewal. In this record, you can view:

-   Contract details
-   Contract Documents
-   Contract requests
-   Contract history

You can view the certificate of completion in the Attachment pane.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to your workspace.

2.  On the **Lists** tab, navigate to **Contract Requests** and open a contract.

3.  Navigate to **Contract repository** tab.

4.  Select the contract repository record.

5.  To view details, navigate to the **Details** tab.

6.  To view all the signed contract and the renewal requests associated with the contract repository record, navigate to **Contract requests** tab.

7.  To view all the contract document, navigate to **Contract documents**.

8.  To view renewal history, navigate to **Contract history**.

9.  To view certificate of completion for the contract and renewal request, navigate to **Attachments** pane.

    The certification of completion is available for electronic signature workflow only. For more information, see [Activate a system property to generate a certificate of completion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/employee-service-management/contract-management-pro/cncore-config-system-prop-COC.md)
