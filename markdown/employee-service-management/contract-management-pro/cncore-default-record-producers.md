# Default availability of out-of-the-box record producers

The out-of-the-box contract request record producers that are enabled by default differ for new customers.

## New customers

For new customers, the following out-of-the-box record producers are disabled by default:

-   Amendment request
-   Non-disclosure Agreement
-   Third-party Contract Review

The out-of-the-box contract request intake form is available by default for new customers who purchase CM Pro Prime, or CM Pro Prime with LSD Prime.
