# Review contract document

Review contract documents using AI-powered analysis or manual review to identify non-compliant terms, remediate them using clause library and validate before execution.

Contract review verifies that contract documents meet your organization's standards, include required clauses, and comply with legal and business requirements.

## Contract analysis using ServiceNow Otto for Contract Management Pro

Use AI-powered analysis to verify contract compliance against standard templates, identify missing required clauses, or perform initial screening before manual review.

For more information, see [Contract review using ServiceNow Otto for Contract Management Pro](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/employee-service-management/contract-management-pro/cncore-NA-review-land.md).

## Manual internal review

Manual internal review involves assigning contract documents to internal subject matter experts for detailed human review. Reviewers provide feedback, request additional information, and validate complex contract terms.
