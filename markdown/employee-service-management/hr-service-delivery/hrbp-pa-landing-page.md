# HRBP productivity assistant

The HRBP productivity assistant is an AI-powered assistant that helps HR business partners review and resolve assigned HR cases in a conversational interface. HR business partners can use this assistant to view open cases, review AI-generated summaries and recommendations, approve or decline requests, defer and resume cases, and schedule meetings.

</table>HR business partners receive HR cases from managers, employees, recruiters, and other parts of the organization. Many of those cases are routine, which leaves less time for strategic work.

The HRBP productivity assistant transforms case management into a conversational experience by enriching HR cases with rule-based urgency, AI-generated summaries, and resolution recommendations. Data access rules assign HR business partners to cases based on the employees they cover. A weekly digest email lists their open cases and links directly to the assistant.

Content that the assistant and the enrichment process generate can be inaccurate. HR business partners should review each summary, urgency, and recommendation before acting on a case.

The assistant runs in the EmployeeWorks Web App and reads and writes HR case records on your instance.
