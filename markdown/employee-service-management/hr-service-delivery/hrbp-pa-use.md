# Using the HRBP productivity assistant

As an HR business partner, use the HRBP productivity assistant in the EmployeeWorks Web App to review and act on your assigned HR cases and schedule case meetings. You can also ask about the employees you support and analyze workforce data.

## Overview of HRBP productivity assistant

A typical week starts with the weekly digest email. The digest lists the active HR cases assigned to you, with the most urgent cases first, and links to the HRBP productivity assistant. From there, use the conversational interface to work your cases in the following order:

1.  [Review and act on cases with the HRBP productivity assistant](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/employee-service-management/hr-service-delivery/review-hr-cases-pa.md)

    Ask the assistant for your open cases. Review the AI-generated summary and recommendation for a case, and then approve, deny, defer, resume, or cancel the case.

2.  [Work an HR case on the case page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/employee-service-management/hr-service-delivery/work-hr-case-using-case-page.md)

    To review more detail about a case, open the case page from the conversation. The page shows AI insights, case details, activity, and attachments, and you can act on the case from the same page.

3.  [Schedule a meeting for an HR case](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/employee-service-management/hr-service-delivery/schedule-meeting-using-pa.md)

    Ask the assistant to schedule a meeting with the people linked to a case. Review the meeting draft, and then send the invitation.


The assistant also answers questions that aren't tied to a single case. You can ask these questions at any point in a conversation.

-   [Ask about the employees you support](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/employee-service-management/hr-service-delivery/ask-about-employee-using-pa.md)

    Ask about an employee's details, work history, or reporting structure, and direct reports, or about new hires and employees on leave. You can also find the knowledge articles that an employee can view.

-   

    Request aggregated workforce data, such as HR case volumes, headcount, and learning or skills metrics. Review the results and AI-generated insights in the conversation.


## What the assistant knows about your cases

When you ask for your open cases, the assistant returns active HR cases that are assigned to you. When you ask for cases pending your approval, it returns active HR cases that are waiting for your approval. Each case carries an AI-generated urgency, summary, recommendation, and confidence score produced during the enrichment process. The assistant uses the AI urgency, not the **Priority** field in the HR Case record, to determine which cases are the most urgent.
