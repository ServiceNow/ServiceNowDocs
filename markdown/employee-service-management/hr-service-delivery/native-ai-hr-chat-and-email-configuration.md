# HR Chat and Email Configuration

Manage notification rules, email templates, scripting logic, and chat queue settings to enable consistent, automated, and real-time communication between HR agents and employees.

|Component|Description|
|---------|-----------|
|HR Chat Queue|Establishes a chat queue that enables real-time communication between HR agents and employees, with configurable assignment groups, wait times, escalation rules, and problem confirmation messaging for support interactions.|
|HR Notifications|Defines email notifications to determine who receives what content when HR process-related changes occur.|
|HR Email Templates|Provides customizable email templates used in HR notifications, including templates for case closure and general HR communications that contain dynamic content and styling for consistent email messaging.|
|HR Email Scripts|Contains scripts that execute business logic and generate dynamic content for HR email templates, enabling personalized and contextual email messages.|
