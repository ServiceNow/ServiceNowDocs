# HR Company

Define the organizational structure, locations, departments, positions, skills, and employee profiles.

|Component|Description|
|---------|-----------|
|Departments|Sets up organizational departments with unique identifiers, department heads, and primary contacts for use in user profiles and HR case management across the application.|
|Locations|Company office locations with address details, geographic coordinates \(latitude/longitude\), and contact information for use across the HR application and user profiles.|
|Positions|Defines job positions available in the organization and their associated departments, enabling consistent role assignment across user profiles and HR workflows.|
|HR Skills|Establishes specialized competencies \(such as Employee Career, Employee Onboarding, Employee Relations, and Employee Transfer\) that are assigned to user profiles to route specific HR cases to the appropriate groups or individuals based on expertise.|
|HR Profiles|Stores user profile information including employee number, department, position, and other user-specific data,|
