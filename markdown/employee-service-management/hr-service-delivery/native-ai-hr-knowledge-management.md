# Native AI HR Knowledge Management

Create and organize self-service knowledge content to support HR processes.

HR knowledge management allows the enterprise HR department to provide employees with a searchable knowledge base of HR policies, procedures, and other articles. For more information, see [HR Knowledge Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/employee-service-management/hr-service-delivery/hr-knowledge-management.md)

|Component|Description|
|---------|-----------|
|HR Knowledge Bases|Provides a guided setup process for configuring knowledge base repositories where HR articles are stored and organized.|
|HR Knowledge Articles|Stores individual HR knowledge articles with version control, metadata \(author, category, workflow status\), and structured content across multiple categories such as Benefits, Hiring, and Content Governance. Articles include titles, descriptions, version numbers, and workflow indicators to track publication status.|
