# Schedule a meeting for an HR case

As an HR business partner, schedule a meeting with the people linked to an HR case from the HRBP productivity assistant. The assistant presents the meeting as a draft and asks for your confirmation before it sends it.

## Before you begin

Role required: HR business partner \[sn\_hrbp\_hub.user\]

## Procedure

1.  Open the EmployeeWorks Web App.

2.  In the side navigation, under Assistants, select **HRBP Productivity Assistant**.

    If the assistant isn't listed, select **Browse assistants** and then select the **HRBP Productivity Assistant** card.

3.  In the message field, enter a request to schedule a meeting regarding the case.

    If you're scheduling a meeting for a case besides the one you're discussing in this conversation, include the HR case number. To search within a specific window, specify the day and time range. For example, type `Schedule a meeting for case HRC0001234 next Wednesday between 2 and 3 p.m.`

    The assistant displays the attendees that it proposes for the meeting.

4.  Review the attendees that the assistant proposes.

    The assistant proposes people linked to the case, such as the subject person, assigned to person, approvers, and collaborators. The list doesn't include you.

5.  Tell the assistant to add or remove a meeting attendee.

    For example, type `Remove Beth Anglin from the meeting`.

6.  Enter `Yes` to confirm the attendees.

    The assistant checks the attendee calendars and displays a meeting draft for a time when all attendees are available.

7.  If the assistant can't find a time when all attendees are available, specify an alternative timeframe.

    For example, type `Check Friday afternoon instead`.

8.  Review the meeting draft.

    The draft includes the title, date and time, attendees, description, and meeting location. The title includes the case number and the case short description.

    **Important:** Always review AI-generated content for accuracy.

9.  Tell the assistant to revise the meeting draft.
