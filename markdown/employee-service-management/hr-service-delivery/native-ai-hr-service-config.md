# Native AI HR Service Config

This module organizes HR services into a searchable topic taxonomy and defines the configuration options and service records that govern case creation and fulfillment behavior.

|Component|Description|
|---------|-----------|
|HR Topic Category|Sets up topic categories for classifying HR services \(such as Accommodations, Benefits, Payroll Administration, HR Operations\), each linked to a Class of Service \(COE\) table.|
|HR Topic Detail|Sets up granular topic details for classifying HR services \(such as Account Access, Background Check, Beneficiaries, Corporate Credit Card Request\) that sit beneath topic categories.|
|HR Service Options|Sets up service options that define case creation behavior for use in an HR service \(such as Add Manager to Watchlist or Hide Attachments from Subject Person\).|
|HR Service|Sets up HR service definitions, including links to Service Catalog record producers, templates, and fulfillment instructions.|
