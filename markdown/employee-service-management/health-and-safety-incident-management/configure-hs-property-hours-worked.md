# Configure property to set employee hours worked

Configure the property that sets the total number of employee hours worked for your organization in the past 12 months. These hours are for the entire organization and aren't broken down per establishment.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_ohs\_im.admin

## About this task

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Health and Safety** &gt; **Health and Safety administration** &gt; **Properties**.

2.  In the property **Total Hours Worked**, enter the total number of employee hours worked for your organization in the past 12 months.

    The default value is 2,500,000.

3.  Select **Save**.
