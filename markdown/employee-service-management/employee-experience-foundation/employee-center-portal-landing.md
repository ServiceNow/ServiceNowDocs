# Employee Center \(Pro\)

Employee Center is a multi-department portal for service delivery and employee engagement. Employee Center Pro brings you integrated communication and other services that extend the portal to other channels and service domains.

## Employee Center

\[Omitted image "ec-portal-homepage.png"\] Alt text: Employee Center portal homepage showing recommended items, popular topics, quick links, and task management widgets.

The Employee Center portal provides everyday, employee service delivery. You can scale service solutions across departments such as IT, HR, Workplace, Legal, and Procurement. Employees can report issues, request items or services, find service-related answers, and complete to-dos in one place.

Employee Center supports AI Search, chat through live agent, Virtual Agent, curated experiences such as taxonomies, dynamic topic pages, Advanced Portal Navigation, and task and request management.

You can add an AI assistant to the Employee Center portal. Moveworks for Employee Center, built on the Moveworks assistant, embeds a conversational assistant in Employee Center. Employees can get answers from the Knowledge Base articles, Service Catalog items, and complete actions such as checking a ticket status or update a request within a conversational interface.

For more information, see [Employee Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/employee-center-landing-page.md) and [Moveworks for Employee Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/moveworks-for-employeecenter.md).

## Employee Center Pro

\[Omitted image "ec-pro-portal-homepage.png"\] Alt text: Employee Center Pro portal homepage showing a featured banner, guided navigation, company news, and active items panel.

Employee Center Pro extends the standard Employee Center portal beyond service delivery with employee communication and engagement tools.

Employee Center Pro adds multi-channel content delivery, campaigns creation, employee communities, enterprise search, and portal analytics. Communicators author and schedule content, then measure engagement and refine the next message.

Employee Center Pro supports Moveworks assistant too. Moveworks for Employee Center, built on Moveworks, embeds a conversational assistant in Employee Center Pro, so employees resolve questions and complete actions in the same conversation. For more information, see [Moveworks for Employee Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/moveworks-for-employeecenter.md).

For more information, see [Employee Center Pro](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/employee-center-pro-landing.md) and [Moveworks for Employee Center](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/moveworks-for-employeecenter.md).

## Employee Center integrations

The following applications extend Employee Center and Employee Center Pro to other channels and service domains.

-   **[Employee Center for IT Service Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/itsm-ec-landing.md)**

    Improve self-service capabilities and perform admin and service management tasks from a dynamic unified employee portal.

-   **[Employee Center Integrations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/integrate-ec.md)**

    Access Employee Center capabilities beyond the portal to further enhance the employee experience.

-   **[Now Mobile app](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/now-mobile-employee-experience/mobile-employee-experience.md)**

    A downloadable app that employees use to submit requests and issues, find answers through global search and the Knowledge Base, and view and complete tasks.

-   **[ServiceNow Add-in for Microsoft 365](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/sn-addin-for-ms365.md)**

    Create an add-in with customized office controls and quick links so that employees submit requests directly from their applications.

-   **[ServiceNow for Microsoft Outlook](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/sn-ms-outlook-integration.md)**

    Employees get self-service without leaving the Outlook client, and agents resolve cases faster.

-   **[ServiceNow for Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/c_ServiceNowForMSTeams.md)**

    Employees get self-service in Microsoft Teams, and agents resolve cases faster.

-   **[ServiceNow for Zoom](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/employee-service-management/employee-experience-foundation/sn_zoomIntegration.md)**

    Employees get self-service without leaving the Zoom client, and agents resolve cases faster using chat, video, and telephony tools.
