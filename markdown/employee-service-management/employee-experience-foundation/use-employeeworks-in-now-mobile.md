# Using EmployeeWorks EmployeeWorks Web App in Now Mobile

Connect Now Mobile® to your instance to use EmployeeWorks on your phone.

## Before you begin

Role required: Now Mobile® user

## Procedure

1.  Open Now Mobile® app.

2.  Sign in to your instance.

3.  On the home page, start using EmployeeWorks to perform your tasks and requests.

4.  To switch back to your classic experience, go to **Setting** and change the **Experience** option.


## Result

EmployeeWorks is available in Now Mobile® on your phone.
