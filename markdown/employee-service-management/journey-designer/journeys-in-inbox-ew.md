# Find journeys and lifecycle events in your Inbox in Employee Works

View your active journeys and standalone lifecycle event cases in the My Requests section of the Employee Works Inbox.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_jny.manager

## About this task

My Requests, in the Employee Works Inbox, shows active journeys and eligible standalone lifecycle event cases associated with you. As an employee, this means journeys where you're the subject person; as a manager, journeys where you're the journey owner or opened-for user. A lifecycle event case that's already associated with a journey isn't shown separately, to avoid duplicate entries.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to Employee Works Inbox and go to **My Requests**.

    Journey and lifecycle event entries follow the same visual pattern as other request cards in My Requests, including fields such as last updated date and state, and are identified as a Journey. A journey entry displays as `JNYxxxx - Journey name`.

2.  Select a journey or lifecycle event entry to open the corresponding Journey Details page.


## Result

Journey and lifecycle event entries in My Requests follow the existing filtering and sorting behavior of that page. Tasks assigned to you continue to be surfaced separately in the To-Dos section of the Inbox.
