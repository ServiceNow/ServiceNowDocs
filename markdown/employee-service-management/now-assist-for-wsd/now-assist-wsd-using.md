# Using ServiceNow Otto for WSD

Using ServiceNow Otto for Workplace Service Delivery \(WSD\) for creating a reservation.

## Overview of ServiceNow Otto for WSD

ServiceNow Otto for Workplace Service Delivery \(WSD\) enhances your ServiceNow® Workplace Services experience with generative AI capabilities. Enhance the employee experience by using a generative experience to interact with your physical workspace.

It allows employees the following:

-   Reserving a space for meeting
-   Adding co-workers to a reservation
-   Adding visitors or guests to a reservation
-   Adding a Zoom meeting link for remote joining
-   Ordering and adding catering services
