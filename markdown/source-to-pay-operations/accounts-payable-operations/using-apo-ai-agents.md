# Use AI agents in ServiceNow Otto for Accounts Payable Operations \(APO\)

Use AI agents to resolve invoice inquiries and recommend business owners for Non-PO invoices and credit memos.

|Available AI agents|Description|
|-------------------|-----------|
|Inquiry resolution provider|Automates responses to high-volume, repetitive invoice inquiries that were raised by suppliers. It also reduces the workload of the AP agents.|
|Recommend invoice owner|When a Missing or invalid business owner exception occurs, the AI agent identifies the missing business owner through semantic matching or by creating tasks.|

The AI agents run autonomously. For more information about the AI agents, see [AI Agent Studio](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/intelligent-experiences/na-ai-agents.md).

**Important:** By default, all agentic workflows and AI agent records are read-only.

There might be AI agents installed on your instance that are not used in agentic workflows. To learn how to see all agents that are available to you, see [Find AI agents](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/intelligent-experiences/find-ai-agents.md).
