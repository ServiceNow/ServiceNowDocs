# Review purchase order confirmations

Review the draft purchase order confirmation generated by the agentic workflow.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_slm.contact

## Procedure

1.  Open the draft confirmation email that you have received

2.  Select **Review draft confirmation** in the email to view the purchase order confirmation in Supplier Collaboration Portal.

3.  To finalize a draft confirmation, select one of these options in the email or the purchase order confirmation in Supplier Collaboration Portal:

    -   **Submit** — Sends the purchase order confirmation to the buyer.
    -   **Retract** — Discards the draft confirmation.
    By default, draft confirmations auto-submit after 24 hours if no action is taken.
