# View AI L1 SLO Service Desk Specialist progress

View the step-by-step progress made by AI L1 SLO Service Desk Specialist on a case record in the contextual side panel.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_slm.fulfiller

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Supplier Lifecycle Operations** &gt; **Source-to-Pay Workspace**.

2.  Select the list icon.

3.  Open a general inquiry case that is assigned to AI L1 SLO Service Desk Specialist.

4.  In the contextual side panel, select the Agentic Processes icon.

5.  Select **Zero Touch Service Desk Agent** to view the details of the agent's work.

6.  Select **Show steps** to view the agent's progress.

7.  In the **Review output** section, view the generated response.
