# Using ServiceNow Otto for Finance and Procurement

As a fulfiller, you can use the AI skills for FSC Common to quickly summarize purchase orders in the S2P workspace using the Purchase order summarization for fulfillers skill.

## What ServiceNow Otto for Finance and Procurement can do for you

ServiceNow Otto for Finance and Procurement works with your purchase order and related procurement data to:

-   **Summarize purchase orders**

    Get a quick overview of a purchase order's status, completed actions, and next steps by using the Purchase order summarization for fulfillers skill. If the purchase order has associated email records, the summary's next steps also factor in relevant email context, such as the sender, date, and key message.

    For information on how to summarize a purchase order, see [Summarize a procurement case](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/source-to-pay-operations/now-assist-for-fsc-common/now-assist-fsc-summarize-po.md).


-   **[Summarize a procurement case](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/source-to-pay-operations/now-assist-for-fsc-common/now-assist-fsc-summarize-po.md)**  
Use the Procurement case summarization for fulfillers skill to summarize procurement cases. Summaries include status, progress, and required actions to keep fulfillers informed.
-   **[Categorize inquiry cases](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/source-to-pay-operations/now-assist-for-fsc-common/categorize-inquiry-cases.md)**  
Categorize invoice inquiry cases into sub-categories, based on case descriptions, using the Inquiry Classifier skill in ServiceNow Otto to route cases accurately and improve resolution speed.
