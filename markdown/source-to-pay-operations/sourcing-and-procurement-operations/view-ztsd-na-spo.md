# View AI L1 SPO Service Desk Specialist progress

Track the steps the AI L1 SPO Service Desk Specialist performs when processing a procurement case.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_shop.procurement\_specialist

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Sourcing and Procurement Operations** &gt; **Source-to-Pay Workspace**.

2.  Select the list icon.

3.  Open a general inquiry case assigned to the AI L1 SPO Service Desk Specialist.

4.  In the contextual side panel, select the Agentic Processes \( \[Omitted image "ztsd-agentic-icon-apo.png"\] Alt text:\) icon.

5.  Select **Zero Touch Service Desk Agent** to view the agent's activity details.

6.  Select **Show steps** to view the steps the agent performed to investigate the request.

7.  In the **Review output** section, review the generated response.
