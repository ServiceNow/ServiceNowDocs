# Exploring the procurement case management implementation

Sourcing and Procurement Operations \(SPO\) provides a guided admin implementation experience and a unified experience for procurement specialists and requesters.

## SPO users

|User|Description|
|----|-----------|
|Administrator|Installs SPO from the Product Hub and configures it through the SPO Configuration Console.|
|Procurement specialist|Manages and resolves procurement cases in the Source-to-Pay Workspace, guided by playbooks and supported by ServiceNow Otto AI skills.|
|Employee or requester|Submits procurement requests through intake forms on the employee center, and tracks case progress using stepper statuses.|
