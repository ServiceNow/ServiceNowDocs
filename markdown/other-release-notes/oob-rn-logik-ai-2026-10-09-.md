## Release notes

This release version includes the following new enhancements and fixes.

## Quote Experience

Enable Quote Experience to create quotes with split lines for Contract and Sold Product amendments to manage subscriptions.

Reconfigurations now save back to Quote Experience and sync to Order Management without breaking the sold-product link.

Support for simple products with product characteristics in Quote Experience configurations.

Specify the number of copies to create when copying a line, allowing runtime users to quickly generate multiple identical lines without repeating the copy action.
