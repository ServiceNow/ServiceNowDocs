# Create a stakeholder form

The create a stakeholder form captures information about individuals or teams with an interest in enterprise architecture outcomes.

|Field|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|Name|Unique name of the stakeholder. Example: Chief Financial Officer or Retail Customer.|
|Stakeholder type|Classification of the stakeholder. Examples: Internal, External, Customer, Supplier, or Regulator.|
|Group|Organizational group associated with the stakeholder.|
|Department|Department associated with the stakeholder.|
|Interest level|Rating of the stakeholder's interest in the organization or a specific service or project. Examples: 1-Strong Resistance through 5-Strong Willingness.|
|Influence level|Rating of the stakeholder's ability to impact the organization or a specific project. Examples: High, Medium, or Low.|
|Description|Explanation of the stakeholder's interests, influence, and relationship to the organization.|
