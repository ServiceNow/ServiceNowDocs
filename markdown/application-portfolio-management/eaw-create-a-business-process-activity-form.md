# Business process activity form

Fields for creating and configuring business process activities that represent discrete units of work in your organization.

|Field|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|Name|Unique name of the business process activity. Example: Approve Purchase Order.|
|Description|Brief explanation of the work performed by the activity.|
|Activity Type|Classification of the activity. Example: Manual Task.|
|Owned by|User who owns the business process activity.|
|Managed by group|Group that manages the business process activity.|
