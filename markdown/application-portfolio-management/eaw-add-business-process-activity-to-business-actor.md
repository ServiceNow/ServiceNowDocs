# Add a business process activity to a business actor

Associate existing business process activities with a business actor, or create activities directly from the business actor record.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_apm.apm\_user

## About this task

Associating business process activities with business actors documents which roles, departments, or individuals are responsible for specific processes in your enterprise architecture.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **Enterprise Architecture Workspace**.

2.  Open the Portfolio List view by selecting the Portfolio icon \(\[Omitted image "portfolio-icon.png"\] Alt text:\).

3.  Select the expand row icon \(\[Omitted image "ExpandIcon.png"\] Alt text:\) next to **Business Architecture**.

4.  Select **Business Actors**.

5.  Select the business actor that you want to add a business process activity to.

6.  Select the **Business process activities** tab.

7.  Add a business process activity using one of the following methods.

    -   To add an existing business process activity:
        1.  Select **Add**.
        2.  In the Add business process activity window, select the business process activity and the relationship type between the business actor and the business process activity.
        3.  Select **Add**.
    -   To create a business process activity, select **New** and complete the fields on the form. For field descriptions, see [Create a business process activity form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/application-portfolio-management/eaw-create-a-business-process-activity-form.md).

## Result

The business process activity appears in the **Business process activities** related list for the business actor.
