# Add a business process to a stakeholder

Add existing business processes to a stakeholder in the Enterprise Architecture Workspace, or create a business process directly from the stakeholder record.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_apm.apm\_user

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **Enterprise Architecture Workspace**.

2.  Open the Portfolio List view by selecting the Portfolio icon \(\[Omitted image "portfolio-icon.png"\] Alt text:\).

3.  Select the expand row icon \(\[Omitted image "ExpandIcon.png"\] Alt text:\) next to **Business Architecture**.

4.  Select **Stakeholders**.

5.  Select the stakeholder that you want to add a business process to.

6.  Select the **Business processes** tab.

7.  Add a business process.

    -   To add an existing business process:
        1.  Select **Add**.
        2.  In the Add business process window, select the business process and the relationship type between the business process and the stakeholder.
        3.  Select **Add**.
    -   To create a business process, select **New** and complete the fields on the form. For field information, see [Create business process form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/application-portfolio-management/eaw-business-process-form.md).

## Result

The business process appears in the **Business processes** related list for the stakeholder.
