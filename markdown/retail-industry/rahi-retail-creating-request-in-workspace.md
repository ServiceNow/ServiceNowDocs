# Create and assign a retail customer complaint case in CRM Workspace

Create and assign a retail customer complain case in CRM Workspace on behalf of a customer.

## Before you begin

Roles required: sn\_rtl\_cs\_cmplnt.agent, sn\_rtl\_cs\_cmplnt.agent\_manager

## About this task

The retail customer complaint case type enables customers to submit complaints anonymously via phone or email.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Lists** &gt; **Retail Customer Complaints** &gt; **All**.

2.  Select **New** and fill in the information about the customer complaint.
