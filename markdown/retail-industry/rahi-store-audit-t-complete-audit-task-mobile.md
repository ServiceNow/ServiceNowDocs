# Complete an Audit Task from Retail Mobile

As a Location Auditor, open an Audit Task in Retail Mobile, complete its questionnaire, and close the task.

## Before you begin

Role required: `sn_rtl_store_audit.location_auditor`

## About this task

Audit Tasks don't have their own work list in Retail Mobile—you drill into them from the **Audit tasks** tab on the parent Store Audit Case.

## Procedure

1.  In Retail Mobile, go to the **My Store** page, open the parent Store Audit Case, then select the **Audit tasks** tab to find the Audit Task.

2.  Open the Audit Task.

    If it's unassigned, tap **Assign to me**, or tap **Assign task** to assign it to another Location Auditor.

3.  Tap **Take questionnaire**, complete it, and submit.

    Tap **View questionnaire** instead to review a completed questionnaire. The **Close task** button stays hidden until every mandatory questionnaire on the task is complete.

4.  Add any observations in the activity stream.

5.  Tap **Close task** and confirm in the popup.


## Result

The Audit Task is closed, with the questionnaire result and your observations recorded. Once every Audit Task on a Store Audit Case is closed, close the parent case to complete the store audit.
