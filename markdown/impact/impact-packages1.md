# Impact packages

ServiceNow® Impact is available in core packages, Guided and Total, with optional add-on packages to extend your entitlements.

## Impact core packages

Impact includes Platform Health, Instance Observer, Value Management, Product Adoption, and access to the Accelerator &amp; Initiative catalog.

|Package|What you get|
|-------|------------|
|Guided|The Impact App, 24/7 support, limited concurrence Accelerator catalog access, and guidance from a Customer Success Manager|
|Total|Everything in Impact Guided, enhanced response times for Priority 1 and Priority 2 tickets, Developer Support, expanded access to the Accelerator catalog, and a dedicated Impact Squad|

## Integrated Success

An Integrated Success package is available that includes all Impact entitlements related to the Impact Total package with the addition of implementation services engagements.

Experts included in your implementation services engagements vary based on your specific agreement. The Implementation Service is initiated through the Free Form initiative in Impact. See [Request Accelerators and Initiatives](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/impact/accelerator-and-initiative-list/accelerator-request.md) for information.

## Entitlements

The entitlements available for Impact features, Accelerator catalog concurrency, Squad, Support, and Add-ons vary between the Impact packages.

## Impact Squad

Impact Squad is a team of dedicated professionals who help you manage your Impact platform based on your Impact package entitlement.

|Squad members|Package entitlement|
|-------------|-------------------|
|Customer Success Manager, Customer Success Executive, Platform Architect, Support Account Manager|Total|
|Customer Success Manager|Guided|

**Note:** See [Work with experts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/impact/impact-squad.md) for details for each Impact Squad member.

## Platform Health capability per package

The Impact packages include certain threshold availability for Platform Health. The following table lists the differences in capabilities per package:

|Capability|Guided|Total|
|----------|------|-----|
|Scan Engine active custom definitions|Up to 10|Unlimited|
|Scan Engine proposed fix|Not included|Included|
|Instance Observer seats|5|10|
|Instance Observer data look back period|45 days|6 months|
|Instance Observer alerts|6 alert types|40 alert types|
|Instance Observer analytics|Not Included|Included|

**Note:** Check your entitlements to determine whether you have access to Now Assist features in the Impact Store Application.

## Accelerator concurrency

Accelerators are scoped engagements with a ServiceNow expert available in various Accelerator sub-catalogs, Technical, Strategy, Architecture, and Optimization. The sub-catalogs available and the number of Accelerators you can run concurrently vary by Impact package. See [Accelerators and Initiatives](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/impact/accelerator-and-initiative-list/impact-initiatives.md) for details.
