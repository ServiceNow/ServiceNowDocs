# Impact Delivery Instance reference

The Impact Delivery Instance is the service provider for the Impact Store Application.

The Impact Delivery Instance serves as follows:

-   Your Impact squad interactive space.
-   Existing data from the Impact IDI is migrated at setup to the Impact Store Application to ensure that critical information is integrated within your ServiceNow instance.

## Impact Delivery Instance migration

Download Impact from the ServiceNow store to take advantage of the latest features directly in your instance- including new Platform Health tools! Refer to [Install Impact](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/impact/install-impact-innovation-lab.md) for installation steps.

**Note:** The Impact Delivery Instance, \[impact.servicenow.com\] won't include new features.

## Sync to the Impact Store Application

Use Guided Setup to connect your data from the Impact Delivery Instance. See [Run Impact Guided Setup](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/impact/guided-setup-impact-in-app.md) for details on the configuration.
