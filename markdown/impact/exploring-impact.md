# Exploring Impact

ServiceNow Impact is a premium customer success product that combines AI-powered software tools with hands-on expertise that helps your organizations maximize the value of your ServiceNow investment.

ServiceNow Impact is an all-in-one offering for accelerating platform value through proactive guidance, embedded tooling, and dedicated expert support.

Impact provides the support and expertise to track progress against your outcomes, share insights and industry benchmark comparisons, and make data-driven decisions to optimize your digital transformation journey.

At its core, Impact helps you excel in the following:

-   Plan implementation
-   Track product adoption
-   Maintain platform health
-   Resolve platform issues

See the Impact overview video to learn more.
