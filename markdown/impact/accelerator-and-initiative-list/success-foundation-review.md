# Success Foundation Review \(SFR\)

This accelerator establishes a baseline of your organizational capability across eight dimensions, run at the start of your Impact journey and revisited annually.

## Accelerator Overview

The Success Foundation Review \(SFR\) is a process that helps identify organizational gaps and which focus areas, if addressed, will unlock the greatest value for your ServiceNow investment.

The SFR consists of a series of stakeholder interviews that gather both quantitative and qualitative feedback. The results establish a measurable baseline that the Impact Squad uses to identify gaps, prioritize recommendations, and guide future improvement efforts. This clarity enables both teams to align early priorities and avoid diffusing effort across lower-impact activities.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact  Guided+ \(Strategic Value or Platform Governance\), Advanced, and Total packages

## Prerequisites

Customers must complete this accelerator early in their Impact journey, as the results establish a baseline for future roadmap planning and ongoing adoption efforts.

## What You Get

-   **Introductory Customer Session \(up to 1 hour\)**
    -   Provide an overview of the SFR and its importance
    -   Review the SFR process and deliverable
    -   Communicate expectation for customer participation
    -   Request data and any other inputs
-   **Discovery Customer Sessions \(up to 1.5 hours per interview\)**

    The SFR interviews help:

    -   Uncover disconnects and misalignment across multiple focus areas
    -   Identify existing gaps that impact long-term success
    -   Locate areas to improve capacity and competency
-   **Read-out Session \(up to 1.5 hours\)**

    This session helps:

    -   Present the results to stakeholders summarizing the SFR findings
    -   Discuss recommendations and next steps based on findings

## Outputs

Executive readout deck with documented recommendations from Impact Squad based on SFR results.
