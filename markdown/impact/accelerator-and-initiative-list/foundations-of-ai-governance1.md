# Foundations of AI Governance

Introduces customers to AI governance fundamentals and the ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS framework, providing the knowledge and resources needed to begin their AI governance journey.

## Accelerator Overview

Foundations of AI Governance \(FoAIG\) is grounded in ISO/IEC 42001 \(the international standard for AI Management Systems\). This accelerator helps organizations begin the journey from AI Curiosity to AI Maturity. With focused set of sessions and latest tools, this accelerator assesses your current AI Maturity and builds shared governance literacy across your team. It also guides you through the Pilot Canvas: a practical, one-page framework that verifies governance is embedded into the design of new AI Systems from day one.

Whether your organization is navigating internal AI policy, preparing for regulatory scrutiny, or aligning stakeholders before your next AI initiative, this accelerator can help. It gives your team a common language, clear maturity baseline, and a governance-ready blueprint for your first or next AI Pilot.

## What You Get

-   **AI Governance Maturity Assessment**

    Use ServiceNow self-paced tool to determine your current state of AI Governance.

-   **Session Preparation**

    Your Impact Squad:

    -   Reviews maturity assessment findings
    -   Confirms custom versus sample use cases and objectives
    -   Completes other preparation activities
-   **Introductory Customer Session \(1.5 hours\)**

    Session includes:

    -   Diagnose
        -   Scorecard vs ISO standard
        -   Role alignment
        -   Confirm target maturity
    -   Prescribe
        -   Vocabulary review
        -   Plan → Do → Check → Act
        -   Pilot Canvas orientation
        -   Use case confirmation
-   **Customer Coaching Session \(up to 1 hour\)**

    Session includes:

    Execute:

    -   Review use cases and objectives
    -   Pilot Canvas activity
    -   Approve pilot\(s\) and assign ownership
-   **Follow-Up Customer Session \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**
    -   Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to Foundations of AI Governance
    -   Provide additional guidance on leading practices
