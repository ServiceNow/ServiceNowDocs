# Jumpstart Your IRM: Entity Framework

This accelerator provides an overview of the Entity Framework and its role as the foundation of any IRM implementation.

## Accelerator Overview

Jumpstart Your IRM: Entity Framework provides Impact customers with an overview and demonstration of the Entity Framework and its role as the foundation of an IRM implementation. The session covers entity types, classes, tiers, and hierarchy, along with leading practices for entity design, data sourcing, and common pitfalls to avoid prior to implementation.

Customers leave with the conceptual clarity needed to make sound entity design decisions from day one, and key resources and leading practice guides.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

Customer must have an active license for at least one of the following: Integrated Risk Management, Third-Party Risk Management, Privacy Management, or Digital Operational Resilience Management.

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance \(if applicable\)
    -   Activates and configures related plugins
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1.5 hours\)**

    Includes the following:

    -   Overview of the Entity Framework:
        -   Entity types, classes, tiers, and hierarchy
        -   Entity design decisions
        -   Data sourcing approaches
        -   Common pre-implementation pitfalls
    -   Applied demonstration, including:
        -   Entity types, classes, hierarchy, and filters
        -   How entity design decisions drive automated risk and control creation
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional upon Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to IRM as it relates to Entity Framework.


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
