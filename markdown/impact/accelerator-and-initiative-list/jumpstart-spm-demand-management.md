# Jumpstart Your Strategic Portfolio Management – Demand Management

This accelerator provides an overview and demonstration of Demand Management within SPM and its role in forecasting, analyzing, and prioritizing business needs to match resources.

## Accelerator Overview

**Jumpstart Your SPM – Demand Management** provides Impact customers with an overview of Demand Management connecting strategy with capacity and managing the demand pipeline. This verifies organizations meet customer and operational demands without disruption. This offering provides key resources and leading practices to get started with Demand Management within SPM.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

-   Foundational data established
-   Portfolio Planning, Strategic Planning, or Collaborative Work Management already deployed

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance
    -   Activates and configures Demand Management components
    -   Loads demo data where required
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1.5 hours\)**

    Includes the following:

    -   Overview of the Demand Management process flow and workbench
    -   Demonstration of *ServiceNow Otto* for Demand Management.
    -   Walkthrough of Demand Management setup and how it fits into the SPM landscape
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to Demand Management within SPM.


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
