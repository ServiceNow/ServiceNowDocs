# ITSM Maturity Assessment – On Demand

A structured, self-service offering to assess your current IT Service Management \(ITSM\) processes and functional maturity within ServiceNow.

## Accelerator Overview

ITSM Maturity Assessment – On Demand provides Impact customers with a structured, self-paced engagement to assess their ITSM maturity, understand current strengths and gaps, and identify priority areas for improvement. The assessment generates a practical action plan aligned to target maturity levels and evaluates the following ITSM domains:

-   Incident Management
-   Problem Management
-   Request Management
-   Change Management
-   Knowledge Management
-   Continual Improvement Management
-   Service Desk
-   CSDM Foundations

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## What You Get

-   **Orientation**

    Review introductory guidance that explains the purpose of the accelerator, the maturity methodology, what to expect, and how to complete the experience successfully.

-   **Set Up Assessment**

    The main user starts the accelerator, adds participants to complete the survey, and confirms the assessment scope. It helps the right stakeholders provide input across the relevant maturity domains.

-   **Assess Current State**

    Complete a guided maturity assessment across the in-scope domains. Participants select the response statement that best reflects the organization's current practice for each question.

-   **Review Results**

    View a read-only results summary that converts responses into domain-level maturity scores, maturity labels, heat maps, and statement-level detail so you can understand where your organization stands today.

-   **Prioritize and Confirm Focus Areas**

    Select the domains you want to strengthen and set a target maturity level for each one. Your priorities can reflect business risk, readiness, upcoming initiatives, regulatory exposure, or executive focus.

-   **Build Action Plan**

    Generate a structured action plan for each selected domain. Each plan includes recommended actions, suggested owners, and clear completion criteria based on the gaps between your current and chosen target maturity level.

-   **Output**

    An action plan that summarizes your assessment results, domain maturity scores, prioritized focus areas, and selected target maturity levels. The plan includes domain-specific action plans with tasks, suggested owners, and "Done when" completion criteria.
