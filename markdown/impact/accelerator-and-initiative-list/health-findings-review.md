# Health Findings Review

This accelerator provides expert guidance to help customers interpret Scan Engine findings, prioritize platform health findings, and take confident action.

## Accelerator Overview

**Health Findings Review** helps Impact customers interpret Scan Engine platform health findings with expert guidance so they can confidently prioritize and act on key issues. By identifying the most relevant findings for their setup and team capacity, customers can focus their remediation efforts where they matter most and reduce time spent interpreting results. It also provides resources and leading practices to help customers remediate their findings.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

-   Impact store app installed on the customer's instance
-   At least one scan completed with findings available for review
-   Customer has reviewed findings and prepared specific questions prior to the session

**Note:** If the Impact Store App is not installed, customers should instead complete Jumpstart Your Impact Platform Health.

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance
    -   If the customer has a Platform Architect \(PA\), collaborates with them to review the findings and customer questions
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1 hour\)**

    Includes the following:

    -   Explanation of findings in context:
        -   Review why a finding was triggered
        -   Review its impact on the customer's environment
    -   Translation of technical findings into business-relevant insights:

        Connect technical findings with business impact to support stakeholders and communication.


## Outputs

-   Customers with an assigned Platform Architect \(PA\) are encouraged to reach out to them for additional guidance and follow-up questions
-   Key resources and leading practice guides, if applicable

**Note:** The temporary instance will be scheduled for retirement following the session.
