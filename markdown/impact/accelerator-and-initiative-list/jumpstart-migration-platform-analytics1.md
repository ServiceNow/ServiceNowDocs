# Jumpstart Your Migration of Platform Analytics

Demonstration of how to successfully prepare and migrate your Platform Analytics content.

## Accelerator Overview

**Jumpstart Your Migration of Platform Analytics** provides Impact customers with an overview of the changes customers must make to migrate their Platform Analytics content. We take a copy of the customer's instance and walk through the migration process, highlighting common issues, typical customer questions, and recommended next steps. We also provide advice and direction on how to prepare themselves for the change.

**Important:** The selected instance must have Next Experience activated.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

-   Customer is using Performance Analytics dashboards \(not Core UI\)
-   Customer is subscribed to Platform Analytics
-   Completion of Jumpstart Your Platform Analytics is recommended

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance
    -   Reviews the customer's Platform Analytics implementation and migration readiness
    -   Runs Platform Analytics HealthScan \(if applicable\)
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 90 minutes\)**
    -   Overview of readiness activities \(organization and platform\) and migration steps
    -   Review updates for:
        -   Core ServiceNow content
        -   Migration process based on customer's install base
    -   Customer migration process walk-through
    -   Discuss recommendations based on the initial review
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to the Platform Analytics migration process


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
