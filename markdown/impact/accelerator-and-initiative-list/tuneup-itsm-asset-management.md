# TuneUp Your ITSM Asset Management

This accelerator provides prescriptive guidance to improve your ITSM Asset Management health.

## Accelerator Overview

**TuneUp Your ITSM Asset Management** provides Impact customers with an analysis of common Asset Management problem areas through HealthScan and recommendations on addressing those findings. It aims to help customers understand how to identify problem areas within their Asset Management setup, reviews the top challenging areas, and provides leading remediation practices.

**Note:** This accelerator is focused on the Asset Management functionality available in the ITSM product.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This feature is available in Impact Guided, Advanced, and Total packages.

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation &amp; Assessment**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance
    -   Runs ITSM Asset Management HealthScan portfolio
    -   Analyzes HealthScan findings
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1 hour\)**

    Review of:

    -   IT Asset Management key concepts
    -   ITSM Asset Management health findings
    -   Prioritized problem or deficient areas \(up to 3\)
    -   Recommended remediation actions and leading practices
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 60 minutes\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to ITSM Asset Management


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
-   HealthScan findings
-   Key resources and guides
