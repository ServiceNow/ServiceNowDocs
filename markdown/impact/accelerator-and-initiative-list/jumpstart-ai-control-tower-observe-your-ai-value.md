# Jumpstart Your AI Control Tower: Observe Your AI Value

This accelerator provides coaching on how to create value templates, measure and monitor the business impact of AI investments using AI Control Tower.

## Accelerator Overview

Jumpstart Your AI Control Tower: Observe Your AI Value provides Impact customers with guided coaching to help them measure and communicate the business impact of their AI investments using AI Control Tower.

Through applied demonstrations, customers learn how to configure value templates to define meaningful KPIs, map AI initiative outcomes to strategic priorities, and build executive-ready reporting. This enables a shift from anecdotal impact claims to a repeatable, data-driven value measurement practice. This offering also includes key resources and leading practice guides to help customers get started.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

-   Customer has completed Jumpstart Your AI Control Tower: Foundations \(Required\) and Jumpstart Your AI Control Tower: AI Lifecycle \(Preferred\)
-   Customer has an active AI Control Tower instance \(latest version preferred\)
-   AI Governance Core Plugin is active
-   At least one AI asset registered and marked as managed

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance \(if applicable\)
    -   Activates and configures related plugins
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1.5 hours\)**

    Includes the following:

    -   Walkthrough of AI Control Tower value template configuration and creation
    -   Review of how to connect AI initiative outcomes to strategic priorities
    -   Guidance on reporting and stakeholder-ready value storytelling using AI Control Tower data
    -   Hand-off documentation and enablement session for customer self-sufficiency
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to AI Control Tower.


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
