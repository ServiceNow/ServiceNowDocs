# Jumpstart Your CSDM for Enterprise Architecture

This accelerator provides Impact customers with structured coaching on the CSDM data model as it applies to ServiceNow's Enterprise Architecture application.

## Accelerator Overview

**Jumpstart Your CSDM for Enterprise Architecture** guides Impact customers through the CSDM data model as it applies to ServiceNow's Enterprise Architecture application. It provides structured coaching on how Business Capabilities, Business Applications, and Information Objects connect to deployed Application Services and Service Instances.

With leading practices and an applied demonstration, customers gain the confidence and clarity to populate and govern their EA data correctly from the outset. This ensures their platform accurately tracks how their business and technology work together.

## Prerequisites

-   It’s recommended that [Jumpstart Your CSDM: Foundation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/impact/accelerator-and-initiative-list/jumpstart-csdm.md) be completed before starting this accelerator
-   CMDB must be in place with at least a foundational level of data populated

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a ServiceNow demonstration instance and a customer temporary instance
    -   Activates and configures related plugins
    -   Prepares demonstration of the data population of each CSDM related table
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1.5 hours\)**

    Includes the following:

    -   High-level overview of the ServiceNow platform data model
    -   Information about CSDM standards around data population for Enterprise Architecture, including personas who use the data
    -   Demonstration of:
        -   Data population
        -   Data usage
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to CSDM as it relates to Enterprise Architecture.


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
