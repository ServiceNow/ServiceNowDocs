# Jumpstart Your Data Encryption

This accelerator provides an overview and demonstration of the possibilities and capabilities of ServiceNow's Field Encryption Enterprise \(FEE\).

## Accelerator Overview

Jumpstart Your Data Encryption provides Impact customers with an overview and demonstration of Data Encryption. It enables platform owners and security administrators to confidently activate Field Encryption Enterprise \(FEE\) as a core capability for protecting sensitive data at the application level. Through coaching the customers learn how to identify encryption candidates and understand operational impacts. They also safely progress from entitlement to production-ready use of encryption capabilities as part of their Vault security strategy. This offering also includes key resources and leading practice guides to help customers get started.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

-   Customer has a ServiceNow Vault subscription with FEE entitlement
-   Customer's ServiceNow instance is running the Zurich release or later

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance \(if applicable\)
    -   Activates and configures related plugins
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1.5 hours\)**

    Includes the following:

    -   Overview of Platform Encryption and Features:
        -   Cloud Encryption
        -   Key Management Framework
        -   FEE
    -   Demonstration of Field Encryption
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to Data Encryption.


## Outputs

-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
