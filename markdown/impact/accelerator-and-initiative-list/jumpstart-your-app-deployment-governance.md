# Jumpstart Your App Deployment Governance

This accelerator guides platform administrators and release managers through the setup and configuration of App Engine Management Center and ReleaseOps.

## Accelerator Overview

**Jumpstart Your App Deployment Governance** provides Impact customers with a structured approach to managing application deployments using App Engine Management Center \(AEMC\) and ReleaseOps. The offering includes an applied demonstration of governance configuration, release pipeline design, and validation gates, including Automated Test Framework \(ATF\), Instance Scan, and App Readiness Score. Customers also receive a guided tour of cross-role coordination workflows and leading practices for establishing deployable, auditable application release processes.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## Prerequisites

Customer is currently deploying or planning to deploy custom applications.

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant:

    -   Provisions a temporary instance
    -   Activates and configures App Engine Management Center and related tools
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1.5 hours\)**
    -   Overview of:
        -   App deployment governance
        -   App Engine Management Center governance
    -   Demonstration of App Engine Management Center
    -   Leading practice recommendations for implementing app deployment governance
    -   Key resources and guides
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(Optional up on Customer request – up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to App Engine Management Center


## Outputs

-   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
-   30-day access to the temporary instance and Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
