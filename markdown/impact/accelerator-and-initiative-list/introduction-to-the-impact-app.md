# Introduction to Impact App

This accelerator provides Impact customers with an introduction to the Impact App, highlighting the purpose and business value of each Impact module.

## Accelerator Overview

Introduction to the Impact App introduces Impact customers to the Impact App and shows how value measurement, product adoption, and consumption come together in a continuous workflow. Participants use Value Management, the Capabilities Map, Product Adoption Roadmaps, and the Consumption Report. They learn how to move from understanding the value they are realizing to identifying the actions they can take next. All of this happens within the platform they already use. This offering also provides key resources and leading-practice guides to help customers put these insights into action and continue their journey after the session.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided, Guided+ \(SV or PG\), Advanced, and Total packages.

## What You Get

-   **Session Preparation**

    The Technical Accelerator Consultant provisions a ServiceNow demonstration instance.

-   **Customer Coaching Session \#1 \(up to 1 hour\)**

    Includes overview and walkthrough of the Impact App:

    -   Value Management
    -   Product Adoption
    -   Accelerators and Initiatives
    -   Consumption Report
-   **Customer Coaching Session \#2 \(optional up on customer request-up to 1 hour\)**

    Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to the Impact App

-   **Outputs**
    -   30-day access to the Technical Accelerator Consultant with whom you met for any further questions or in-depth explorations
    -   Copy of the Coaching Session Deck
