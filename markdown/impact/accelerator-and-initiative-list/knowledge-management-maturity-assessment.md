# Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment

This accelerator assesses your current Knowledge Management processes and functional maturity within ServiceNow.

## Accelerator Overview

**Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment** provides Impact customers with prescriptive recommendations based on a structured assessment conducted by key stakeholders within the customer's business. By offering customers a snapshot of their current process maturity and targeted next-step recommendations, this accelerator helps maximize the value of their ServiceNow investment.

## Package Availability

**Note:** This Accelerator is available for Impact Guided+ \(Strategic Value add-on\), Advanced and Total Packages.

## What You Get

-   **Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment Kickoff Session \(up to 1.5 hours\)**
    -   Set expectations on the process and depth of deliverable
    -   Communicate expectations for customer participation
    -   Request data and any other inputs
-   **Maturity Assessment Questionnaire**

    Each of the assessment participants fills out a prebuilt Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment Questionnaire. It asks them to grade targeted statements to assess gaps and opportunities with their Knowledge Management deployment.

-   **Questionnaire Results Analysis \(time as needed, determined by the Impact Squad\)**

    The Impact Squad:

    -   Reviews and analyzes the questionnaire scores
    -   Compiles tailored recommendations and leading practices
    -   Provides guidance that will help you understand how to maximize your Knowledge Management investment
-   **Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment Report**

    The report includes:

    -   Maturity score
    -   Key recommendations based on ServiceNow leading practices
    -   Additional Impact initiatives or Accelerators to conduct, or specific recommendations to achieve outcomes
    -   A complete list of recommendations as observed
-   **Customer Read-out Session \(up to 1.5 hours\)**
    -   Deliver Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment report
    -   Review Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment report
    -   Present the assessment results
    -   Discuss targeted recommendations
    -   Provide a suggested plan and resources to support decisions on which recommendations to implement
-   **Follow-up Customer Session \(optional upon customer request, up to 1 hour\)**
    -   Opportunity for Q&amp;A related to the Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment
    -   Provide additional guidance on leading practices

## Outputs

The Knowledge Management Maturity Assessment Report, which includes:

-   Maturity score
-   Key recommendations based on ServiceNow leading practices
-   Additional Impact initiatives or Accelerators to conduct, or specific recommendations to achieve outcomes
-   A complete list of recommendations as observed
