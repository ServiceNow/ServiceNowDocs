# Get started with Impact

A curated bundle of activities that help new Impact users begin their ServiceNow Impact journey and establish a foundation for achieving business objectives.

Getting started with Impact is an onboarding experience that supports Impact offerings, configure relevant features, align platform activities with business priorities, and create a personalized plan to achieve their objectives. The Impact Squad works with you throughout this process to discuss your business goals and develop a strategy for success.

Getting started with Impact provides prescriptive guidance across three key areas:

-   Learning about Impact and its offerings
-   Setting up Impact with its relevant features
-   Outlining your objectives and priorities

This onboarding experience is available for Guided and Total Impact users as part of the Impact deliverable.

## Getting started activities

The activities included in Getting started with Impact vary based on your Impact package:

Guided users:

-   Impact Kickoff Meeting
-   Impact Product Tour

Advanced and Total users:

Along with the activities available to Guided users, Advanced and Total packages include:

-   Executive Kickoff Meetings
-   Get Started with Instance Observer
-   Get Started with Developer Support

## Impact Foundations

Impact Foundations extends the getting started experience with account-level initiatives that accelerate your time to value and maintain momentum. This framework is suitable for both new and experienced users and provides baseline information of your current state with clear next steps.

Impact Foundations operates quarterly so you can maintain momentum on the platform and realize continuous value.

## Impact Foundations deliverables

The deliverables available through Impact Foundations vary by Impact package. All packages receive standard foundational deliverables, while Total package includes package-specific features.

|Impact deliverable|Description|Guided|Advanced|Total|
|------------------|-----------|------|--------|-----|
|Get started with Impact|Kicks off the onboarding experience and evaluates the current state of targeted areas for analysis on the ServiceNow Platform.|✓|✓|✓|
|Customer Impact Plan|Provides general guidelines and recommendations based on your organizational goals and ServiceNow product mix, curated by your Customer Success Manager.|✓|✓|✓|
|Product Adoption Roadmaps|Shows a recommended implementation sequence for your capabilities.|✓|✓|✓|
|Value Management|Supports identification of high-level goals and connects them to measurable outcomes and success metrics that demonstrate value over time.|✓|✓|✓|
|Capabilities Maps|Shows the applications and capabilities at your disposal for achieving your objectives.|✓|✓|✓|

## Key benefits

-   Receive a personalized onboarding experience tailored to your specific needs and business objectives
-   Establish a clear baseline assessment about your current state
-   Define measurable outcomes aligned with your business goals
-   Accelerate your time to value on the ServiceNow Platform
-   Access prescriptive guidance from the Impact Squad
-   Maintain momentum through structured quarterly engagements

## Next steps

To begin your Getting started with Impact experience, contact your Customer Success Manager to schedule your Impact Kickoff Meeting. They will guide you through the onboarding process and help you define your objectives and priorities for the platform.
