# Exploring Impact

ServiceNow Impact is a premium customer success product that combines AI-powered software tools with hands-on expertise that helps organizations maximize the value of their ServiceNow investment.

## What Impact does

Impact is an all-in-one offering for accelerating platform value through:

-   Proactive guidance
-   Embedded tooling
-   Dedicated expert support

At its core, Impact helps you to perform the following key functions:

-   Plan implementation
-   Track product adoption
-   Maintain platform health
-   Resolve platform issues
-   Access expert support

ServiceNow Impact provides the support and expertise to track progress against your outcomes, share insights and industry benchmark comparisons, and make data-driven decisions to optimize your digital transformation journey.

See the Impact overview video to learn more.

## Impact package levels

Depending on your Impact package level \(Guided, Advanced, or Total\), you receive:

-   Support tools and self-help resources
-   Human-led engagements
-   Credits, discounts, and additional benefits

## Human expertise

Impact provides access to expert support through the following offerings:

-   24/7 Support: Provides 24/7 technical support to resolve platform issues
-   Accelerator: Offers a tactical engagement with an expert
-   Developer Support: Seasoned developers help with code fixes
-   Impact Squad: Experts work directly with you to answer your questions

## Impact operating model

The Impact operating model is a structured approach delivered by your Impact Squad to maximize value from the ServiceNow platform. This model maintains focus, prevents scope drift, and enables adaptation to evolving organizational needs.

The model connects your strategic vision to action through two phases:

-   Foundations: Establishes your baseline and plan
-   Steady State: Helps sustain progress through ongoing reviews and support

## Foundations phase

The Foundations phase launches the experience by baselining your current state and creating a tailored roadmap to accelerate time to value. Foundations lays the groundwork for every action you take on the platform.

## Steady State phase

The Steady State phase maintains momentum through regular, consistent initiatives such as:

-   Platform health reviews
-   Operational checks
-   Performance assessments

These activities maintain alignment with your business goals.

## How to get Impact

Impact is delivered through the ServiceNow Store and runs directly within your ServiceNow instance.

The Impact Store Application is included with an Impact subscription, as is the Service Bridge connector that links the app to the Impact Delivery Instance. See [Install Impact](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/impact/install-impact-innovation-lab.md) to get started.

**Note:** The Impact Store Application is the exclusive hub for all innovative features starting with the Yokohama release \(March 2025\).
