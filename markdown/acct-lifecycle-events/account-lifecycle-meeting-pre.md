# Meeting preparation brief

When a meeting is in **Draft** or **Scheduled** state, the meeting page displays the preparation brief to help customer success managers prepare for the conversation.

## Meeting preparation brief

-   **Meeting insights**

    Displays the AI-generated preparation guide for the upcoming meeting. The guide provides a summary of recent account interactions and key discussion topics to help you prepare for the conversation. The guide content is stored in the **Preparation Guide** field in the **Meeting** table. The preparation guide is generated automatically before a scheduled meeting. You can do the following:

    -   If the guide content is long, select **Show more** to expand it. Select **View** to open the full guide.
    -   To edit the guide, select **Refine** to modify tone, length, and audience.
    -   To share the guide by email, select **Draft email**. An email composer opens pre-populated with the guide content.
-   **Participant AI insights**

    Displays AI-generated insights for each meeting participant, including main focus areas, communication style, and attendance history. Participant insights are generated by processing the transcripts of previous meetings that included the same participants. Each participant is shown as a card. Each participant card displays the following AI-generated information derived from prior meeting transcripts. Select **Show more** on a card to expand additional detail.

-   **Main focus area**

    The participant's primary areas of professional focus. Multiple focus areas may be listed.

-   **Communication style**

    An AI-generated characterization of the participant's communication style. Multiple style attributes may be listed.

-   **Attendance**

    A summary of the participant's attendance across prior touchpoint meetings, displayed as a count of meetings attended.


## Agenda and internal notes

-   **Agenda**

    Displays the meeting agenda. Select the pencil icon to edit the agenda. Select **Save** to apply edits or **Cancel** to discard them.

-   **Internal notes**

    A free form notes field for the internal team. Internal notes aren't displayed in external or customer-facing views. Select the pencil icon to edit and **Save** to apply changes.
