# Generate a product adoption roadmap using ServiceNow Otto for Telecommunications, Media, and Technology \(TMT\)

Generate a product adoption roadmap with products or capabilities organized into lanes based on engagement data and insights from similar engagements.

## Before you begin

-   The **Lookup similar engagements** skill must be activated.
-   Role required: `sn_acct_lc.customer_success_agent`

## About this task

Use ServiceNow Otto to generate a roadmap by analyzing the engagement's details, including health, success blueprints, product adoption data, and renewal date. The AI agent identifies similar engagements in the system that have existing roadmaps and uses those patterns to organize items into lanes. After the roadmap is generated, you can further refine it as needed.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspace** &gt; **CRM Workspace** and navigate to the **Success portfolio overview** page.

2.  Select an engagement from the list and navigate to the **Product adoption** tab.

3.  In the Product adoption roadmaps section, select **New product adoption roadmap**.

4.  Enter a name and description for the roadmap and select **Next**.

5.  Select your product adoption implementation strategy: **By product** \(a high-level, strategic plan\) or **By capability** \(short-term, tactical planning\).

6.  Select **ServiceNow Otto** to create a quick draft of your roadmap.

7.  Select a value from the **Phase** drop-down field to determine the lanes for your roadmap.

8.  Select **Generate roadmap**.

    The Product adoption roadmap agent creates lanes based on the **Phase** field. It collects contextual data from the current engagement, identifies similar engagements with existing roadmaps, and arranges products or capabilities into lanes based on those patterns. You can view the steps in the ServiceNow Otto panel.

9.  Review the generated roadmap in the panel: a link to the completed roadmap \(in Draft state\) and a summary of the rearrangement logic.

    You can also request an explanation of the rearrangement logic in the ServiceNow Otto panel.

10. Review the generated roadmap, make any necessary adjustments by dragging items between lanes, and select **Finalize roadmap**.
