# ServiceNow Otto agentic workflows

Customer Success Management agentic workflows collect health data, monitor engagement health scores, analyze and resolve risks, support renewals, schedule touchpoint meetings, identify squad resources, and summarize full account context.

The following agentic workflows are available for Customer Success Management. Each workflow's full overview is documented in the Using section, next to the record and skill it pairs with.

-   **Monitor engagement health**

    Monitors the health score of active engagements and generates risk signals when a decline is detected. See [Dashboards](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-dashboards.md).

-   **Recommend risk signal solutions**

    Analyzes unaddressed risk signals and recommends solutions with minimal user intervention. See [Risk and issues page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-risk-issues-page.md).

-   **Draft and schedule touchpoint meetings**

    Creates and schedules a touchpoint meeting automatically when a risk signal's probability is high. See [Touchpoints](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-use-touchpoints.md).

-   **Support renewals and expansion**

    Assesses engagement and contract health, value, and adoption, and recommends renewal and expansion plays. See [Manage engagements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-manage-engage.md).

-   **Squad resource identifier**

    Identifies and recommends internal experts to support success plays, initiatives, or escalations, based on skill set, region, and availability. See [Manage engagements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-manage-engage.md).

-   **Product release email communication**

    Drafts, refines, and distributes product release announcement emails to customers when a Digital Product Release record is completed. See [Touchpoints](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-use-touchpoints.md).


Agentic workflows included with the base system are read-only. Before you use one, you must duplicate it and adjust the settings for your instance. See [Duplicate an agentic workflow](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/clone-aia-usecase.md).

To activate and configure these workflows, see [Configure agentic workflows for Customer Success Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/acct-lifecycle-events/account-lifecycle-otto-workflows-config.md).
