# Partner Passport Portal release notes

Partner Passport Portal is available in Workflow Data Fabric that enables you to apply committed fabric credits toward partner applications, such as Boomi. Partner Passport Portal is a new feature in the Brazil release.

## About Partner Passport Portal

-   Purchase partner applications directly from your instance using committed fabric credits, without a separate procurement process.
-   Set up access to Boomi, a low-code integration platform as a service \(iPaaS\), and purchase Boomi plans using your fabric credits.
-   View your fabric credit balance, including available credits, total entitled credits, and the reset date.
-   Review every purchase made on the instance in the purchase log.

-   **Activation information**

    Partner Passport Portal is installed on every instance with no separate plugin to activate. Check your entitlements to determine whether you can purchase partner applications. For more information, see .
