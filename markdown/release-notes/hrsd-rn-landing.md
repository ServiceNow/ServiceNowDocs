# HR Service Delivery release notes

HR Service Delivery has new and updated applications and features in the Brazil release.

-   Drive employee productivity from hire to retire — Connect HR services and self-service experiences across the entire employee lifecycle to help employees succeed and engage.
-   Empower employees, managers, and HR agents with self-service and intelligent support — Reduce manual HR work and improve response times through AI-powered insights, case automation, and collaborative workspaces.
-   Unify HR operations across systems and touchpoints — Integrate employee data, workflows, and communications to break down silos and create a seamless HR experience.
-   Accelerate HR delivery and case resolution — Enable HR teams to standardize processes, resolve inquiries faster, and scale support without adding headcount.
-   Gain actionable insights into employee engagement and organizational health — Leverage data and analytics to identify trends, proactively address challenges, and drive strategic HR outcomes.
