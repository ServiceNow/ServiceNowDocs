# Manage refresh of assets using Zero Touch Refresh

Enable your employees to request hardware refreshes through an external vendor by using the Zero Touch Refresh flow on your ServiceNow instance. This process enables you to eliminate having to maintain a local inventory of new assets.

For details on the stages in the Zero Touch Refresh flow, see [Zero Touch Refresh](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/zero-touch-asset-refresh.md).

-   **[Fulfill a Zero Touch Refresh Fulfillment Request](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/process-zero-touch-refresh-order.md)**  
As a provider, ship a replacement asset requested through a Zero Touch Refresh Fulfillment Request to the requester.
-   **[Process a Zero Touch Refresh request](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/process-zero-touch-asset-request.md)**  
Process a Zero Touch Refresh request to receive the assets from your provider and complete the Zero Touch Refresh flow.
