# Configure HAM success goals

Track the success of the Hardware Asset Management application in your instance by creating success goals. You can also create a success goal category for a success goal and track the success of your goals by creating success activities.

A success goal defines a measurable outcome your organization wants to achieve, such as increasing normalization coverage or reducing unmanaged assets. Success activities are the specific steps that contribute to achieving a goal. Success categories group related goals for reporting and workspace display.

|Step|Task|Purpose|
|----|----|-------|
|1|[Create a success goal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/create-suc-goal.md)|Defines a measurable HAM maturity target for your organization.|
|2|[Create success activities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/create-suc-act-hw.md)|Defines a specific step that contributes to achieving a success goal|
|3|[Create a success goal category](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/create-ham-suc-cat.md)|Groups related success goals for reporting and workspace display|

For more details, see [Success portal view in Hardware Asset Workspace](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/hard-suc-port.md) and [View all maturity items for Hardware Asset Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/view-ham-maturity-items.md).

-   **[Create success activities for HAM success goals](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/create-suc-act-hw.md)**

-   **[Create a success goal category for hardware assets](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/create-ham-suc-cat.md)**  
Create a success goal category to associate it with the Hardware Asset Management success goal.
