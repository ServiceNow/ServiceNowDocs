</table>-   **[Installing Hardware Asset Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/installing-ham-app.md)**  
The Hardware Asset Management \(HAM\) application can be installed from the Product Hub or the ServiceNow Store.
-   **[Configuration Console for Hardware Asset Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/config-console-ham.md)**  
The Configuration Console gives administrators a centralized, module-by-module setup experience to configure Hardware Asset Management features after installation.
-   **[Opt-in to the Hardware Asset Management Content Service](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/opt-in-hardware-normalization.md)**  
Opt in to the Hardware Asset Management Content Service to improve the normalization process by sharing hardware and consumable model data from your organization with ServiceNow.
-   **[Opt-in or opt-out of HAM license resource categories](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/optin-optout-ham-license-resource-categories.md)**  
Opt in the Hardware Asset Management \(HAM\) license resource categories that are part of the HAM subscription. You can purchase and opt in the resource categories that you use, and you can opt out and not pay for the categories that you don't use.
-   **[Update associated Decision tables for HAM flows](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/trigger-flow-ham.md)**  
Update associated Decision tables for Hardware Asset Management \(HAM\) flows to trigger a new or customized HAM flow.
-   **[Configure stockrooms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/configure-stockrooms-ham.md)**  
Stockrooms are physical locations where hardware assets are stored before deployment or after return. Configure stockrooms before stockroom technicians begin receiving assets, fulfilling requests, or running inventory audits.
-   **[Integrations and advanced configuration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/integrations-advncd-configs.md)**  
Configure HAM integrations and advanced settings to connect external carriers, enable Zero Touch workflows, track cost and performance, define success goals, and manage charity organizations for asset disposal.
-   **[Configuring ServiceNow Otto for Hardware Asset Management \(HAM\)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/configuring-now-assist-ham.md)**  
Configure the ServiceNow Otto for Hardware Asset Management \(HAM\) application to use the agentic workflow capabilities for managing your hardware asset requests autonomously.
-   **[Applying the CSDM guidelines to Hardware Asset Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/ham-csdm-guidelines.md)**  
Hardware Asset Management manages \(contributes data to\) CSDM tables. Hardware Asset Management also uses data from CSDM tables that other applications generate. Several ServiceNow products, therefore, benefit from and add value to Hardware Asset Management.
