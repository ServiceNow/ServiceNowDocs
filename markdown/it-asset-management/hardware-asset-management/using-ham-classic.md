# Using Hardware Asset Management

Use the Hardware Asset Management application to manage your hardware and consumable assets.

-   **[Procurement and fulfillment](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/procurement-fulfillment-ham.md)**  
Manage the end-to-end procurement and fulfillment of hardware assets, from processing incoming shipments and creating stock orders to handling returns, loaner assets, and AI-assisted request management.
-   **[Inventory and stockroom operations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/manage-your-stockrooms.md)**  
Manage hardware asset inventory and stockroom operations, from receiving and storing assets to monitoring stock levels, conducting audits, and tracking asset location.
-   **[Repair and maintenance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/repair-maintenance-ham.md)**  
Manage hardware asset repairs, lifecycle templates, and warranty information to keep operational and maintenance records of the assets up to date.
-   **[Asset lifecycle and disposal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/asset-lifecycle-disposal-ham.md)**  
Manage the full lifecycle of hardware assets from refresh and replacement through reclamation, disposal, and donation.
-   **[Attestation and conformance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/attestation-compliance-ham.md)**  
Audit hardware asset assignments using attestation and manage HAM subscriptions to maintain accurate and conforming asset records.
-   **[Reporting and performance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/reporting-performance-ham.md)**  
Monitor hardware asset performance, track total cost of ownership, manage stockroom inventory reports, and view HAM maturity items to measure and improve your Hardware Asset Management program.
-   **[Employee self-service](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/employee-self-sevice-ham.md)**  
Request hardware assets, acknowledge deliveries, confirm asset assignments, and report asset issues directly from the Employee Center or Now Mobile.
-   **[Manage hardware asset tasks using the Mobile Agent application](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/hardware-asset-management/manage-hardware-asset-tasks-mobile-agent.md)**  
Manage hardware asset tasks such as repair tasks and asset pick tasks using the intuitive Mobile Agent application.
