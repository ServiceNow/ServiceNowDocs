# Views in AI Cost Management

The AI Cost Management application contains various views that enable you to monitor and analyze your AI spend and usage.

## AI Cost Management landscape

The AI Cost Management application provides a unified interface with multiple views. These views give you comprehensive insights into the spend and usage across all products associated with your AI accounts, such as Claude Code and Copilot . They also enable you to create and manage spend limits.

The AI Cost Management application contains the following views:

-   [Home view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-asset-management/asset-management/home-view.md)
-   [Spend view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-asset-management/asset-management/spend-view.md)
-   [Alerts center view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-asset-management/asset-management/alerts-center-view.md)
-   [Settings view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-asset-management/asset-management/settings-view.md)
