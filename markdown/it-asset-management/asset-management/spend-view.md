# Spend view in the AI Cost Management application

The Spend view provides detailed insights into your AI spend and usage. You can view this information by account, product, model, cost center, department, or user.

You can access the Spend view by selecting **Spend** in the navigation panel of the AI Cost Management application.

\[Omitted image "aicm\_spend.png"\] Alt text: Spend page showing account data table with consumption spend, subscription spend, total seats, spend limit, and total tokens columns.

Use the following tabs to view the corresponding AI spend and usage:

**Note:** You can filter your results by time period using the time period drop-down list. The default time period is **This month**.

-   **Account**: View your AI spend and usage by AI account. Select an account to view additional details.
-   **Product**: View your AI spend and usage by AI product, for example, Claude Code or Claude Chat. Select a product to view additional details.
-   **Model**: For consumption accounts, view your spend and usage by AI model, for example Sonnet or Haiku. Select a model to view additional details.
-   **Cost center**: View your AI spend and usage by cost center. Select a cost center to view additional details. Cost centers are the cost centers configured for the ServiceNow AI Platform.
-   **Department**: View your AI spend and usage by business department. Select a department to view additional details. Departments are the departments configured for the ServiceNow AI Platform
-   **User**: View your AI spend and usage by user. Select a user to view additional details.

When you view the details for one of the entities \(like Product or Model\), the **Overview tab** shows the aggregated spend across all accounts for the entity. To view the entity's breakdown across accounts, products, or model, select the appropriate tab at the top.
