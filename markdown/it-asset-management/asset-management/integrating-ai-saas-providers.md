# Integrating with AI providers

You can integrate with AI providers to track and monitor spend and usage for your AI accounts. Use this information to maintain AI budgets and optimize costs.

When you create an integration, the AI Cost Management application retrieves the corresponding cost and usage data from the given AI provider account. You can then use this data to monitor spend and track usage for that account. You can drill down into your spend and usage by account, product, model, cost center, department, or user.

## Overview of integrating with AI providers

Integrate with AI providers by performing the following tasks:

-   [Add an Anthropic Claude Enterprise integration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-asset-management/asset-management/add-integration-anthropic.md)
-   [Add a Microsoft Copilot integration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-asset-management/asset-management/add-a-microsoft-copilot-integration.md)
