# Subscriptions for Software Asset Management

ServiceNow Software Asset Management \(SAM\), part of the IT Asset Management suite, uses a subscription-based licensing model. Your cost depends on the IT resources you manage, measured in Subscription Units.

Not all IT resources take the same amount of work to manage. For example, a server needs more management than a laptop. To account for this, each resource category is assigned a ratio that sets how many Subscription Units one resource of that type uses. Every Subscription Unit costs the same, so the more work a resource takes to manage, the more units it uses.

For example, suppose your organization manages 50 servers and 200 workstations, and each server uses more Subscription Units than a workstation. The servers can then account for a larger share of your Subscription Unit count and licensing cost, even though there are fewer of them.

-   **[Subscription summary for IT Asset Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/software-asset-management/itam-subscrip-summary.md)**  
The IT Asset Management Subscription Summary dashboard tracks purchased and consumed subscriptions, so you can monitor usage, plan renewals, and stay within your license entitlements.
-   **[Software Asset Management licensing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/software-asset-management/managed-it-resource-types.md)**  
Software Asset Management \(SAM\) licensing is based on five categories of managed IT resources: Server, End User Computing Device, SaaS Subscription User, PaaS Resources, and IaaS Storage. Each category is counted toward your subscription at a different ratio.
-   **[View license report for the IT Asset Management application](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-asset-management/software-asset-management/view-itam-license-sum.md)**  
IT Asset Management \(ITAM\) applications discover, monitor, and provision resources that are stored in the CMDB as configuration items \(CIs\). The ITAM licensing module combines this CI information with your organization’s purchased subscription data to show subscription usage by application. This helps you confirm that your subscription usage aligns with what your organization purchased, so you can avoid under-licensing or overspending.
