# ServiceNow Otto for Telecommunications, Media, and Technology \(TMT\)

Use ServiceNow Otto for TMT to add generative AI skills and agentic workflows to the applications your service and operations teams use every day.

**Note:** Depending on your license, you will have access to certain application features, generative AI skills, agentic workflows, and AI agents. For more information, see [ServiceNow product tiers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/ai-native-sku-overview.md).

Service providers and technology companies often run customer service, order management, and network operations on separate, disconnected systems, which leaves agents without context and slows resolution. ServiceNow Otto for TMT addresses this by working from live data in your instance on the ServiceNow AI Platform. Agents, managers, and customers get relevant information and suggested next steps in the applications where the work happens.

Skills and agentic workflows support the work your teams do every day across TMT applications. For example, agentic workflows diagnose and repair service issues, analyze network incidents, and resolve billing discrepancies for telecommunications providers. For technology providers, AI agents triage and resolve cases, and escalate to a human agent with full context when needed. The same skills and workflows also help monitor customer health, support renewals, and surface customer insights.

Administrators can decide which skills and agents to activate and use. Combine AI agents with deterministic workflows and approvals where your business requires them, and use AI Agent Studio to build and monitor AI agents. The same licensing, security, and data-handling model applies across every TMT application that builds on ServiceNow Otto.

## ServiceNow Otto for TMT benefits

-   Reduce manual work with generative AI skills that summarize records and draft routine notes and communications, so agents spend less time on repetitive tasks.
-   Give agents and managers unified, AI-generated context directly on the record, in configurable workspaces, and in the ServiceNow Otto panel.
-   Automate multi-step work with agentic workflows that act on application data with minimal manual intervention.
-   Configure once and extend consistently. Activate and customize skills from the AI Admin Hub, or copy and edit skills in AI Skill Kit.

## AI limitations

This application uses artificial intelligence \(AI\) and machine learning, which are rapidly evolving fields of study that generate predictions based on patterns in data. As a result, this application may not always produce accurate, complete, or appropriate information. Furthermore, there is no guarantee that this application has been fully trained or tested for your use case. To mitigate these issues, it is your responsibility to test and evaluate your use of this application for accuracy, harm, and appropriateness for your use case, employ human oversight of output, and refrain from relying solely on AI-generated outputs for decision-making purposes. This is especially important if you choose to deploy this application in areas with consequential impacts such as healthcare, finance, legal, employment, security, or infrastructure. You agree to abide by [ServiceNow’s AI Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.servicenow.com/ai-acceptable-use-policy.html), which may be updated by ServiceNow.

## Data processing

This application requires data to be transferred from ServiceNow customers' individual instances to a centralized ServiceNow environment, which may be located in a different data center region from the one where your instance is, and potentially to a third-party cloud provider, such as Microsoft Azure. This data is handled per ServiceNow's internal policies and procedures, including our policies available through our [CORE Compliance Portal](https://support.servicenow.com/kb?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0564067).

## Data collection

ServiceNow collects and uses the inputs, outputs, and edits to outputs of this application to develop and improve ServiceNow technologies including ServiceNow models and AI products. In addition, this application will collect record information used by generative AI skills across ServiceNow Otto for TMT applications. Customers can opt out of future data collection at any time, as described in the [AI Opt-Out page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/opt-out-of-data-sharing-for-now-assist.md).

For more information, see the [ServiceNow AI documentation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/intelligent-experiences/ai-products.md).
