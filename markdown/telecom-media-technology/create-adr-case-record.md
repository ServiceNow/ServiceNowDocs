# Create a Customer Dispute Management case

Register a customer dispute by capturing information about the initiator, the affected product or service, and supporting documents. Resolve the dispute by investigating the issue and proposing an appropriate resolution.

## Before you begin

Verify that the Customer Dispute Management \(CDM\) application is activated.

Role required: sn\_telco\_adr\_mgmt.manager

## About this task

Disputes can be submitted directly by the customer or by an authorized representative such as a legal guardian, legal firm, or regulatory body.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Workspaces** &gt; **CSM/FSM Configurable Workspace**.

2.  Select the lists \(\[Omitted image "Lists.png"\] Alt text: ListIcon.\) icon.

3.  Navigate to **Customer Dispute Management** &gt; **All**.

4.  Select **New**.

    A new customer dispute record opens.

5.  On the Capture initiator details form, enter the initiator details.

    For field descriptions, see [Capture initiator details form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/telecom-media-technology/capture-initiator-details-form.md).

6.  Select **Continue**.

7.  On the Capture CDM information form, enter the dispute details.

    For field descriptions, see [Capture CDM information form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/telecom-media-technology/capture-adr-information-form.md).

8.  Select **Continue**.

9.  On the Upload documents form, select **Add file** to attach supporting evidence.

10. Select **Mark Complete**.


## Result

The dispute record is created with any uploaded attachments. An email notification is sent to the customer or authorized representative confirming the dispute registration. The dispute moves to the Investigation stage. For more information, see [Investigate customer disputes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/telecom-media-technology/investigate-customer-dispute.md).
