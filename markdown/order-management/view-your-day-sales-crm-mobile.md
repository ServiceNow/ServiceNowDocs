# View your day on Sales CRM Mobile

View your meetings, tasks, and priority opportunities for the day on the My Day tab of Sales CRM Mobile.

## Before you begin

Role required: **sales\_mobile\_user**

## About this task

## Procedure

1.  Open Sales CRM Mobile and tap **My Day**.

    The **My Day** tab shows the following sections:

    -   **Today's meetings**: your scheduled meetings for the day.
    -   **Tasks due**: tasks assigned to you, such as lead and opportunity tasks.
    -   **Opportunities in focus**: a summary of your critical and at-risk opportunities.
2.  Select the search icon \[Omitted image "field-search.png"\] Alt text: on the **My Day** tab to search your records using AI Search.

    You can search for the following records and their line items:

    -   Opportunities
    -   Leads
    -   Accounts
    -   Contacts
    You can also use voice search. For more information, see [Enable voice search](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/mobile/turn-on-voice-search.md).

    **Note:** AI Search is not available in offline mode.

3.  Scroll to the bottom of the **My Day** tab to access **Quick Access**.

4.  The Quick Access tab shows the following sections:

    -   Tasks: View the list of tasks accessible to you and the status of those tasks. You can select a task to view more details.
    -   Calendar: You can view the scheduled meetings.

## What to do next

Visit the Sales Hub tab to manage your accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities.
