# Configure Channel Partner table

With the Partner Relationship Management application, you can use the channel partner \[sn\_prm\_channel\_partner\] table to manage and store information related to channel partners.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_prm.enterprise\_partner\_admin or sn\_prm.channel\_partner\_writer

## About this task

The channel partner \[sn\_prm\_channel\_partner\] table is an extension of External Business Location.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Partner Relationship Management** &gt; **Channel Partner**.

2.  Select **New** to open the Channel Partner Registration details form.

3.  On the form, fill in the fields.

4.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Partner Relationship Management** &gt; **Channel Partner**.

5.  Select **New** to open the Channel Partner Registration details form.

6.  In the **Name** field, enter the name of the channel partner.

7.  From the **Business function** list, select the business function associated with the channel partner.

8.  In the **Enterprise partner manager** field, select the manager from the enterprise associated with the channel partner.

    When an enterprise manager is added on the channel partner form, they are also added as a member of the enterprise with a role and responsibility.

9.  In the **Partner manager** field, select the manager from the channel partner that supervises partner member activities.

    When a partner manager is added on the channel partner form, they are also added as a member of the enterprise with a role and responsibility.

10. From the **Status** list, select the status of the channel partner.

11. In the **Parent channel partner** field, select the parent entity of the channel partner.

12. In the **Company** field, select the company associated with the channel partner.

13. Select **Submit**.
