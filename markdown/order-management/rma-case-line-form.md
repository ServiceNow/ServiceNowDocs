# RMA case line form

Fields on the Return Merchandise Authorization \(RMA\) case line form.

|Field|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|Number|Number associated to the case.|
|Account|Account associated to the case.|
|Contact|Contact associated to the case.|
|Channel|Channel associated to the case.|
|Request source|Source through which a case can be initiated, whether install base item or sold product.|
|Short description|Description of the case.|
|State|State of the case, whether closed, new, or draft.|
|Priority|Level of importance assigned to the case.|
|Assignment group|Assignment group associated to the case.|
|Assigned to|Agent to whom the case is assigned.|
|Needs attention|Check box to signify whether case needs immediate attention.|
