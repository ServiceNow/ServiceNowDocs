# View RMA cases on the Business portal

Enable customers to view and respond to RMA cases directly on the Business Portal, without having to contact support.

## Before you begin

Role required: customer, sn\_csm\_rma\_case.csm\_rma\_case\_viewer

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to the Business portal.

2.  From the **Requests** menu in the header, select **View submitted requests** &gt; **View cases and tasks**.

3.  From the **Categories** list, select **My RMA cases** to view all the cases assigned to you.

4.  Open a case to view the short description, status, and other details associated with the case.

    |Field|Description|
    |-----|-----------|
    |Status|Status of the RMA case.|
    |Created|Date on which the RMA case was created.|
    |Last updated|Date on which the case details were last updated.|

5.  Select the RMA case lines related list tab to view the activity details, status, parent case, proposed resolution and more information associated to the case line item.
