# Enable Contracts and Entitlements on the Business Portal

Admins can enable contracts and entitlements on the Business Portal so customers can view their contracts and entitlements.

## Before you begin

The application scope must be set to Business Portal. You can change the application scope using the application picker \[Omitted image "globe-outline-24.svg"\] Alt text: in the Unified Navigation bar.

Role required: admin

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Service Portal** &gt; **Portals**.

2.  Select **Business Portal**.

3.  Enable the **Enable Web Embeddables** option.

    The **Select components being embedded** field becomes available.

4.  In the **Select components being embedded** field, add **sn-embedx-object-list**.

5.  Select **Save**.

    Contracts and entitlements are now enabled on the Business Portal.


## What to do next

Customers can now access and view contracts and entitlements from the Business Portal. For more information, see [View contracts on the Business Portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/order-management/view-contracts-business-portal.md) and [View entitlements on the Business Portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/order-management/view-entitlements-business-portal.md).
