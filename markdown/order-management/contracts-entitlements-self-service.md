# View contracts and entitlements on the Business Portal

Customers can view contracts and entitlements associated with their accounts on the Business Portal.

From the Business Portal, customers can view their all their contracts and entitlements. They can select a contract or entitlement and view all the related information like Contract number, Product offer, Customer Contract Line, Start date, End date, and so on.

-   **[View contracts on the Business Portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/order-management/view-contracts-business-portal.md)**  
Customers can view contracts associated with their accounts on the Business Portal, including contract details and contract line items.
-   **[View entitlements on the Business Portal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/order-management/view-entitlements-business-portal.md)**  
Customers can view entitlements associated with their accounts on the Business Portal, including entitlement details and related information.
