# Fill in the General Inquiry form

Use the General Inquiry form on the Partner portal to raise queries and get in touch with the enterprise.

## Before you begin

Role required: none.

## Procedure

1.  In the **Partner Organization** field, select the partner organization to which you belong.

2.  In the **Requested by** field, confirm the user raising the request.

3.  From the **Priority** list, select the level of importance of the inquiry.

4.  In the **Subject** field, enter an overview of the inquiry.

5.  In the **What can we help you with** field, enter a detailed explanation of the inquiry.
