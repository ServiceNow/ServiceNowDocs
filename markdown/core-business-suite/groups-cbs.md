# Groups in Core Business Suite module

Groups enable you to organize people for tasks and projects across Core Business Suite. Assign team members to access, create, and manage shared settings for one or more business units.

Core Business Suite version 3.3.2 has analytics editor and analytics viewer roles by default. You can select the edit button to add or remove people from those roles.

\[Omitted image "groups-cbs.png"\] Alt text: Groups sub-module under the Core Business Suite module in Configuration Console.

Groups enable you to manage permissions and access control efficiently across your Core Business Suite implementation. Any business rules, assignment rules, system roles, or attributes that refer to the group apply to all group members automatically.

Edit default groups to add or remove members in Core Business Suite.
-   **[Create group in Core Business Suite module](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/core-business-suite/create-group-cbs.md)**  
Create custom groups for specific Core Business Suite functions and assign roles to group members.
