# Agent Experience widgets

Core Business Suite Agent Experience includes three widgets that help agents view and manage their workload: On Your Plate, Approvals, and My Work.

The Agent Experience dashboard includes three widgets designed for Core Business Suite agents. Each widget serves a distinct purpose to help you manage your workload efficiently.

## On Your Plate widget

The On Your Plate widget provides a quick snapshot of your current workload. This widget displays counts only and does not include clickable actions.

The widget shows the following categories:

-   **Awaiting your approval**

    Number of approval requests pending your action

-   **SLA risk**

    Number of items that will breach their SLA within 24 hours, including both tasks and cases

-   **Due today**

    Number of items with a due date of today

-   **Closed today**

    Number of items closed today

-   **Others**

    Remaining work items not included in the other categories


**Note:** The On Your Plate widget includes only cases, tasks, and approvals from Core Business Suite business units. Incidents are not included.

## Approvals widget

The Approvals widget displays pending approval requests and lets you take action directly from the dashboard.

You can perform the following actions from the Approvals widget:

-   Approve or reject approval requests
-   Open the full record for detailed review
-   View all pending approvals

The widget displays a maximum of two approval items by default. If you have more than two pending approvals, select **See all** to view the complete list.

## My Work widget

The My Work widget provides a comprehensive view of all work items assigned to you. It includes more content types and functionality than the On Your Plate widget.

The My Work widget includes the following item types:

-   Cases from Core Business Suite business units such as Finance, Procurement, and SLO
-   Tasks assigned to you
-   Approval requests
-   Incidents, if ITSM is installed on your instance

The widget displays five items by default. Use pagination to view additional items.

You can filter work items by the following criteria:

-   Department
-   SLA risk
-   VIP cases, based on whether the requester has VIP status
-   Recently updated items

You can sort work items by record priority, newest first, or oldest first. Use the search field to find specific work items by keyword.

## Platform widgets

In addition to the three Core Business Suite widgets, you can add other widgets from the ServiceNow platform to your Agent Experience canvas.
