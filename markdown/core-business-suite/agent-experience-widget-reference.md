# Agent Experience widget reference

Quick reference for the three Core Business Suite widgets available in Agent Experience, including content types, available actions, and display limits.

## Widget comparison

The following table compares the three Core Business Suite widgets available in Agent Experience.

|Widget|Purpose|Content types|Actions available|Display limit|
|------|-------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|
|On Your Plate|Workload snapshot|CBS cases, tasks, and approvals only|View only|All items shown|
|Approvals|Manage pending approvals|Approvals|Approve, Reject, Open record, See all|2 items \(expandable\)|
|My Work|Comprehensive work list|CBS cases, tasks, approvals, and incidents \(if ITSM is installed\)|Open record, Filter, Sort, Search|5 items \(paginated\)|

## On Your Plate categories

The On Your Plate widget displays work items in the following categories.

|Category|Description|
|--------|-----------|
|Awaiting your approval|Approval requests pending your action|
|SLA risk|Items that will breach their SLA within 24 hours \(includes tasks and cases\)|
|Due today|Items with a due date of today|
|Closed today|Items closed today|
|Others|Remaining work items not included in other categories|

## My Work widget filters and sorting

The My Work widget supports the following filters.

|Filter|Description|
|------|-----------|
|Department|Filter work items by department|
|SLA risk|Show only items at risk of breaching their SLA within 24 hours|
|VIP cases|Show only cases where the requester has VIP status|
|Recently updated|Show items that were recently modified|

The My Work widget supports the following sorting options:

-   Record priority
-   Newest first
-   Oldest first
