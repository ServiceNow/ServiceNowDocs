# Access Agent Experience

Access the Agent Experience dashboard to view and manage your work items across Core Business Suite business units.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_cbs\_fulfiller

## About this task

You can access Agent Experience through the application navigator or by entering the URL directly in your browser.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Core Business Suite** &gt; **Agent Experience**.

    Alternatively, enter `https://<instance-name>.service-now.com/cbshome/home` in your browser, replacing `<instance-name>` with your instance name.

    The Agent Experience dashboard opens, displaying your work items across all Core Business Suite business units.
