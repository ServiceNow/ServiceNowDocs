# Create a Knowledge Base

Knowledge bases are created in Knowledge Center and can be created at a business unit level or at a product level in the Core Business Suite.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_cbs.admin

## About this task

Create knowledge bases to organize and manage knowledge articles for your organization.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Core Business Suite** &gt; **Configure**.

2.  Select **Knowledge** in Core Business Suite module under the Configuration Console, and select the **Create new** button.

3.  On the Create New Knowledge Base form, fill in the fields.

4.  Select **Save**.

    For a description of the field values, see [Create a knowledge base](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/servicenow-platform/create-a-knowledgebase.md).
