# Simplified IT Service Management glossary

Learn about the terms and concepts in Simplified IT Service Management.

**Parent Topic:**[Simplified IT Service Management reference](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-service-management/ai-native-it-service-desk-reference.md)

## A

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### AI-native IT Service Desk

An AI-native solution that provides simplified, automated implementation for administrators and a unified experience for employees and fulfillers across multiple ITSM applications.

### AI summary and next steps

A section on the incident form in AI-native IT Service Desk that presents an AI-generated summary of the issue, key actions taken, resolution, and proposed next steps.

### Assess state

A state in the Simplified Change Management Normal change model that confirms a change has proper documentation and is ready for authorization. Approvers are configured separately for Low, Moderate, and High risk.

### Authorize state

The business approval gate in Simplified Change Management change models, which verifies that a change is approved to proceed to implementation.

## B

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### blackout schedule

A schedule in Simplified Change Management that blocks changes during critical periods, such as financial year-end or major platform releases.

## C

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### Change Registration change model

A change model in Simplified Change Management that records changes implemented outside the standard workflow, such as vendor-managed changes or platform upgrades. It has no approval workflow.

### Configuration Console

The page in Simplified IT Service Management where all required setup is consolidated into a step-by-step guided experience, so administrators can track configuration progress in one place.

### Configure with AI

A conversational flow in Simplified IT Service Management that administrators can use instead of the form-based wizard to complete a configuration. It's accessible from the banner at the top of a configuration page.

## E

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### Emergency change model

A change model in Simplified Change Management that provides a fast-track workflow for urgent changes requiring expedited authorization. It restricts who can submit these changes and notifies stakeholders when one is created.

## I

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### implementation group

A group assigned in Simplified Change Management team roles whose members receive change write access, allowing them to create change requests, be assigned as implementers, and complete review tasks.

## M

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### maintenance window

A schedule in Simplified Change Management that designates periods when changes are actively expected, such as recurring patching cycles. Changes are allowed only during this time.

## N

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### Normal change model

A change model in Simplified Change Management that provides a CAB-governed workflow for planned changes requiring risk assessment, approvals, and post-implementation review.

## R

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### risk assessment question

A survey question presented to the change requester in Simplified Change Management. Each response carries a score, and the cumulative score determines the risk level of the change.

## S

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### score threshold

A score boundary in Simplified Change Management that maps a cumulative risk assessment score to a risk level of Low, Moderate, or High.

### Service Desk Team Dashboard

A dashboard in AI-native IT Service Desk that service desk managers use to view key performance metrics, including incident and requested item backlog and team MTTR.

### Simplified Change Management

A version of Change Management that offers the core functionality of the full Change Management application with opinionated defaults and guided setup. It's purpose-built for organizations that need a fast, low-friction path to change governance.

### Standard change model

A change model in Simplified Change Management for pre-approved, low-risk, repeatable changes. Requesters create these changes by selecting a template rather than using the change type picker, and the changes bypass the full approval workflow.

## T

Glossary terms are grouped alphabetically.

### team roles

The configuration area in Simplified Change Management that defines who can approve, build, or review a change by mapping individuals and groups to their roles in the change process.
