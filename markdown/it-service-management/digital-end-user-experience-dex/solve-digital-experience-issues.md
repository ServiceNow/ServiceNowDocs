# Solving digital experience issues

As an employee, use Desktop Assistant and Digital End-user Experience Self-service to check your device's health, run network diagnostics, review IT notifications, and self-resolve common device issues without opening a support ticket.

## Overview of self-solving digital experience issues

When you experience a device or application issue, you can often resolve it yourself with Digital End-User Experience tools before contacting IT. Desktop Assistant gives you a dedicated interface on your device for network diagnostics, IT notifications, Employee Center access, and Virtual Agent chat. Digital End-user Experience Self-service lets you check your device's health from Desktop Assistant, Employee Center, or the ServiceNow Otto for ITSM Virtual Agent and self-apply suggested resolutions.

-   [Use DEX Desktop Assistant](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-service-management/digital-end-user-experience-dex/use-dex-desktop-exp.md)

    Use Desktop Assistant to check your device's health, run network diagnostics, chat with ServiceNow Otto, open Employee Center, view outages, and see IT notifications on your desktop.

-   [Using Digital End-user Experience Self-service](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/it-service-management/digital-end-user-experience-dex/using-dex-self-service.md)

    Use Digital End-user Experience Self-service to monitor your device's performance and apply suggested resolutions to common device issues, from either Desktop Assistant, Employee Center, or the ServiceNow Otto for ITSM Virtual Agent.
