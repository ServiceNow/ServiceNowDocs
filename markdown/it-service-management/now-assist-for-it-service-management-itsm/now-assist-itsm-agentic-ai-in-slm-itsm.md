# Agentic AI in Service Level Management

Use the ITSM Service Level Management agentic workflows to manage and complete tasks autonomously.

## Agentic workflows for the Service Level Management application

**Important:**

To access the Service Level Management agentic workflows, make sure that you install the following applications:

1.  ServiceNow Otto for IT Service Management \(ITSM\) \(sn\_itsm\_gen\_ai\) plugin.
2.  Service Level Management application. For more information, see [Configuring Service Level Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-service-management/service-level-management/configuring-service-level-management.md).

**Note:**

-   Before you begin, review these [Important considerations for using ServiceNow Otto for IT Service Management \(ITSM\) agentic AI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-service-management/now-assist-for-it-service-management-itsm/important-considerations-itsm-agentic-ai.md).
-   For all available ITSM Agentic AI applications, see [Use agentic AI in ServiceNow Otto for IT Service Management \(ITSM\)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-service-management/now-assist-for-it-service-management-itsm/now-assist-itsm-ai-agents-use-cases.md).
