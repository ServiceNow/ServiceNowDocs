# Reject multiple major incident candidates using the Actions menu

Reject one or more major incident candidates at once from the Actions menu when they don't qualify as major incidents, and provide a rejection reason.

## Before you begin

Role required: major\_incident\_manager

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Incident** &gt; **Major Incidents** &gt; **Candidates**.

    **Note:** If the UI16 module link redirection feature is enabled in Service Operations Workspace \(SOW\) and the UI16 module supports the redirect configuration, navigating through UI16 paths automatically redirects you to the equivalent list or record pages in SOW instead of displaying the UI16 forms or lists. For more information, see [Redirect UI16 module links to Service Operations Workspace](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-service-management/service-operations-workspace/redirect-ui16-module-links-sow.md).

2.  Select the candidates that you need to reject.

3.  On the Actions menu, select **Reject Major Incident Candidate**.

    \[Omitted image "reject-major-inc-candidate.png"\] Alt text: Major incident candidates list with the Reject Major Incident Candidate option selected on the Actions menu

4.  In the Rejection Reason dialog box, enter the reason for rejecting the candidates.

5.  Select **Reject**.

    The major incident candidates are rejected.
