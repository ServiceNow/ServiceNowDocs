# Incident Management glossary

Definitions of the terms and concepts used in Incident Management, grouped alphabetically.

**Parent Topic:**[Incident Management reference]()

## A

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter A.

### Awaiting caller

An On hold reason in Incident Management that shifts responsibility for an incident to the caller. It makes Additional comments mandatory and is cleared when the caller updates the incident.

## B

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter B.

### Breached incident

In Incident Management, an incident whose Service Level Agreement \(SLA\) has exceeded its due date.

## C

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter C.

### Child incident

An incident in Incident Management that is linked to a parent incident and whose state is synchronized with the parent's state.

### Closed Incomplete

The state that Incident Management sets on open incident tasks when their parent incident is closed.

### Closed Skipped

The state that Incident Management sets on open incident tasks when their parent incident is canceled.

### Communication plan

A plan contains and groups the communication tasks that is performed to keep the stakeholders informed during a major incident.

### Communication task

A task within a communication plan that is executed to communicate and notify about a major incident, for example by email or conference.

## D

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter D.

### Deny-Unless ACL

An access control list \(ACL\) used by the Incident Management enhanced security model to restrict access to incident-related tables for non-authenticated users.

## I

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter I.

### Incident

In Incident Management, a record of an unplanned interruption or degradation of a service. It moves through states from New to Closed, and the goal is to restore normal service operation.

### Incident at risk

In Incident Management, an incident that is at risk of breaching its SLA. It is listed in the ITSM Mobile Agent app dashboard.

### Incident state model

The set of states an incident moves through in Incident Management: New, In Progress, On Hold, Resolved, Closed, and Canceled.

### Incident task

A record in Incident Management used to track, collaborate with and request work from other stakeholders on an incident. Its state is synchronized with the state of the incident.

### Incident template

In Incident Management, a template that populates fields on the incident form so that information about similar incidents is captured consistently thereby reducing time and effort.

### Incident ticketing integration

An integration that exchanges incident ticket data between your instance and a third-party system. It can be uni-directional or bi-directional.

### ITSM Mobile Agent

A mobile app that gives IT agents base system mobile-first experiences to triage, act on, and resolve incidents on the go.

## K

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter K.

### Known Error

A resolution code in Incident Management. When you select it, you can attach a Known Error article to the incident record.

## M

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter M.

### Major incident

In Incident Management, an incident that causes significant disruption to the business. It needs a response beyond the routine incident process, with shorter timescales and higher priority. The incident is escalated to major incident. The incident can be proposed as a major incident candidate or promoted directly to major incident.

### Major incident candidate

An incident proposed or automatically flagged based on the set major incident trigger rules as a potential major incident candidate. A major incident manager can promote the major incident candidate to a major incident or reject. In case, rejected the major incident candidate becomes an incident again.

### Major incident manager

The user in Incident Management who analyzes major incident candidates and manages major incidents life cycle and process. This user has the major\_incident\_manager role.

### Major incident trigger rule

A rule in Incident Management that defines the conditions under which an incident is automatically marked as a major incident candidate. The base system rules are disabled by default.

### Major incident workbench

A single pane view in Incident Management for major incident managers, communication managers, and resolver groups. It aggregates actionable information for managing major incidents.

## O

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter O.

### On hold reason

A field in Incident Management that records why an incident is On hold: Awaiting Caller, Awaiting Change, Awaiting Problem, or Awaiting vendor.

## P

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter P.

### Parent incident

An incident in Incident Management whose state drives the state of its associated child incidents.

### Post incident report

A report on the major incident workbench that records the cause, resolution, findings, and timeline of a major incident. It is used to assess the response and determine follow-up actions.

## R

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter R.

### Record producer

A service catalog item that creates an incident, using an incident template to populate fields such as Category, Subcategory, and Assignment Group.

## W

Incident Management glossary terms that start with the letter W.

### Wrap-up and resolve incident agentic workflow

An agentic workflow in Incident Management that generates resolution notes, selects a resolution code, and attaches or drafts knowledge and Known Error articles to resolve an incident.
