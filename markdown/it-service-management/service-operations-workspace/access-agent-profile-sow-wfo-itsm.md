# Access your profile in Service Operations Workspace

Track the real-time status of your work by accessing your profile in Service Operations Workspace. View the number of incidents and interactions that you have closed or are working on, your roles and groups, and information about your manager and teammates.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_wfo\_cfg\_itsm.employee

## About this task

Access your profile to analyze information such as:

-   Key performance indicators that matter most to you and Performance Analytics targets set for you by your manager. For information on Performance Analytics, see [Performance Analytics targets](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/now-intelligence/pa-targets.md).
-   Pending training that has been assigned to you and when they're due.
-   Service channels that you are assigned to and their current utilization.
-   Skills and skill levels that you currently use and skill gaps that you may want to address.
-   Upcoming paid time off \(PTO\).

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **Service Operations Workspace**.

    When you select the home icon \(\[Omitted image "home-icon.png"\] Alt text:\), you can view the status of your work, organizational performance, current status of incidents, and unassigned incidents in the [Service Operations Workspace landing page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-service-management/service-operations-workspace/sow-ui-landing-page.md).

2.  Select the lists icon \(\[Omitted image "lists\_icon.png"\] Alt text:\).

3.  In the **New List** screen, select **Create your own**.
4.  In the **List Name** field, enter a name for the list; for example, My Profile.
5.  In the **Select Source** field, select **User** \(sys\_user\).
6.  In the **Add Filters** condition builder, select **\[Name\] \[is\] \[&lt;your name&gt;\]**.

 The workspace adds your profile to the **My Lists** tab.
