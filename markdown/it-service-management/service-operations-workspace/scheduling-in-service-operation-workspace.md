# Scheduling in Service Operations Workspace

View and manage schedules, shifts and time-off requests.

**Schedules** menu helps agents view and manage day-to-day work, request time off and propose coverage for absence.

Shift manager can create and manage schedules, shifts, team members, escalation trigger rules and policies for their group. They can also approve or reject time-off requests, and resolve conflicts and gaps for their group. An on-call admin can perform these tasks for any group. \[Omitted image "Scheduling-in-SOW.png"\] Alt text: Schedules page in Service Operations Workspace
