# Left navigation bar in Simplified IT Service Management

The left navigation bar provides quick access to Simplified IT Service Management core functions and workspace navigation without navigating away from your current work.

## Overview

The left navigation bar appears as a persistent vertical rail on the left side of the fulfiller interface, positioned above the existing Simplified IT Service Management navigation. The rail provides icon-based access to key fulfiller functions and allows you to navigate between workspaces and saved views without interrupting your current task.
