# Use Surveys for EmployeeWorks

Answer the questions in a survey assigned to you, save your progress, and submit your responses using Surveys for EmployeeWorks.

## Before you begin

-   Your admin must install the Surveys for EmployeeWorks app.
-   Your admin must configure the **Surveys** quick link for [Employee Slate for Moveworks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/core-business-suite/config-es-cbs-moveworks.md) or [Employee Slate for Now Assist](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/core-business-suite/config-es-cbs-na.md) depending on the application you are using.

Role required: none

## Procedure

1.  From the EmployeeWorks Web App homepage, select the **Surveys** quick link.

    The **Surveys** page opens.

2.  On **Surveys**, review the surveys currently assigned to you.

    Each card shows the survey title, a short description, and — when applicable — your progress and how soon it's due. A card marked **Due today** or **Due tomorrow** is called out so it's easy to spot.

    If you have no surveys assigned, the page shows a message saying you're caught up instead of the card list.

3.  Select a survey card to answer and submit that survey.

4.  From **Surveys**, select a survey card to open it.

    The survey opens to the first section or question you haven't answered yet. If you've already answered everything, the survey opens to the last section.

5.  Answer each question on the page.

    A required question is marked with an asterisk. Depending on how the survey is set up, some questions only appear after you answer an earlier question a certain way.

    Depending on the question, you select an option, enter a response, choose a date, rank a list of items, or attach a file.

6.  Select **Next** to save your answers on the current page and move to the next one, or **Previous** to go back.

    Your answers are saved as you move between pages. You can leave and come back to the survey later — your progress is kept.

7.  If a question accepts file attachments, select **Add files** to attach one or more files, or select the delete icon next to a listed file to remove it.

8.  On the last page, select **Submit**.

    **Warning:** You can't change your answers after you submit the survey.


## Result

Your responses are recorded and the survey is marked complete.
