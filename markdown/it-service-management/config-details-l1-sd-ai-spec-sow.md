# Configure L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist basic details

Customize the name, profile icon, department, and description of your L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist to match your organization's specifications.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_itsm\_common.sn\_service\_desk\_manager

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Service Operations Workspace**.

2.  On the Homepage, in the **L1 Service Desk AI Specialist** card, select **Activate**.

3.  In the Confirm L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist Assignment window, review or modify the following details.

    To modify the details, select the **Edit** icon and then select **Save**.

Assignment groups

 For example, to stop the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist from handling your team’s tickets, you can remove your team user group.

</td></tr><tr><td>

</td></tr><tr><td>

Language

</td><td>

Language in which the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist communicates when interacting across workflow and user communications.**Note:** This is a read-only field.

</td></tr></tbody>
</table>    **Note:** You can also select the **View full details** action to open the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist details page in a separate tab within the SOW experience and setup the L1 Service Desk AI Specialist.

4.  Select **Add to team**.

    A success message of the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist added to your team is displayed.
