# Process an unassigned incident

Use the Unassigned incidents applet to assign, reassign, edit, or promote an incident to a major incident.

## Before you begin

Role required: group manager

## Procedure

1.  On the My team landing screen, tap **See All** on the **Unassigned incidents** applet.

    The **Unassigned Incidents** screen opens with a list of incidents.

2.  To reduce the number of records on the screen, see [Filter records in ITSM Mobile Agent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/it-service-management/itsm-mobile-agent/filter-records-agent-mobile.md) to search for records that match specific search criteria.

3.  On the **Unassigned incidents** screen with the list of incidents, swipe left on an incident to open the menu.

    \[Omitted image "unassigned-incidents.png"\] Alt text: List of unassigned incidents with the swipe-left menu open.

4.  On the menu, choose one of the actions to process the incident.

    |Action|Description|
    |------|-----------|
    |**Add comments**|Add any additional comments or work notes about the incident in this field and tap \[Omitted image "mobile\_instances\_send\_droid.png"\] Alt text: send icon.|
    |**Assign to me**|Use this action to assign an incident to yourself.|
    |**Assign**|On the Assign incident screen, tap the **Assignment group** field to change the assignment group, select the agent from the list in the **Assigned to** field, and add any notes in the **Work notes** field, and tap \[Omitted image "mobile\_instances\_send\_droid.png"\] Alt text: send icon to submit.|

5.  On the **Unassigned incidents** screen, tap a record in the list to open it and view more details.

6.  On the **Unassigned Incidents** screen as shown in the following figure, choose one of the actions in the table as appropriate.

    \[Omitted image "unassigned-process.png"\] Alt text: Unassigned incident record with its tabs and available actions.
