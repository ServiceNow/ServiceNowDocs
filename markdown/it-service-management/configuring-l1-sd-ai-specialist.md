Configure the name, skills, assignment groups, roles, language proficiencies, and tasks of the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist.

    Assign the Service Desk Manager role to add the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist to your team.

2.  [Configure basic details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-service-management/config-details-l1-sd-ai-spec-sow.md).

3.  [Configure profile settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-service-management/config-profile-l1-sd-ai-spec-sow.md).

    Configure the skills, roles, assignment group, and language proficiencies of the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist.

4.  [Configure the task settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-service-management/config-tasks-l1-sd-ai-spec-sow.md).

    Configure the tasts that the L1 IT Service Desk AI Specialist is capable of.

5.  [Test the L1 Service Desk AI Specialist](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/it-service-management/test-l1-sd-ai-spec-sow.md).
