# Case create component

Enables you to create a case to address any issues related to products and services.

Use the Case create component to let customers submit support cases from your own website. The form collects the details of the case, such as the product, asset, description, and urgency. After a customer submits a case, it appears in the Case list component.

**Note:** Case create component supports a mobile adaptive layout.

-   [Playbook intake component](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/australia/customer-service-management/customer-self-service-and-omnichannel-engagement/we-playbook-intake-comp-overview.md): Creates a case through a guided experience that shows the stages of resolution.
