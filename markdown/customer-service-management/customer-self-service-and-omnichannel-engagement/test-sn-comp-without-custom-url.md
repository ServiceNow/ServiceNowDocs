# Test the ServiceNow components without setting up a custom URL

Enables you to test the ServiceNow components on your sandbox instance without setting up a custom URL.

## Before you begin

Make sure third-party cookies are enabled in your browsers, such as Google Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge.

Role required: admin

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **Web embeddables** &gt; **Configure**.

2.  On the Configure tab, in the **Specify your custom domain** field enter the ServiceNow instance URL.

3.  In the browser, open the test site.


## Result

The ServiceNow component appears on the test browser page.
