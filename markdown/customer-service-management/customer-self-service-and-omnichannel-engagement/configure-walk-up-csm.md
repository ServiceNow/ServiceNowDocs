# Configure Walk-up Experience for Customer Service Management

Set up Walk-up Experience to match your organization's service requirements, including locations, schedules, appointments, notifications, and portals.

## Before you begin

Role required: admin

## About this task

Users with the **sn\_csm\_walkup.walkup\_admin** role and administrators can activate and configure the application. Perform these tasks based on the plugin you activated.

Configure appointments, notifications, and physical queue locations with operating schedules. Associate stockrooms with queue locations and integrate with CSAT surveys. Customize the Walk-up Experience portal with a unique logo and branding.

To configure CSM Walk-up Experience, complete the configuration tasks in the following table.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **All** &gt; **CSM Walk-up Experience** &gt; **Administration**.

2.  Complete the following tasks to configure the CSM Walk-up Experience feature.
