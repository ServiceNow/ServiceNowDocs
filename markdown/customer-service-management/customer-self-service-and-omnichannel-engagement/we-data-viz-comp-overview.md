# Data visualization component

Use the Data visualization component to show data from any ServiceNow table as a score, pie, donut, or semi-donut chart.

Use this component to monitor activity such as cases and incidents and to support decisions based on the data shown.

Following are the visual elements which you can embed on your webpage to display information:

-   Single score: A type of visualization that lets you create a single score visualization.
-   Pie chart: Display individual data segments that are part of a whole. It helps you to compare different data pieces as they relate to each other.
-   Donut: Display information similar to a pie chart but includes an empty space at the center of data to display total value.
-   Semi donut: Displays information in a 180-degree view and fits in a smaller area than a full donut chart.
