# View work orders on Business Portal

View work orders and work order details related to your cases from the Business Portal.

## Before you begin

Activate the Customer Service with Field Service Management plugin \(com.snc.csm\_fsm\_integration\).

Role required: sn\_customerservice.customer

## About this task

When an agent creates a work order from a customer service case, customers can view the related work orders and work order details from the Business Portal.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to the Business Portal.

2.  Navigate to **Requests** &gt; **View submitted requests** &gt; **View work orders** in the portal header.

    A list of work orders is displayed, including the work order number, priority, state, and short description.

3.  Select a work order to view its details.
