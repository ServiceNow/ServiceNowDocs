# CRM Workspace form features

Use features available on CRM Workspace forms to highlight information, edit the text in the record header field, and view special handling notes.

|Feature|Description|
|-------|-----------|
|[Form headers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/csm-workspace-form-headers.md)|Provide a brief description of the case, typically the information included in the **Short description** field.|
|[Form ribbon and ribbon components](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/csm-agent-workspace-ribbon.md)|Display relevant information about a case, such as Customer 360, the timeline, and active SLAs.|
|[Visual field indicators](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/crm-workspace-visual-field-indicators.md)|Indicate form fields with unsaved changes.|
|[Field label positions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/crm-workspace-field-label-positions.md)|Select the position of field labels, either above fields or next to fields.|
|[Editable record headers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/crm-workspace-editable-headers.md)|Edit the short description of a record directly from the record header.|
|[Special handling notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/crm-workspace-special-handling-notes.md)|Display important information about individual records when a record opens in the workspace.|
|[Account hierarchy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/crm-workspace-account-hierarchy.md)|View the parent-child relationships for an account.|
|[Agent assist](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/customer-service-management/crm-workspace-agent-assist.md)|Search for relevant information such as knowledge articles or community posts.|

For more information about working with forms, see the following topics in the [Configurable Workspace UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/platform-user-interface/workspace-landing-page.md) documentation:
