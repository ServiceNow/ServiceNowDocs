# Branding and theming

Learn about branding and theming for CSM Configurable Workspace.

Branding and theming enables you to customize CSM Configurable Workspace to match your company's brand. Configure branding and theming for your workspace using Theme Builder.

Branding and theming wasn't available in CSM Agent Workspace, so there's nothing to migrate. Configure it after your agents are live. Use Theme Builder to apply your organization's colors and logo, create multiple themes, and set a default. Agents can choose their preferred theme from the available options.

Theme Builder is available as a store app. When you create a theme, choose colors that support an effective agent experience and align with your overall branding strategy, and follow the guidelines for logo size and accepted formats.
