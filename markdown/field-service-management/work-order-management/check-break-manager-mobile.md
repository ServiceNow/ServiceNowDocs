# Check a break in Manager Mobile

Check whether a break on a work order task has been taken or is still planned in Field Service Manager Mobile.

## Before you begin

Role required: wm\_manager, sn\_fsm\_tp.fsm\_territory\_resource\_manager

## About this task

An agent's schedule shows all of their breaks for the day, so you can check on their breaks without opening a specific work order task.

## Procedure

1.  Open Field Service Manager Mobile.

2.  Tap **Schedules**.

3.  Tap the assignment group or territory under **Schedules by team**.

4.  Tap the agent under **Today's Roster**.

5.  Tap the day that shows the break.

6.  Tap the break to view its details.
