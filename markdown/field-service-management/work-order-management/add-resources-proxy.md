# Add resources to take proxy actions

Add roles or individual users to the default proxy actions policy so more people can perform proxy actions on behalf of their managed agents.

## Before you begin

Role required: sn\_fsm\_proxy\_actn.proxy\_admin

## Procedure

1.  Select **All** &gt; **Field Service** &gt; **Proxy Configuration** &gt; **Policy**.

2.  Select the policy called **Proxy actions for Managers on behalf of their agents**.

3.  Choose from the following.
