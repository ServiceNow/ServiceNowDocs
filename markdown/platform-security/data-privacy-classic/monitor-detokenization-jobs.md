# Review de-tokenization job summaries

View summaries of de-tokenization jobs, monitor access to original sensitive data, and review audit logs for compliance purposes.

## Before you begin

Role required: data\_privacy\_admin, admin, or users assigned specific roles for viewing de-tokenization job summaries and audit logs.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **System Security** &gt; **Data Privacy** &gt; **Tokenization**.

2.  Review the De-tokenization jobs section of the interface.

3.  Click on a specific job to view its detailed summary.

    The summary provides the following information:

    |Field|Description|
    |-----|-----------|
    |Summary|A brief summary of the findings of the job scan.|
    |Job description|The unique identifier for the job.|
    |Created by|The user who created the job.|
    |Start time|The time the job began running.|
    |End time|The time the job completed.|
    |Preview expiry|The date and time at which the job's preview will expire.|

4.  Export or download job summaries for compliance reporting if needed.
