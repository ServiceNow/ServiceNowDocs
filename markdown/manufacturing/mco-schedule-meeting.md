# Schedule a meeting

Schedule a meeting from PNCC to coordinate follow-up actions with relevant stakeholders.

## Before you begin

Role required: admin or product\_non\_conformance\_case\_triager

## About this task

Quality Review Meetings enable you to discuss and agree on a fix for a product defect or quality investigation. The discussion, summary, and decision history remain attached to the issue, giving the team a clear record to reference later.

## Procedure

1.  Navigate to **Workspaces** &gt; **CRM Workspace** &gt; **List** &gt; **Product Non Conformances** &gt; **Related items** &gt; **Meetings**.

2.  Select **Create one**.

3.  On the Create New Meeting form, fill in the fields.

    For a description of the field values, see [Create new meeting form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/brazil/markdown/order-management/create-meeting-touchpoints-form.md).

4.  Select **Save**.

    You can also schedule a meeting in PQI with product\_quality\_investigation\_member role.
