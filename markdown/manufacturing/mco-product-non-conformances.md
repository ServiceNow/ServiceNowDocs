# Product non-conformance

The Agents workspace enables you to create and view product non-conformance cases.

-   **[Create a product non-conformance case](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/manufacturing/mco-create-product-non-conformance.md)**  
Create a non-conformance case for the products that have issue.
-   **[Product non-conformance without playbook](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/manufacturing/mco-related-lists-product-non-conformance.md)**  
In the workspace, you can use the product non-conformance related list to log the case.
-   **[View a product non-conformance](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/zurich/markdown/zurich/manufacturing/mco-view-a-product-non-conformance.md)**  
Review submitted product non-conformance cases to track their status and resolution.
