# Effectiveness target form

The Qualitative targets form enables you to add the qualitative target details, such as name, goal, progress, ownership, and target values.

|Field|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|Name|Name of the qualitative target.|
|Goal|Parent effectiveness goal that the target contributes to.|
|Description|Free-text description of the qualitative target.|
|State|Current lifecycle state of the target, such as Draft, In progress, Approved, Complete, Pending, Achieved, or Not achieved.|
|Progress|Percentage progress toward the target, calculated from the start value, final target value, and actuals to date.|
|Owner|User responsible for the target, set to the current user by default.|
|Status|Health indicator of the target, such as Green, Yellow, or Red.|
|Impact on goal|Weight of the target relative to its sibling targets, ranging from No impact to Maximum.|
|Contributors|Users who contribute to achieving the target.|
|Unit of measure|Unit used to measure progress toward the target.|
|Start value|Starting qualitative value of the target.|
|Type|Nature of the target — Maximize, Minimize, or Milestone — indicating whether the tracked value increases or decreases over time.|
|Final target value|Planned qualitative value the target must reach by the end of its duration.|
