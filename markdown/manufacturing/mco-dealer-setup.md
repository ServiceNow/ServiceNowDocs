# Set up Dealer

Configure dealer accounts and roles to enable dealers to manage campaigns and submit claims through the dealer portal.

Complete the following tasks to set up dealer accounts and grant access to the dealer portal.

|Tasks|Description|
|-----|-----------|
|[Create channel partner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/manufacturing/mco-create-channel-partner.md)|Create channel partner records that define dealer relationships and hierarchies within MCO.|
|[Create internal business location](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/manufacturing/mco-create-internal-business-location.md)|Create internal business location records to represent dealer operating locations and regional coverage.|
|[Create dealer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ServiceNow/ServiceNowDocs/australia/markdown/manufacturing/mco-create-dealer.md)|Create dealer records and assign users, roles, and permissions to enable portal access and transaction management.|
